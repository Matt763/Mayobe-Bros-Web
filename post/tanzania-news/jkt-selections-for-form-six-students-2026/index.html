JKT Form Six Selection: How It Works and What to Bring

How JKT selection for Form Six leavers works, how to check your camp, what recruits are told to bring, and where to find official notices.

JKT (Jeshi la Kujenga Taifa, the National Service) calls up Form Six leavers every year for a period of training before they move on to university, college or work. This post explains how the call-up works, how to find your name and camp, what recruits were told to bring in 2026, and what we could not confirm. It is written for Form Six leavers, parents and teachers.

Everything here was checked in November 2026. JKT intakes change from year to year, so always read the latest official notice before you act.

The short answer

  • JKT calls up Form Six leavers each year under the National Service Act. A news report on the 2025 intake names it as Act No. 16 of 1964 (revised 2002).
  • In 2026, JKT called up 60,000 Form Six leavers from Mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar, up from an earlier intake of 40,000 (The Citizen, 28 May 2026).
  • Recruits were told to report to their assigned camps between 1 and 7 June 2026.
  • You check your camp on the official JKT website, not through a link sent on WhatsApp.
  • Recruits with vision or physical disabilities report to Ruvu camp in Mlandizi, Coast Region, which has suitable facilities.

What JKT is

JKT is the national service organisation under the Ministry of Defence and National Service. Its job is to give young people discipline, practical skills and a sense of service to the country.

We do not state a start date for JKT because we have not found one in an official source. The Citizen reports that national service was reinstated in 2013 after a long suspension, with an intake of 5,000 youths. Compare that with the 60,000 called up in 2026.

How selection works

Here is what the news reports say, and what they do not say.

  • The call-up covers Form Six leavers of that year. In 2026 that meant students who finished Form Six in 2026.
  • JKT assigns each recruit to a camp. You do not choose.
  • The Daily News report on the 2025 intake says recruits can check their camp and required items on the JKT website by entering their school name and index number.
  • Reports say the long-term plan is for all Form Six graduates to go straight to camps. That is a stated plan, not a date.

We could not find an official explanation of how names are picked from the total list of leavers. Some blogs say it is random. Treat that as unconfirmed.

How to check your name and camp

  1. Wait for the official announcement. It is usually made around the time of ACSEE results or shortly after, and has been reported in the media (The Citizen, Daily News, The Guardian).
  2. Open the official JKT website. Daily News gave the address as jkt.mil.tz for the 2025 intake. Other pages mention jkt.go.tz. Websites change, so search for "JKT Tanzania" and make sure the address ends in .go.tz or .mil.tz.
  3. Choose the selection or "mujibu wa sheria" section and enter your school name and index number.
  4. Write down your camp, your reporting dates and the list of items to bring.
  5. If you cannot find your name, ask your headmaster or the JKT office in your region. Do not pay anyone to "find" or "move" your name.

Fake "selection lists" and paid "camp transfer" offers are common online. JKT recruits do not pay a fee to be selected.

What recruits were told to bring in 2026

The Citizen listed these items in May 2026. Your own notice may differ slightly, so follow the list on the JKT website first.

  • Dark blue shorts with elastic waistbands
  • Knee-length green T-shirts
  • Green or blue sports shoes
  • Two sea-blue bedsheets
  • Black socks
  • Warm clothing, for camps in colder areas
  • Birth certificate and Form Four completion certificate

Keep your documents in a plastic folder so they stay dry. Write your name on your clothes and bedsheets. Bring enough fare for the trip and a small amount of cash, and tell a family member the camp name and the date you reach it.

What camp life is like

We do not have an official day-by-day timetable, and we will not invent one. From JKT's stated purpose and from news coverage, you can expect discipline training, group work, and practical skills. Each camp runs its own programme.

Two practical points are worth knowing:

  • Some pages say the Form Six programme lasts three months. We could not confirm that from an official source, so ask JKT or read your notice for the exact length.
  • JKT has said not all recruits will go on to university, and that some will stay in their communities and use the skills they learn to earn a living.

Common questions

Is JKT optional for Form Six leavers?

Official and press statements say the law requires secondary school leavers to join. The 2025 report quotes the Chief of National Service asking Mainland leavers to report "as required by law". We did not find an official page that lists penalties or exemptions. Ask JKT directly if you have a health or family reason that affects your reporting.

Can I go to university first and do JKT later?

We could not find an official rule on this. Forum posts give mixed advice, and forums are not official. Ask JKT or your university admissions office before you decide. For loan questions, ask HESLB, and read our guide to the student loan system.

Is selection based on my ACSEE grades?

We found no official statement that grades decide who is called up. The call-up is for leavers of that year. Check the official notice.

Where do students with disabilities report?

The Citizen reports that recruits with vision or physical disabilities report to Ruvu camp in Mlandizi, Coast Region.

When is the next intake?

The 2026 reporting window was 1 to 7 June 2026, and it has passed. The next one will be announced by JKT, normally with reporting dates and a camp list. We have not seen a 2027 notice, so we cannot give you dates.

What to do next

  1. Bookmark the official JKT website and check it after your ACSEE results come out.
  2. Prepare copies of your birth certificate and Form Four certificate now.
  3. Ask your school, not social media, if a list or link looks suspicious.
  4. Plan your university or college application dates around the official reporting window. Our guide to what jobs your A-level combination can lead to may help while you wait.
