GTA 6 Cheat Codes for PS5 and Xbox: What Is Reported

GTA 6 has 61 cheats. Inputs for 17 are reported so far, none confirmed by Rockstar. See how to enter them on PS5 and Xbox, plus a free PDF list.

GTA 6 came out on 19 November 2026, and players are already asking for cheat codes. Rockstar has not published a list. What exists comes from gaming sites that played the game in its first days, so this page shows what they report and what nobody has confirmed. You can also read or download our free PDF list inside the page.

Everything below was checked on 20 November 2026. This list changes every day, so treat it as a snapshot.

The short answer

  • Reported by several outlets: GTA 6 has 61 cheats, and you must finish the story before they unlock.
  • Inputs are known for 17 of them. The rest are named in the game, but no reliable input has been found yet.
  • All inputs use the D-pad. PS5 and Xbox differ only in the shoulder buttons (L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox).
  • Reported: using a cheat turns off trophies and achievements, and stops saving for that play session.
  • We found no Rockstar statement about cheats. Most lists trace back to one first source, so we do not call any code verified.

Read or download the PDF

The PDF has the table you asked for: S/N, cheat, PS5 and Xbox. It opens right here with the site's own reader, and you can download or print it.

How to enter a cheat

Outlets that tested the game describe the same steps on both consoles:

  1. Finish the story, then open the Progress menu to see which cheats you have unlocked. Each cheat also has its own task.
  2. Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone.
  3. Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox), then press R1 or RB.
  4. Press the D-pad sequence for that cheat.

An input for a cheat you have not unlocked does nothing. There are no phone-number cheats this time.

Cheats named but without a known input

The game lists 61 cheats. For 44 of them, including No Fall Damage, Parachute, Repair Vehicle, Unlimited Nitrous, many vehicles and several screen filters, we could not find an input that reliable sources agree on. Two are disputed: Drunk has an input in only two outlets, and Raise Wanted Level has conflicting inputs, so we do not print either. The PDF names all 61.

What cheats cost you

Reported by the same outlets: a cheat turns off trophies and achievements for that session, and progress made afterwards is lost when you quit. Save first, and use cheats for fun, not for a trophy run.

Common questions

Are there GTA 6 cheat codes for PC?

No. GTA 6 has no PC version at this time, so any "PC cheat list" is not from Rockstar. Read what is known about a PC release.

Can I download a cheat tool or trainer?

No. A real cheat is typed on the controller. A download, trainer or generator is a scam, as we explain in our GTA 6 scams guide.

Why do other sites list different codes?

Some copy each other, and some recycle GTA V codes. We list only what the main outlets agree on and mark the rest as not confirmed.

What to do next

  1. Finish the story and check the Progress menu for unlocked cheats.
  2. Save before you try a cheat.
  3. Come back to this page: we will update the list when more inputs are confirmed.
