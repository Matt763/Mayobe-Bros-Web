eFootball vs EA Sports FC: Which Football Game to Play

eFootball is free on phone, console and PC. EA Sports FC 27 has a free Lite version and a paid full game. See which fits your device and budget.

If you love football, you have probably argued about this one with friends: eFootball or EA Sports FC? Both let you score a last-minute winner, both have fans who will defend them to the end, and both are made by companies that want you to keep playing. Choosing between these two football games (michezo ya video ya mpira wa miguu) is mostly about your device, your internet and your money, not about which one is "best".

This guide compares them fairly. Everything about the two games was checked on their official pages as of 19 November 2026. Games change often, so treat prices and licences as a snapshot and check the store before you pay.

The short answer

  • Phone only, small budget: start with eFootball. Konami lists it as free to play on Android and iOS.
  • Console or PC, no money to spend yet: try both free routes first: eFootball, and EA Sports FC 27 Lite.
  • You want the biggest list of real leagues and clubs: EA Sports FC 27 says it has more than 800 clubs and national teams. The full game is paid.
  • You do not want to spend on packs: be careful with both. Both have in-game purchases.
  • Your internet is slow or costs a lot per MB: read the data section below before you install anything.

Which game is which

eFootball is made by Konami. The old name was Pro Evolution Soccer, often called PES. EA Sports FC is made by Electronic Arts (EA). Before 2023 its football games were called FIFA. The current one, EA Sports FC 27, launched on 25 September 2026.

There is also EA Sports FC Mobile, a separate game for phones. It is not the same as FC 27 on console. We cover it below because many Tanzanian fans play on a phone.

Where you can play

This is the first question, because it decides everything else.

Two details matter. On Android, Konami says eFootball needs "Version 7.0 or above" and 2 GB of RAM or more, and on iPhone and iPad it needs iOS 15 or above. If your phone is older or very basic, check those numbers first. Our list of free games for low-end Android phones may help if neither game runs well.

Also, EA says "The Grounds", which includes its Clubs mode, is only on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2. A PS4 or Xbox One player gets the main game, but not that mode.

What is free and what costs money

eFootball

Konami's page says eFootball is "Free-to-play (in-game purchases available)". You can download it and play without paying. Money buys extra things inside the game. Konami's page does not explain in detail what each purchase gives, so open the in-game store and read it slowly before you spend.

EA Sports FC 27

The full game is paid. EA lists a Standard edition and an Ultimate edition, and an Ultimate Plus edition that was sold only to people who pre-ordered early. The price depends on your country and store, so we are not printing one here. Look at the store page for your console or PC.

EA also launched a free version on 25 September 2026 called EA SPORTS FC 27 Lite. EA's post lists it for PS4, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, the EA app, Steam and the Epic Games Store. It is not on Nintendo platforms. We could not find on EA's pages exactly what Lite includes, so check what it offers before you spend data downloading it.

EA Sports FC Mobile

EA's mobile page has a Download Now button and says it "Includes optional in-game purchases (includes random items)". That last phrase is important. Read the next section.

Packs, random items and your wallet

Football games love "packs". You pay, or you use a game currency you may have paid for, and you open a pack of players without knowing who you will get. It feels like the stadium roaring when a star appears. It is also a way to spend a lot of money quickly.

EA's FC Mobile page says its purchases include random items. For EA's Ultimate Team mode in FC 27, we did not read the store label, so check "Includes random items" on the store page before you decide. For eFootball, Konami's page only says in-game purchases are available, and it does not say whether they include random items. Look at the item description in the game.

What matters is how this affects skill. In any mode where a better squad can be bought, a player who spends more can start with an advantage. Spending does not replace practice, but it can make matches less fair. If that bothers you, play modes where you pick from the same pool, or play friends only.

Our guide to loot boxes and in-game purchases shows how to add a check before any purchase on Google Play. Do it before you install a football game, especially if a child uses your phone.

A simple rule for the budget: decide a number before you open the store, for example the price of a few bundles of mobile data, and do not go past it. Mobile money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money) makes payments easy, which is exactly why a limit helps.

Game modes

Both games cover the basics: quick matches, playing online against others and building a team.

eFootball. Konami's page names "Authentic Team" (real clubs and national teams) and "Dream Team" (an original team from current players and football legends). It also lists offline play: for example, 1 to 4 players on PlayStation. On the page, online play is listed separately.

EA Sports FC 27. EA's page highlights Ultimate Team, with a new Future Stars feature and a Future Stars Academy for developing young players, and mentions The Grounds and Clubs. The page we opened did not describe Career Mode, so we cannot say how it looks in FC 27.

EA Sports FC Mobile. EA's page lists Quick Match, Ultimate Team, Skill Games (ball control, shooting, passing), a Gauntlet mode and the option to change formation and mentality during a match.

If you mostly play against a friend sitting next to you, check the offline player counts on your platform before you decide.

Real teams, leagues and licences

Licences are the rights to use real club names, badges and player faces. They change from year to year, so the official page is the only safe source.

EA's FC 27 page says the game has "21,000+ players from 800+ clubs and national teams", 140+ stadiums and 35+ leagues. The same page also says 19,000+ players in another place, so the numbers on EA's own page do not fully agree. Treat them as "about 19,000 to 21,000".

EA's FC Mobile page lists Premier League and LALIGA EA SPORTS clubs, and says 19,200+ players from 726 teams across 36 leagues.

Konami's eFootball page credits Arsenal, Manchester United, Wembley National Stadium and several leagues and associations, such as the Eredivisie, LFP, RFEF, DFB, FIGC and the Football Association. Player likenesses are licensed through FIFPRO and the MLSPA. It also names a product partner, adidas.

Do not read these lists as a full ranking. A club may be missing, or may appear under a different name, in either game. Before you play for a favourite club, search for it in the game, or look at the game's official page.

Online play and data use

Both games work best online. Konami's PC requirements list a broadband connection, and EA's Ultimate Team and Clubs modes are online features. On a phone, that means data.

A football match is not huge, but updates are. Konami's news page showed a notice on 16 November 2026 titled "Game Slowdown During Matches". We only saw the title, not the cause, so we cannot tell you why it happened. Still, it is a reminder that online games can lag, whichever one you pick.

Some tips that work for both:

  • Download updates on Wi-Fi when you can, not on a mobile bundle.
  • Turn off automatic updates in the store, so a big update does not eat your bundle in the background.
  • Play against friends on the same network when you can.

We wrote two guides for this. See how to save mobile data while playing games and how to reduce lag and high ping in online games.

Which one should you pick

This is a decision guide, not a trophy.

You have a phone and little data. Try eFootball first, as Konami lists it free on Android and iOS. If you prefer EA's style and licences, EA Sports FC Mobile is also on phones. Check your storage and RAM first.

You have a PS4 or Xbox One. You can play eFootball free, or FC 27 Lite free, or buy FC 27. Remember that The Grounds is not on the older consoles.

You have a PS5, Xbox Series X|S or a PC. All three routes are open to you. Try the free ones for a week. If you still enjoy it, then think about paying.

You want real clubs above everything. Compare the lists above and look inside each game. A game with fewer licences can still be great fun.

You do not want to spend money. Choose a free game, set a limit and never buy packs you cannot afford to lose.

You play with children. Check the age label. Konami's page shows a PEGI 16 rating for eFootball. For other games, read our guide to age ratings and parental controls.

Common questions

Is eFootball really free?

Konami's page says it is free to play, with in-game purchases available. You can download it and play without paying, but it will keep offering things to buy.

Is EA Sports FC 27 free?

The full game is paid. EA also launched a free version, FC 27 Lite, on 25 September 2026 for PlayStation, Xbox and PC. It is not on Nintendo platforms.

Can I play these games on my phone?

eFootball is on Android and iOS. EA Sports FC 27 is not, but EA Sports FC Mobile is a separate phone game from the same company.

Which game has more real clubs?

By the numbers on the official pages, EA lists more clubs and leagues for FC 27 (800+ clubs and national teams, 35+ leagues) than Konami names on its eFootball page. The lists are written differently, so look inside the game for your own club.

Do I need to spend money to be good?

No, you do not need to spend to enjoy either game, and practice always helps. But in modes where a stronger squad can be bought, spending can give others a head start. Decide your limit before you start.

What to do next

  1. Check your device: phone model, storage, Android or iOS version, or console type.
  2. Turn on purchase checks, using our loot-box guide, before you install.
  3. Try a free route for a week: eFootball, or FC 27 Lite if you have a console or PC.
  4. Set a monthly spending limit and write it down.
  5. Read the store page for the "Includes random items" label before buying anything.
