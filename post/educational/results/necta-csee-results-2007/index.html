About these results

This page lists the centres in the CSEE 2007 results from the National Examinations Council of Tanzania (NECTA): 2,106 centres in all, and 2,092 of them with results on this site. Open a centre to see how its candidates did, or type a candidate number to go straight to one result. The other centres are listed, but their results are not on this site.

The information is copied from NECTA’s public results website and was last checked on 2026-11-11. Mayobe Bros is an independent site. It is not run, approved or endorsed by NECTA. If anything here differs from NECTA, the result published by NECTA is the correct one, so check important results on the official NECTA results website (opens in a new tab).

How to read the results

  • Candidate number: the centre number, a slash and the candidate’s own number, for example P0134/0001.
  • Aggt: the aggregate, the total NECTA published. A dash means no total was published for that candidate.
  • Div: the division, I to IV, or 0.
  • Subjects: each subject with the grade for it.
