This page lists what {{site-name}} stores in your browser. Cookies are small text files. Browsers also have "local storage", which works in a similar way. We keep both to a minimum.

{{#analytics}}

Analytics cookies (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics)

We use Google Tag Manager to measure how many people visit and which pages they read. Google may set cookies for this. We load it only after you press Accept. If you press Reject, nothing is loaded and no analytics cookies are set by us. You can change your choice any time with the Cookie choices link at the bottom of any page. Google explains its use of data in its Privacy Policy.

{{/analytics}} {{#comments}}

Article read counts (no cookies, nothing stored)

Our own server counts how many people read each article, to order the Popular page. This stores nothing in your browser and sets no cookie. It counts a read only after an article has been open for about 20 seconds and you have scrolled part of it, and it skips browsers that send the Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. See "Which articles are read most" in the Privacy Policy.

{{/comments}} {{#cfanalytics}}

Visitor counts without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We also count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so it runs even if you press Reject. We see only totals, such as visits per day, pages, countries and browsers. A browser extension that blocks trackers can stop it.

{{/cfanalytics}}

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

We may show Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may set cookies to show ads and to measure them. We load the ad code only after you press Accept. If you press Reject, no advertising cookies are set by us. More information is in our Privacy Policy. You can also manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

Other services

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages){{#comments}} and our comments service (Cloudflare){{/comments}} process requests to deliver the site and keep it safe. They do not set cookies for tracking on our pages.{{^turnstile}} If we switch on an anti-spam check such as Cloudflare Turnstile, it may set a cookie of its own, and we will say so here.{{/turnstile}}

{{#turnstile}}

Spam check (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The comment{{#newsletter}}, newsletter{{/newsletter}} and contact forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from automated programs. Its code is loaded from Cloudflare only when you start to use one of these forms, not when a page opens, and it is not loaded at all on other pages. We set no cookie for it. Cloudflare may use cookies or similar browser storage for the check, and it receives your IP address and technical details about your browser; this is described in Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy notice. Because it is needed to keep the forms safe, it runs whatever you chose in the cookie notice.

{{/turnstile}}

The share buttons are plain links. They do not load Facebook, X, WhatsApp or LinkedIn code until you click one.

Videos

Videos on {{site-name}} are played by Plyr, an open-source video player that we host ourselves, so it does not contact any other company. Video files that we host ourselves set no cookies.

Some articles may show a YouTube video. Until you press play, the page only shows a picture and a button, and nothing is requested from YouTube. When you press play, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced address (youtube-nocookie.com), together with the player's script from youtube.com and a thumbnail from i.ytimg.com. We do not ask any other service for the video's title. Google may still store information in your browser when you watch, under its own privacy policy.

Change your choice

  • Use the Cookie choices link at the bottom of any page to see the notice again.
  • Or clear this site's data in your browser settings. This removes every item in the table above.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Parts of the site still work, but your choices will not be remembered.
