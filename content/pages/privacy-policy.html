2. What we collect and why

{{#comments}}

Comments

If you post a comment we collect the name you type, your optional email address, your comment, the time, a random visitor ID saved in your browser, and a short code made from your IP address. We use this to show your comment, to contact you if needed and to stop spam. An automatic check (a list of abusive and spam words in English and Swahili, run inside our own comments service, with no other outside service involved) decides whether your comment appears at once or waits for a person to review it. If the check flags a comment we keep a short note of why, visible only to us. Your email address is never shown publicly. The IP code is a one-way hash that changes every day, and we do not store your raw IP address.

When you post a comment, your browser also keeps a secret code. It proves the comment is yours, so that you can edit or delete it. We store only a one-way hash of that code, never the code itself. If you edit a comment, we keep the text from before the edit so that we can check what changed. When you delete a comment, we erase the name, text, email address and IP code, the secret hash and the earlier text. If the comment has replies, a short note "This comment was deleted" stays in its place. We also remove control characters from names and comments.

Reactions and likes

When you press Like, Amazed, Sad or Angry on a post we store which post, which reaction and the same random visitor ID, so that each browser counts once and can change its mind. When you like a comment we store which comment, the visitor ID and the time. This ID is not linked to your name or email.

Contact messages

The contact form opens your own email app with your message ready to send. When you send it, we receive your name, email address and message through our email provider. We use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

Your preferences

Your browser stores your theme choice (light, dark or device) and your cookie choice. These stay on your device. See the Cookie Policy.

Listen to this article

The "Listen" button reads an article aloud using the speech voices already built into your device and browser. We do not make or store any audio. Some browsers and devices use online voices run by the browser or phone maker (for example Google or Microsoft). In that case your browser may send the sentences it is reading to that company to turn them into sound. We never receive that text or the audio. Your chosen speed and voice are saved only in your own browser.

Videos

Videos are played by Plyr, a player we host ourselves. A video that we host ourselves does not send your data to anyone else. If an article shows a YouTube video, nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play. After that, YouTube (Google) provides the video from its privacy-enhanced address and may collect data under its own policy.

Search

Site search runs inside your browser. The words you search for are not sent to us.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Ads are loaded only after you accept cookies on this site. When ads are switched on, we will use a consent message certified by Google for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads do not change what we write. We never sell your personal information.

Our articles link to other websites, and the share buttons open Facebook, X, WhatsApp or LinkedIn in a new tab. We do not load their tracking scripts on our pages. Once you click, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, for example{{#comments}} to remove one of your comments. You can also edit or delete your own comment yourself, in the browser where you posted it{{/comments}}. Email {{email:general}}{{#comments}} with the link to the article and the text of the comment{{/comments}}. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights set out in the GDPR, including the right to object and to complain to your data protection authority. In Tanzania, the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 gives you similar rights, and you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

You can change your cookie choice at any time with the "Cookie choices" link at the bottom of every page, and you can clear your browser's stored data to remove your preferences and visitor ID.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS. 9. Children

{{site-name}} is for a general audience. Our articles are written for students and adults and are not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's permission, because Tanzanian law treats under-18s as children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.
