Comments are a place for readers to ask questions, add useful experience and respectfully disagree. These rules keep the conversation safe and useful for everyone.

How comments work

  • You do not need an account. Give a name (it can be a first name or a nickname) and, if you like, an email address. Your email is never shown.
  • Most comments appear straight away. An automatic check runs when you send a comment. It looks for abusive or insulting language in English and Swahili, advertising and scam wording, phone numbers, email addresses and text written entirely in capital letters.
  • A comment the check flags waits for a person to read it. This can take a few days, and we cannot promise it will be published. A flag is not an accusation: the check can be wrong, and we read every flagged comment.
  • A person can still remove or hide any published comment at any time, for example after someone reports it.
  • You can reply to a published comment. Replies are checked in the same way.
  • A reply with a Team badge was written by the Mayobe Bros team. Only we can add that badge, and no one else can use our name when commenting.
  • You can edit your own comment for 30 minutes after you post it, as long as nobody has replied to it. An edited comment is checked again and shows the word "edited". If the check flags the new text, the edit does not go through.
  • You can delete your own comment at any time. If it already has replies, we keep the replies and show a short note, "This comment was deleted", in its place. Your name and your words are erased.
  • You can like a comment. Each browser can like a comment once.
  • You can also react to a post with Like, Amazed, Sad or Angry. A reaction is anonymous and you may change it at any time.

What we publish

  • Comments that are on topic and add something: a question, an experience, a correction or a different view.
  • Polite disagreement. You do not have to agree with us or with each other.

What we do not publish

  • Links, web addresses and "visit my page" messages. Comments containing links are blocked automatically.
  • Spam, adverts, promotions, requests for money and anything that sells.
  • Abuse, threats, hate, bullying or discrimination based on tribe, religion, gender, disability, nationality or any other personal feature.
  • Private information about you or anyone else, such as phone numbers, addresses, ID or bank details.
  • False or misleading claims presented as fact, and anything illegal or that encourages crime.
  • Requests for personal loan, legal, medical or financial advice. We cannot give it.
  • Comments that are off topic, repeated or written entirely in capital letters.

Our rights

We may edit a comment for length, refuse it, hide it or delete it at any time, without notice. A comment shows the writer's opinion, not ours. By posting you accept our Terms of Service and confirm the comment is yours.

Remove your comment

The quickest way is to delete it yourself, in the same browser where you posted it. Open the article and use the Delete button on your comment. This works because your browser keeps a secret that proves the comment is yours. If you changed browser or device, or cleared your browser's data, the secret is gone. In that case email {{email:general}} with the article link and the text of your comment, and we will delete it. See our Privacy Policy for how comment data is stored.

Report a comment

If you see a comment that breaks these rules, email us with the article link and the commenter's name and we will review it.
