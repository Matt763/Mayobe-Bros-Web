What we send

After you sign up, we send one email with a link to confirm. Nothing else is sent until you press the button on this page. Then we send:

  • one welcome email with our latest articles,
  • one email when we publish a new article, with its title, a picture, a short summary and a link,
  • one last email after you unsubscribe on this page, so you know it worked.

We do not promise a fixed number of emails. If we publish nothing, we send nothing. We use your address only to send the newsletter, and we never sell it. Our email provider, Resend, delivers the emails for us.

How to stop

Every newsletter email has an "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom. Open it and press the button on this page. Some email apps also show their own Unsubscribe button, which can unsubscribe you in one step. You can also write to {{email:general}} and we will remove your address.

How we keep and protect your address is explained in our Privacy Policy.
