Who reads Mayobe Bros

Our readers are students, job seekers, small business owners and curious people in Tanzania, many reading on a phone. We do not publish audience numbers on this page until we can back them with real, current data. Ask us and we will share what we can verify.

What we can do together

Sponsored article

A useful, honest article about your product, programme or service, clearly labelled "Sponsored". We write or edit it so it is accurate and helpful.

Display advertising

We may show ads through Google AdSense. Direct banner placements can be discussed once the site is established.

Information partnerships

Schools, training centres, NGOs and public bodies that want accurate information explained in plain language.

Our rules

  • Sponsored content is always labelled. Payment never changes our facts or conclusions.
  • We do not promote loans with hidden costs, "get rich quick" schemes, gambling, fake jobs, miracle cures or anything illegal or misleading.
  • We may refuse or remove any advertisement or sponsored post that breaks these rules or the policies of our ad partners.
  • Our editors may disagree with a sponsor. We will not publish praise we cannot support.
