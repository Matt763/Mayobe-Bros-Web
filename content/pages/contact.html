Common questions

How do I report a mistake in an article?

Send us the article link and what is wrong, with a source if you have one. We check it and, if you are right, we fix the article and note the correction. See our Editorial Policy.

Can I suggest a topic or write a guest post?

Yes. Put "Topic idea" or "Guest post" in the subject. We accept only original, accurate work that helps readers, and we edit and fact-check everything we publish.

Can you help with my personal student loan, job or business problem?

We cannot give personal advice. Our articles are general information. For loans contact HESLB, for licences contact the relevant authority, and for legal or medical questions speak to a qualified professional.

{{#comments}}
Why has my comment not appeared?

Most comments appear straight away. An automatic check holds some of them until a person has read them, which can take a few days, and we cannot promise a held comment will be published. Comments with links, abuse or off-topic content are not published. See the Comment Policy.

{{/comments}}
