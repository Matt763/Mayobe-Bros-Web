What Mayobe Bros is

Mayobe Bros is an independent blog for people in Tanzania who want clear answers. We explain how student loans work, which careers fit which subjects, how to start a small business without losing money, and what a new rule or price change means for you.

We write in simple English because many of our readers use English as a second language, often on a phone. We would rather give you three useful steps than thirty paragraphs of padding.

What we write about

Education

Student loans, university entry, study methods and choosing a subject combination.

Business ideas

Low-cost business ideas with honest costs, permits and risks. No promises of quick money.

Technology

Phones, apps and online tools explained without jargon.

Tanzania news

Plain-language explainers on fares, fuel prices, national service and tourism.

How we work

  • Sources first. Facts about money, dates, rules and prices come from official or reputable sources, and we link to them at the end of each article.
  • We say what we could not confirm. If a figure changes often or we could not check it, we tell you and point you to the official site.
  • We show our dates. Each article shows when it was published and when it was last updated.
  • We correct mistakes in the open. If you find an error, tell us and we will fix it.

Read the full rules in our Editorial Policy and Fact-Checking Policy.
