ECA means Economics, Commerce and Accountancy as your three principal subjects in Form Five and Form Six. It is the combination built for money, trade and the books of a business. This guide is for Form Four leavers choosing a combination, Form Six leavers choosing a degree, and parents who want to know where ECA leads.

The degree rules come from the TCU admission guidebook for 2026/27. Universities add their own rules, so treat this as a map and confirm details before you apply.

## Key points

- ECA fits accounting, banking and finance, tax, insurance, procurement, business and economics degrees.
- Many of these degrees accept two principal passes from a long list. Economics, Commerce and Accountancy are on that list almost everywhere.
- Some degrees ask for Mathematics at O-Level or a pass in Advanced Mathematics, so your Form Four Mathematics grade still matters.
- ECA does not lead to medicine, nursing or engineering.
- Accountancy also has its own professional path through NBAA, the national board for accountants.
- No place or job is guaranteed, and we give no salary figures because we could not confirm official ones.

## What the three subjects do for you

**Economics** explains prices, jobs, trade, taxes and money in a country. It is accepted in almost every business and planning programme.

**Commerce** is about how goods and services move from the maker to the buyer: trade, transport, banking, insurance and marketing.

**Accountancy** teaches you to record money, prepare accounts and read financial statements. It is the subject most directly tied to an accounting career.

Together they give you a clear picture of how a business earns, spends and survives.

## How points work

For results from 2016 the guidebook scores principal passes as A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and S = 0.5. Most degrees need at least 4.0 points from two relevant subjects.

Imagine you get C in Accountancy, D in Economics and D in Commerce. Your best two are Accountancy and Economics, so 3 + 2 = 5 points. That clears 4.0. If many people apply for the same programme, a higher score helps.

## Work that follows these degrees

These are directions, not promises.

- **Accounting and auditing.** Keeping accounts, preparing statements and checking them. Firms, banks, schools, hospitals, shops and government offices all keep accounts.
- **Banking and finance.** Work in banks, savings groups and other financial institutions.
- **Tax.** Helping businesses and people follow tax rules.
- **Insurance and risk.** Understanding how insurance works and how risk is priced.
- **Procurement and supply.** Buying goods and services for an organisation and managing stock and suppliers.
- **Business and entrepreneurship.** Running your own business. In our opinion, Accountancy and Commerce are among the most practical subjects for this, because you learn to track money and to read a market. Our article on [low-cost online business ideas](/post/business-ideas/e-commerce/low-cost-online-business-ideas-tanzania/) shows some starting points.
- **Teaching.** The College of Business Education lists a degree in Accountancy and Economics with Education that accepts Advanced Mathematics, Geography, Economics, Accountancy or Commerce, plus passes in Book-keeping and Commerce at O-Level. It is listed at 4.0 points, with a small intake.

## The accountancy route with NBAA

Many accountants in Tanzania also sit professional exams with the National Board of Accountants and Auditors (NBAA), the body that oversees accountancy and auditing professionals. This is separate from a university degree.

On its entry-requirements page, as read in November 2026, NBAA lists these routes for the Accounting Technician (ATEC) exams:

- **ATEC Level I:** a Form Six certificate with at least one principal pass, plus a pass in English and Mathematics at O-Level.
- **ATEC Level II:** a Form Six certificate for students who studied a commercially biased combination, with two principal passes.

For the professional exams, NBAA lists a degree in a non-accounting field for the Foundation level, with exemptions decided subject by subject, and an accounting or accounting and finance degree for the Intermediate level. The page is short and may not show every rule. Confirm current entry rules, fees and exam dates with NBAA before you plan around them.

## A note to your heart

If you are choosing ECA, you may be thinking about a future where money is not always a worry. Maybe your family has sacrificed to get you to Form Five, and you want to repay that with a steady job. That wish is honest and worthy.

It is also fine to be afraid of choosing wrong. Here is the calm truth. A combination opens some doors first, and it does not lock the rest. You can add a diploma, a short course or a professional exam later. Work steadily, check your facts, and ask for help when a subject feels heavy. No one can promise you a job, but you can promise yourself good habits.

## Where ECA does not fit

Medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and nursing need Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Read our [PCB guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/) for that route.

Engineering normally asks for Advanced Mathematics with Physics. ECA students would not meet those rules. Look at our [PCM guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcm-combination/) if that is your dream.

Law degrees are more open. Several accept two principal passes from long lists that include Economics, Commerce or Accountancy.

## If you are in Form Four: is ECA right for you?

1. **Check your Mathematics.** You do not need to be a mathematician, but many programmes ask for an O-Level pass or credit in Mathematics. Practise now.
2. **Check Commerce and Book-keeping.** Several entries ask for these at O-Level. If you enjoy them, ECA will feel natural.
3. **Ask if you like detail.** Accounting rewards care and patience with numbers.
4. **Ask which schools offer ECA.** Not every school does.
5. **Compare with other combinations.** If you also like Geography or Mathematics, EGM may suit you better. Read our [EGM guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-egm-combination/).

## If you are in Form Six: choosing and applying

1. When your results arrive, add up your points and find your best two relevant subjects.
2. Choose four or five degrees from the table and read each institution's exact entry line.
3. Apply only through the official university or institution channels in the current admission period. Nobody can sell you a place.
4. Plan the fees and apply for a loan if you qualify. Our [HESLB student loan guide](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/) explains how.
5. If the points system is new to you, read our [guide to Form Six results](/post/educational/results/how-to-read-acsee-results-points-and-divisions/).

## What if your points are not enough?

1. **Try a related degree.** Procurement, tax, banking, business and economics degrees often accept the same subjects.
2. **Take a diploma first.** Accredited colleges offer diplomas in accounting and business. Our guide on [diploma to degree](/post/educational/career-development/diploma-to-degree-tanzania-entry-requirements/) explains how to move up later.
3. **Use the NBAA route.** The ATEC exams are a separate way into accounting. Check the current rules with NBAA.
4. **Build skills while you wait.** Our guide on [building a career from zero](/post/educational/career-development/how-to-build-a-career-from-scratch/) has practical ideas.

## Common questions

### Can an ECA student become an accountant?

Yes. ECA is closely linked to accounting degrees, and the ATEC route with NBAA is open to Form Six leavers who meet its rules. Both need steady study.

### Do I need Advanced Mathematics for ECA degrees?

Not always. Many entries accept two principals from a long list and ask for an O-Level Mathematics pass or credit instead. A few entries ask for Advanced Mathematics, so read each one.

### Can ECA lead to banking?

Yes. Banking and finance degrees at institutions such as the Institute of Finance Management accept Economics, Commerce and Accountancy. Entry to a particular bank job follows that employer's rules.

### Is ECA a good combination for business owners?

It can help, because you learn about trade, money records and markets. It does not guarantee a profit. Business success also depends on capital, location, customers and luck.

### How many points do I need?

Most degrees ask for at least 4.0 points from two relevant subjects. Some institutions ask for more, so check each programme.

## What to do next

1. Download the TCU guidebook and search for three degrees from the table. Note the subjects, grades and years of study.
2. If you are in Form Four, check your Mathematics, Commerce and Book-keeping marks and ask your school if it offers ECA.
3. Visit the NBAA website to read the current ATEC entry rules.
4. Talk to an accountant or banker and ask what a normal week looks like.
