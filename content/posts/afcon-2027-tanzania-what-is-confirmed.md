In June and July 2027, Tanzania will host part of the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) together with Kenya and Uganda. It is a big moment for football fans, hotel owners, drivers, food sellers and anyone who plans to travel in those weeks. This post sets out what the Confederation of African Football (CAF) has confirmed, what is still unknown, and what you can do now.

The facts here were checked in November 2026. Plans for stadiums and tickets change, so read the CAF notices before you spend money.

## The short answer

- The tournament is called TotalEnergies CAF Africa Cup of Nations PAMOJA 2027. The opening match is on 19 June 2027 and the final is on 17 July 2027.
- It is the first AFCON hosted by three countries, and the first in East Africa since Ethiopia in 1976.
- As co-hosts, Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda are sure of a place at the finals. Twenty-four teams will play.
- CAF has not yet published the final list of stadiums, the match schedule or ticket sales. Treat any ticket offer you see today with care.
- In Tanzania, work is under way at the Benjamin Mkapa Stadium in Dar es Salaam, and new stadiums are being built in Arusha and in Zanzibar (Fumba).

## What CAF has confirmed

CAF's own "everything you need to know" page, published on 17 June 2026, gives the main facts. The tournament opens on Saturday 19 June 2027 and ends on Saturday 17 July 2027. Twenty-four teams take part. CAF says the exact match schedule, the venues and the kick-off times will be announced later.

The word "Pamoja" is Swahili for "together". CAF launched the official logo on 19 November 2026 at 13:00 local time in all three countries. It is shaped like a traditional East African shield. CAF says Tanzania's own pattern uses diagonal lines inspired by the national flag.

## How the hosts qualify

CAF says the three hosts have a place at the finals as co-hosts. That does not mean they skip the qualifying matches. The hosts still play in the qualifying groups, and those results count in the group table.

The qualifying format is simple to follow:

- Forty-eight teams, including the three hosts, play in 12 groups of four.
- In most groups the top two teams qualify.
- In a group that contains a host, only the best-placed team that is not a host goes through, because the host is already in.
- The 21 teams that qualify join the three hosts, which brings the total to 24.

Qualifying is played in three windows: 21 September to 6 October 2026, 9 to 17 November 2026, and 22 to 30 March 2027.

### Where Taifa Stars stand

Tanzania is in Group L with Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria and Madagascar. After two matches, The Citizen reported on 12 November that Tanzania had one point. Guinea-Bissau, with six points, had beaten both Tanzania and Nigeria. Tanzania were due to play Nigeria away on 13 November and at home in Zanzibar on 17 November. The coach is Miguel Gamondi.

Because Tanzania is already in the finals, the group matches are about preparing a team and testing the pitches, not about getting in. For the latest table, use the CAF website.

## Where the matches may be played in Tanzania

CAF has not yet confirmed the final stadium list, so be careful with lists you see online. These are the Tanzanian stadiums that CAF and news reports have named in 2026:

| Stadium | City | What reports say (checked November 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Benjamin Mkapa Stadium | Dar es Salaam | Being renovated. CAF said in February 2026 that it needed a new pitch and stronger lighting (at least 2,500 lux). CAF's Samson Adamu said on 31 October 2026 that he was encouraged by the renovation work. |
| Samia Suluhu Hassan Stadium | Arusha | New stadium, still under construction. |
| Fumba Stadium | Zanzibar | New stadium. The Citizen reported it was 87 percent complete, with completion planned for 31 December 2026. |

Training grounds named in news reports include the Gymkhana ground and the Law School grounds in Dar es Salaam, and the Major General Isamuhyo Stadium.

The Citizen quoted the National Sports Council's Neema Msitha as saying no stadium project had reached 100 percent yet, but that work was moving ahead and the rest was expected on schedule. CAF's February report set January 2027 as the deadline for full readiness. Another inspection is likely before the tournament, so the picture can still change.

## What it can mean for ordinary people

Big tournaments bring visitors. Fans from other countries will need rooms, transport, food and guides. That can mean extra work for hotels, guesthouses, taxi and bajaji drivers, and small traders near the stadiums.

Be realistic. We found no official figure for how many fans will come or how much money Tanzanian businesses will earn, so any number you see is a guess. If you plan to rent out a room or sell food, wait for the final match schedule and venues. Then you will know which city will host which match.

The same care applies to rules for travellers. CAF's June article does not describe visas or border arrangements. If you plan to cross to Kenya or Uganda for a match, check the immigration rules of each country before you book. Our post on [Tanzania tourism and what visitors spend](/post/tanzania-news/tanzania-tourism-boom-kilimanjaro-serengeti-zanzibar-world/) explains how to plan trips and avoid unlicensed operators.

## Why the host list matters for travel plans

Three countries mean long distances between matches. Dar es Salaam, Arusha and Zanzibar are in different parts of Tanzania, and Nairobi and Kampala are in other countries. A fan who wants to follow one team might have to move between cities, so the order of the fixtures will decide how much a trip costs. This is why we suggest waiting for CAF's final schedule before you book.

The big stadium works also have a second use. A new stadium in Arusha or Zanzibar can host local league games and school events after the tournament. That is a hope, not a promise, and we did not find an official plan for how each stadium will be used afterwards.

## Common questions

### When is AFCON 2027?

It starts on 19 June 2027 and the final is on 17 July 2027, according to CAF.

### Does Tanzania need to qualify?

No. As a co-host, Tanzania has a place at the finals already. Taifa Stars still play the qualifying matches, and the results count in the group table.

### Which Tanzanian cities will host matches?

CAF has not confirmed this. The cities named in reports are Dar es Salaam, Arusha and Zanzibar. Wait for CAF's official schedule.

### Are tickets on sale?

As of CAF's June 2026 article, ticket information was still to be announced. We did not find an official ticket sale in November 2026. Buy only from channels that CAF or the local organising committee names.
