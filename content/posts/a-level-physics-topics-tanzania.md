Physics is the subject behind engineering, telecommunication, geology and many science degrees. In Tanzania it is a principal subject in combinations such as PCM, PCB and PGM, and students choose it at the Form Five selection (*selection ya kidato cha tano*). This guide shows what the subject contains, how the national exam is built, and how to prepare. It is for Form Four leavers choosing a combination, Form Five and Form Six students, and parents.

## Key points

- Physics has three ACSEE papers: two theory papers of 3 hours each and one practical paper. The practical is worth 50 marks.
- The Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE) published a 2023 Physics syllabus for Form Five and Six. The exam papers set so far were still built on the older 2010 syllabus (see the section on the two syllabi).
- In the 2025 NECTA report, Current Electricity, Electrostatics, Mechanics and Measurement were the weaker topics. The practical paper lost marks on graphs.
- Engineering degrees in the TCU guidebook usually ask for principal passes in Physics and Advanced Mathematics, so Physics is only half of the story.
- Free official material exists. You do not need an unofficial PDF.

## What the subject is in the ACSEE

NECTA gives Physics the subject code 131. There are three papers:

| Paper | Type | Time | What you answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 131/1 Physics 1 | Theory | 3 hours | Seven short questions (10 marks each, all compulsory) and two of three longer questions (15 marks each). Total 100 marks. |
| 131/2 Physics 2 | Theory | 3 hours | Five of six structured questions, 20 marks each. Total 100 marks. |
| 131/3 Physics 3 | Practical | 3 hours 20 minutes | Three questions worth 20, 15 and 15 marks. Total 50 marks. |

These details come from NECTA's ACSEE formats booklet (revised July 2019), and the 2025 NECTA report on the Physics paper confirms the same structure. Non-programmable calculators and mathematical tables are allowed, according to the format.

The practical paper comes in three equal versions, 3A, 3B and 3C. Your school sits one of them. NECTA's format says the version depends on how many candidates a school has. For ACSEE 2026, the NECTA timetable put Physics 1 on 5 May, Physics 2 on 7 May and the practical (3A) on 14 May, with 3B and 3C later in May.

Physics is also a subject you can take in a combination only if your school has the teachers and the laboratory for it. Ask your school before you choose.

## Topics by Form Five and Form Six

The TIE syllabus (2023) groups the content into three strands: concepts and theory, experiments, and a project. The project starts in Form Five and is finished in Form Six. Here are the topic areas, in our own words.

**Form Five**

| Area | What you study |
|---|---|
| Measurement | Units and dimensions, accuracy and precision, errors and uncertainty |
| Mechanics | Forces and motion, projectiles, circular motion, rotation of rigid bodies, gravitation, fluids |
| Thermal physics | How heat moves, the kinetic theory of matter, thermodynamics, thermal radiation |
| Vibrations and waves | Simple harmonic motion, how waves travel, interference, diffraction, polarisation |
| Electrostatics | Electric fields, potential and capacitors |

**Form Six**

| Area | What you study |
|---|---|
| Current electricity | Direct and alternating current, networks of circuits |
| Electromagnetism | Magnetic force, induction, electromagnetic waves |
| Electronics and telecommunication | Semiconductors, transistors, logic gates, satellites |
| Atomic physics | Atomic transitions, nuclear physics, lasers, X-rays, alpha, beta and gamma radiation |
| Energy sources | Solar, wind, hydropower and thermal reactors |
| Medical physics | How nerves and the heart are studied, imaging such as X-ray and ultrasound, radiotherapy |

The syllabus asks for ten periods of 40 minutes each week. It also names ICT tools for handling experiment data, such as Excel, R and Python.

## The two syllabi: what to expect

TIE lists a Physics syllabus for Form Five and Six dated 2023. The syllabus says it follows the 2023 Advanced Secondary Education Curriculum. The older syllabus is from 2010 (an earlier reprint is described as 2009 or 2010).

NECTA's report on the 2025 Physics exam says the paper followed the 2019 subject format, which is based on the 2010 syllabus. We did not find a NECTA format for the new syllabus as checked on 24 November 2026. Here is what we could confirm about the rollout:

- In July 2024 the Ministry of Education postponed the improved Form Five curriculum to 2025, according to The Citizen (28 July 2024).
- The 2026 Form Five joining instructions we opened ask for books under the "new curriculum" (*mtaala mpya*). These are school documents, not a ministry statement.
- We could not find an official page that says which ACSEE year is the first on the new syllabus.

So two cohorts may be affected differently. Ask your teacher which syllabus your class follows, and check the NECTA website for the format that applies to your exam year. The topic names above overlap strongly with the older exam list (mechanics, heat, electricity, electronics, waves, atomic physics), but some details differ. Do not drop a topic because it is missing from one list.

## How the marks are made up

The 2023 syllabus says continuous assessment counts for 30% and the national Form Six exam for 70%. In Form Five, the first and second term exams are worth 5% each. In Form Six, the first term exam is 6%, the project 7%, the mock exam 7% and the national exam 70%. Your project matters, so start it early.

## Where students lose marks

NECTA's 2025 report on Physics (29,604 candidates, 97.48% passed) rates topics by the share of candidates who scored at least 35% on the question. Four topics were only "average":

| Topic | Share who scored 35% or more |
|---|---|
| Mechanics | 51.90% |
| Measurement | 50.62% |
| Current Electricity | 43.27% |
| Electrostatics | 38.67% |

In the practical paper, Current Electricity was the weak spot (38.07%). The report says many candidates left out graph features such as titles, scales, labelled axes, the best-fit line and the slope. Others used the wrong formula. The report also lists topics where candidates did well, for example Environmental Physics, Electromagnetism and Heat.

Read this as a map for your own revision, not as a rule. Questions change every year.

## A study plan for Physics

1. **Do the maths every week.** Physics at this level is problem solving. Rework the examples in your TIE student book until you can do them without looking.
2. **Learn units and dimensions first.** Measurement and errors appear in every other topic, and the 2025 report shows it was a weak topic.
3. **Draw the diagram before you calculate.** Forces, circuits and waves are easier when you can see them.
4. **Practise graphs by hand.** Plot points, choose a scale, draw the line of best fit, and work out the slope with units. This is exactly where the practical marks were lost.
5. **Practise circuits.** Kirchhoff's laws, the Wheatstone bridge and AC circuits come up often, and Current Electricity was weak in both theory and practical.
6. **Use past papers in a plain way.** Work one old question, mark yourself with the marking guide if your teacher has one, and list your mistakes. Our guide on [using NECTA examiners' reports to revise](/post/educational/study-tips/use-necta-examiners-reports-to-revise/) shows how to read the feedback.
7. **Join the practical sessions.** Do not skip laboratory time. If your school has few instruments, ask the teacher to let you handle them in turns.

## Which combinations include Physics

- **PCM** (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics): the route to engineering. See our [PCM guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcm-combination/).
- **PCB** (Physics, Chemistry, Biology): medicine and health degrees. See our [PCB guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/).
- **PGM** (Physics, Geography, Mathematics): land, maps and planning. See our [PGM guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pgm-combination/).
- **PMCs** (Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science) is also on the TAMISEMI list of combinations.

Students in science combinations who do not study Mathematics must also take Basic Applied Mathematics, unless they passed Additional Mathematics at O-Level. This comes from the 2023 curriculum. Every student also takes Academic Communication and History of Tanzania and Ethics under that curriculum.

## What degrees Physics can open

The TCU admission guidebook for 2026/27 lists the entry rules. Examples we read:

- Many engineering degrees ask for two principal passes in Physics and Advanced Mathematics with at least 4.0 points.
- Bachelor of Science in Applied Physics at the University of Dodoma asks for a C in Physics and a D in one of several other subjects.
- Medicine, dental surgery and pharmacy need Physics, Chemistry and Biology with at least 6 points, and a D or better in each.

Rules change every year and differ by university. Read our guides on the [TCU admission guidebook](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-read-tcu-admission-guidebook/) and [programme entry requirements](/post/educational/career-development/how-to-read-tcu-programme-entry-requirements/) before you decide. If you plan engineering, also read about [A-Level Advanced Mathematics](/post/educational/a-level-subjects/a-level-advanced-mathematics-topics-tanzania/), because the two subjects work as a pair.

## Free official learning materials

- TIE publishes the Physics syllabus for Form Five and Six on its website, and it runs an online library (Maktaba Mtandao). Check the link on the TIE home page for the current address, because we could not open the library site from our connection on 24 November 2026.
- TIE sells its books in Dar es Salaam, Morogoro and Dodoma, and at sales outlets in several other towns, according to its pages.
- NECTA publishes its examiners' reports (CIRA) for free. They show real questions, good answers and common mistakes.

Avoid websites that offer unofficial copies of books. Many are copied without permission, and files from unknown sites can be unsafe.

## Common questions

### Do I need Advanced Mathematics to study Physics?

Not to take the subject in a combination such as PCB, but most engineering degrees need Physics and Advanced Mathematics together. If engineering is your goal, choose a combination with both.

### Is A-Level Physics mostly theory or practical?

Both. Two of the three papers are theory, and the practical paper is a separate 50-mark paper. Practise experiments, because practical skills like graphs also help in theory questions.

### Which syllabus will my exam follow?

It depends on your year and the NECTA format for it. As checked on 24 November 2026, NECTA's latest published ACSEE format we could find is the 2019 one. Ask your teacher and check the NECTA website.

## What to do next

1. Download the Physics syllabus from the TIE website and tick off the topics you have covered.
2. Pick your two weakest topics from the list above and give each one an hour a week.
3. Practise one graph and one circuit problem every week.
4. If you are still choosing a combination, compare the degrees you want in the TCU guidebook.

Steady practice beats last-minute cramming, and Physics rewards students who keep going.
