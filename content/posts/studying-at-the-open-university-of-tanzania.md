Not everyone can leave a job, a family or a town to sit in a lecture hall every day. Some people missed their chance at university the first time. Some are working and want to grow. If that sounds like you, the Open University of Tanzania (OUT) may be worth a close look.

This post explains how OUT describes its way of teaching, how to start an application, and the Foundation Certificate route that TCU lists. It does not give fees or centre details, because we could not confirm them. You will find where to ask instead.

## The short answer

The Open University of Tanzania describes itself as offering open and online learning, with open admission, blended study and both regular and on-demand examinations. You apply through OUT's online admission portals. Some people can also enter degree programmes at other universities through OUT's Foundation Certificate.

- OUT says it offers "Open and online learning", "Open admission" and blended learning (a mix of one-on-one and online study).
- It says it is available in all regions of Tanzania.
- Undergraduate and postgraduate applications use separate links.
- Fees, programme lists and exam rules should be confirmed with OUT directly.
- Intake and application dates can change, so read the current admission page.

This information was checked on OUT and TCU pages in November 2026.

## What does open and distance learning mean?

In a traditional university you attend classes on campus at fixed times. In open and distance learning, much of the studying happens where you live, at times you can plan around your life. You still have courses, assignments and exams. It is not easier. It asks for more self-discipline, because nobody is knocking on your door at 8 a.m.

Here is how OUT describes itself on its home page, in its own words under "Our Identity":

- Open and online learning.
- Affordable and quality education for all (this is its stated aim; we cannot judge the costs for you).
- Open admission.
- Available in all regions.
- Blended learning, a mix of one-on-one and online study.
- Regular and on-demand examinations.

"Open admission" is OUT's phrase. It does not mean that anyone with no school results can join any programme. Each programme has its own entry rules, so read them for the course you want.

The TCU, which regulates universities in Tanzania, has guidelines for online and blended study that say programmes must follow the university's and TCU's approval processes. If you want to understand the difference between online and blended, see [is an online degree recognised in Tanzania](/post/educational/online-learning/is-an-online-degree-recognised-in-tanzania/).

## How do I apply to OUT?

OUT's admission page lists two main paths.

1. **Undergraduate.** For a bachelor's degree. The page gives an online application link at admission.out.ac.tz/Registration.
2. **Postgraduate.** For postgraduate diplomas, master's and doctoral programmes. The page links to pgoa.out.ac.tz.

Before you open the link, prepare:

- Your school certificates, such as your Form Four and Form Six results, or your diploma documents if you are entering with a diploma.
- A phone number and an email address that you check often. Most steps are online.
- A way to pay any application charge. We could not confirm the amount, so ask OUT or read the portal.
- Time to read each programme's entry rules.

OUT's home page also links to a students' portal, an alumni portal and email systems. You will use some of these after you join.

### What about intakes and dates?

The admission page shows a heading for an "April and October intake". Under the undergraduate section it also lists three admission batches:

| Batch | Dates shown on the page |
| --- | --- |
| Batch one | 1 June to 30 September |
| Batch two | 1 October to 31 January |
| Batch three | 1 February to 30 May |

The batch dates do not obviously match the April and October heading, and the page does not explain how they relate. Because of that, do not plan your year from this table alone. Contact the admissions office and ask which batch leads to which start date for your programme.

As shown on the page in November 2026, admissions can be reached at admissions@out.ac.tz or +255 222 668 992. The postal address is P.O. Box 23409, Kinondoni.

## What is the Foundation Certificate route?

Some people do not have the usual Form Six results needed for a degree. TCU publishes an admissions guidebook for people entering with diplomas or equal qualifications (for the 2026/2027 academic year). It lists the Foundation Certificate granted by OUT as one of the "equivalent entry qualifications" for degree programmes recognised by TCU.

For entry using the OUT Foundation Certificate, the guidebook's general table says an applicant needs:

- a GPA of 3.0 from six core subjects in the relevant cluster (Arts, Science or Business Studies), plus an Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination result of at least 1.5 from two subjects; or
- an Ordinary Diploma from a recognised institution with a GPA of at least 2.0, or an NTA Level 5 or Professional Technician Level II certificate.

Many programme entries in the guidebook also say "Foundation Certificate of the OUT with a minimum GPA of 3.0". Requirements differ by programme and university, and can change each year.

Please do not treat this summary as the full rule. Read the current guidebook on the TCU website, then ask OUT how the Foundation Certificate programme works, how long it takes and what it costs. We did not confirm those details.

Say you finished Form Six some years ago and your results are not enough for your dream course. Ask OUT whether the Foundation Certificate suits you. It could be a bridge. Only OUT and the receiving university can say for sure.

## What about study support and exams?

OUT's home page says it runs regular and on-demand examinations. We could not find, in the pages we read, how these work in practice: where you sit them, how often, or how to book one. Do not guess. Ask OUT before you choose a programme.

Questions worth asking:

- How do I get my study materials?
- Are there classes or tutorials, online or in person, and how do I join?
- Where can I sit exams near where I live?
- How do I get help if I fall behind?
- What happens if I miss an exam?

OUT says it is available in all regions. Ask which place you would use, and write down the answer.

Practical habits help with any distance course. Set fixed study hours. Keep a calendar for assignment dates. Our post on [study techniques that help you remember more](/post/educational/study-tips/10-proven-techniques-to-study-effectively-for-students/) has simple methods you can use. And if you worry about paying, read about [HESLB student loans](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/), then ask HESLB whether your programme qualifies.

## Is it right for you?

Think about these honestly.

- **Time.** Can you give regular hours each week for several years?
- **Discipline.** Will you study when no one checks?
- **Internet and power.** Do you have a way to go online when you need to? If not, ask OUT what options exist.
- **Goal.** Do you need this degree for a job, a promotion or further study? Ask whether it is accepted for that goal.
- **Support.** Do your family and employer understand your plan?

If most answers are yes, you may do well. If some are no, that is only information. You can fix many of them, or choose a different path.

## Common questions

### Is the Open University of Tanzania a real, registered university?

OUT is a university in Tanzania. To check any university's current status, use TCU's list of registered universities. We did not search that list for OUT in this post, so check it yourself before you apply.

### How much does it cost?

We could not confirm current fees. Ask OUT's admissions office or read the programme page on its website. Ask for a full list that includes any exam or registration fees.

### Can I study while I work?

OUT describes its model as open, blended and available in all regions, which points to flexible study. How flexible depends on the programme. Ask about deadlines and exam times.

### Do I need Form Six to join?

Many programmes need it, and some entry routes use a diploma or the Foundation Certificate. Rules differ by programme, so read the entry requirements for your course.

### Where do I get help with my application?

Use the admissions contacts above. Keep a written record of replies.

## What to do next

1. Open OUT's admission page and read the current intake and batch information.
2. Choose two or three programmes and read their entry rules.
3. Write to admissions with your questions about fees, exams and study support.
4. If you need the Foundation Certificate, read the TCU guidebook and ask OUT about it.

Going back to study is a brave step, and it does not have to be fast. Take it one careful question at a time.
