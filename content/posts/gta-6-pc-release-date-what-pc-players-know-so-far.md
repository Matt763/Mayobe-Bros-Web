Vice City is open. Grand Theft Auto VI went on sale on 19 November 2026, and millions of players are already inside. If your game lives on a PC, you are standing outside the door with your face against the glass.

We know how that feels, so this post is straight with you. Rockstar has not announced a PC version and has not given a PC date. Below, we separate what is confirmed, what is only reported, and what nobody outside Rockstar knows. Everything was checked on 20 November 2026.

## The short answer

- **Confirmed:** GTA 6 is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Confirmed:** Rockstar and Take-Two have announced no PC version, no PC date and no PC system requirements.
- **Reported, not confirmed:** one outlet says Rockstar is aiming at the first three months of 2027 for PC. Its sources are anonymous.
- **Not announced:** Steam, Game Pass, streaming and any other way to play it on a computer.
- **Be careful:** anyone offering a "GTA 6 PC download" today is not offering the real game.

## What is confirmed

Confirmed means it comes from Rockstar or Take-Two themselves.

Rockstar's page for the game, at rockstargames.com/VI, says the game is now available. It lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. We could not find PC anywhere on the page.

On 19 November 2026 Take-Two posted a release titled "Rockstar Games Announces Grand Theft Auto VI Now Available". It names the same two platforms. It says the game is sold at the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Games Store and other shops. PC is not mentioned.

Take-Two's results release of 9 November 2026 has a table of upcoming games. GTA VI appears there for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the date 19 November 2026. There is no PC line for it.

That is the full list of official facts about platforms. It is short, and it is clear.

## What Take-Two's boss has said about PC

Strauss Zelnick is the chairman and CEO of Take-Two, the company that owns Rockstar. He was asked about PC three times this year. The wording below comes from public transcripts of the calls (the November and May ones by Motley Fool, the September one by StockAnalysis). A transcript can contain small errors, so treat quotes as close, not perfect.

**21 May 2026, results call.** An analyst asked about plans for a PC release. Zelnick said PC is a great market that is growing for console-type games. Then he said: "Rockstar Games has announced GTA 6 for console only so far."

**17 September 2026, annual meeting.** Answering a shareholder question, he said Take-Two's general strategy is to launch on platforms that have a meaningful audience. He also said PC "is becoming more and more important to our company over time". That is about the whole company, not about GTA 6. In the same meeting he described GTA VI as launching as a single-player game on two major platforms.

**9 November 2026, results call.** An analyst said console prices keep rising and asked whether that would speed up GTA coming to PC or streaming. Zelnick said any information about new versions "will always come from our labels". Rockstar is one of those labels. He gave no date and no yes or no. Later on the same call he said GTA VI "is available on 2 consoles when it launches on November 19".

Put together, the message is plain. Take-Two has said GTA 6 is console only so far, that PC matters more and more to the company, and that Rockstar will announce anything new. It has not said PC is coming, and it has not said PC is not coming.

## What is only reported: the early 2027 story

On 13 October 2026, the website GamesBeat published an article by Alexander Lee with the title "Grand Theft Auto VI's PC release is slated for Q1 2027". GamesBeat updated it on 18 November 2026.

Here is exactly what the article says.

- It relies on **four anonymous industry sources**.
- It says Rockstar plans a PC release in the **first quarter of 2027**, which means January to March.
- It says the PC version must pass the same quality bar as the console version first.
- It says a Rockstar representative did not answer GamesBeat's request for comment before publication.

No date is given. No store is named. The report is a claim by people who did not give their names. It could be right. It could be out of date by now, because Rockstar has moved this game's date twice before. Until Rockstar says it, this is a rumour with a respectable source, and we will call it that.

Other sites repeated the report. When many sites repeat one report, it is still one report.

## What history says, and what it does not

Rockstar has brought earlier games to PC after the console launch. The gaps were very different each time. These dates are for history only. They do not tell us when, or whether, GTA 6 will come to PC.

| Game | Console launch | PC launch | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grand Theft Auto IV | 29 April 2008 | 2 December 2008 | About 7 months |
| Grand Theft Auto V | 17 September 2013 | 14 April 2015 | About 19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | 26 October 2018 | 5 November 2019 | About 12 months |
| Red Dead Redemption | 18 May 2010 | 29 October 2024 | More than 14 years |

(Dates from each game's Wikipedia page, as checked in November 2026. Treat them as a guide. The shortest gap in the table was 7 months and the longest was over 14 years.)

The gap has gone from months to a year to over a decade. A rumour of "a few months" would be faster than GTA V and RDR2, but the table cannot predict anything. Only Rockstar can set the date.

## What you can do right now

You cannot pre-order or wishlist a PC version, because it does not exist yet. We searched Steam's store on 20 November 2026 and found no GTA VI page. There are other GTA games there, but not this one. That fits what Rockstar says.

So here is what helps.

1. **Follow the source.** Bookmark [Rockstar's page for GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) and the Rockstar Newswire. A real PC announcement will appear there first.
2. **Ignore "system requirements" you see online.** Rockstar has published none for PC. Any list of "GTA 6 PC system requirements" is a guess by someone else.
3. **Do not buy parts for a rumour.** When Rockstar publishes official requirements, compare them with your computer. Until then, spend nothing for GTA 6 alone.
4. **Plan for a big download.** The PlayStation Store page asks for at least 212 GB of free space on PS5, according to our [console preparation guide](/post/video-games-updates/get-your-console-ready-for-gta-6-storage-internet-and-budget/). We do not know the PC size. If you pay for mobile data, plan carefully.
5. **Play what is out.** The earlier games are on PC. Our post on [every GTA game in order](/post/video-games-updates/every-gta-game-in-order-history-of-grand-theft-auto/) shows where to start.

## Fake PC downloads: what to avoid

When people want something that does not exist yet, scammers sell it. Since there is no official PC game, these are all fake:

- a "GTA 6 PC download" or "GTA 6 PC crack"
- a "setup.exe" in a video comment or chat group
- a torrent or a "leaked PC build"
- a "key" that wants a survey, a share or a payment to a personal mobile money number

These files often carry programs that steal passwords or lock your computer. Read our guide to [GTA 6 scams, fake downloads and leaks](/post/video-games-updates/gta-6-scams-fake-downloads-and-leaks-how-to-stay-safe/) for the simple checks. The short rule is this: if the page is not Rockstar's own site or a store you know, close it.
