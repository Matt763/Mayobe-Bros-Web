Tax is a worry for many new owners. Often the fear is bigger than the real bill. If you run a small business alone, Tanzania has a simple turnover-based tax. This post turns the official table into plain words with worked examples. It is general information, not tax advice. All figures are as checked in November 2026 on the TRA website. Tax rules can change every year, usually after the national budget, so confirm the current table with TRA before you rely on a number.

## The short answer

If you are a resident individual whose only income is from a business in Tanzania, and your yearly turnover is not more than TSh 200 million, you can fall under the presumptive tax. Turnover means your total sales, before you take away any costs. Your tax depends on which band your turnover falls in.

- Turnover up to TSh 4,000,000: no tax.
- New taxpayer: TRA's table says no tax for the first 12 months of the business, if you qualify and claim it.
- Above TSh 4,000,000, the tax is higher with incomplete records than with complete records, up to TSh 11,000,000.
- Above TSh 11,000,000 and up to TSh 200,000,000, TRA's table shows 4 percent of turnover.
- Keeping complete records lowers your tax in the middle bands.

## Who falls under the presumptive tax?

TRA lists four conditions:

1. You are a resident individual for that year.
2. All your income for the year comes from a business based in Tanzania.
3. Your business turnover is not more than TSh 200 million.
4. You do not choose to leave the presumptive rules.

If you meet all four, TRA says your income tax for the year equals the amount in the table. You do not have to file a final return with audited books unless you choose to.

Transport businesses such as taxis, small passenger vehicles, three-wheelers and goods vehicles have their own fixed amounts on the TRA page. We do not repeat them here, so check the TRA table if that is your business.

If you do not meet the conditions, you are taxed on your taxable income instead. TRA says you must then keep proper records, send provisional returns by 31 March, and send a final return with audited financial statements by 30 June of the next year.

## The tax table in plain words

Here is the table as read on the TRA page in November 2026.

| Yearly turnover | Tax with incomplete records | Tax with complete records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to TSh 4,000,000 | Nil | Nil |
| Above TSh 4,000,000 up to TSh 7,000,000 | TSh 100,000 | 3% of the turnover above TSh 4,000,000 |
| Above TSh 7,000,000 up to TSh 11,000,000 | TSh 250,000 | TSh 90,000 plus 3% of the turnover above TSh 7,000,000 |
| Above TSh 11,000,000 up to TSh 200,000,000 | 4% of turnover | 4% of turnover |

The TRA page uses one cell for the last band, which we read as the same 4 percent for both record types. Check the live table to be sure.

### Worked examples (made up for learning)

These are examples only. They are not predictions of what any business earns.

**Example 1: turnover of TSh 3,000,000.** This is below TSh 4,000,000, so the tax is nil.

**Example 2: turnover of TSh 6,000,000.**
- Incomplete records: TSh 100,000.
- Complete records: the turnover above TSh 4,000,000 is TSh 2,000,000. Three percent of TSh 2,000,000 is TSh 60,000.
- Difference: TSh 100,000 minus TSh 60,000 is TSh 40,000.

**Example 3: turnover of TSh 9,000,000.**
- Incomplete records: TSh 250,000.
- Complete records: the turnover above TSh 7,000,000 is TSh 2,000,000. Three percent of that is TSh 60,000. Add TSh 90,000 and you get TSh 150,000.
- Difference: TSh 250,000 minus TSh 150,000 is TSh 100,000.

**Example 4: turnover of TSh 20,000,000.** Four percent of TSh 20,000,000 is TSh 800,000.

Turnover is not profit. A shop can pay tax on its sales in a month when stock costs were high. Plan for it from the first day.

## New business? The first 12 months

TRA's table says a new taxpayer pays no tax for the first 12 months of business when turnover is above TSh 4,000,000 and up to TSh 200,000,000. This is a relief that you must claim. TRA says the Commissioner must be satisfied that:

- You are getting a TIN to start a business for the first time.
- Your projected yearly turnover falls entirely within the presumptive limits.
- The business will use only the presumptive tax regime for the whole relief period.

To claim it, TRA says you send an electronic application with your TIN application. It must include a statutory declaration that the business is new and will stay in the presumptive regime, a detailed description of the business activity, and a statement of projected yearly turnover.

There is one more duty. Within 30 days before the 12 months end, you must send TRA an end-of-relief turnover declaration. Put the date on your calendar from the start. For the TIN steps themselves, see our post on [how to register a small business](/post/business-ideas/startups/register-small-business-tanzania-brela-tin-licence/).

## How records change your bill

Complete records are not only for tax. They tell you what you sold, what you bought and what is left. TRA's table shows that in the bands from TSh 4,000,000 to TSh 11,000,000, complete records give a lower tax than incomplete ones, as the examples above show.

What are simple records? A notebook or phone sheet with the date, what you sold and for how much, what you bought and from whom, and your costs. Keep the receipts and invoices for purchases. TRA does not give a one-page definition of "complete" on the pages we read, so ask TRA or an approved tax consultant what they will accept for your business.

## When and how to pay

TRA's "Paying Taxes" page says that tax paid in instalments (provisional assessed tax) is paid quarterly. For a business with a year ending 31 December, the instalments are due by the end of March, June, September and December. Ask TRA how your own payments are set, because it depends on your assessment.

If you cannot pay on time, TRA says you may ask the Commissioner General in writing for more time. You must show good cause. We did not check penalty amounts, so we give none. Do not ignore a due date; ask TRA first.

## When do EFD receipts and VAT start?

- **EFD (electronic fiscal device).** TRA says a person whose yearly turnover is assessed at TSh 11 million or more must buy a device from an approved supplier and give fiscal receipts. You must not pass the device to another person.
- **VAT.** TRA says VAT registration is required when taxable turnover reaches TSh 200 million within twelve months, or TSh 100 million within six months. You must apply within 30 days. The standard VAT rate is 18 percent. TRA lists some exceptions to the threshold, so read the VAT page if you are close to it.

## Common questions

### Do I pay tax if I earn less than TSh 4,000,000?
Under the presumptive table, the tax is nil up to TSh 4,000,000 of turnover. You may still need a TIN and a licence. Ask TRA what else applies.

### Is turnover the same as profit?
No. Turnover is total sales. Profit is what is left after costs. The presumptive tax uses turnover.

### Can I choose a different tax method?
TRA lists "does not choose to leave the presumptive rules" as one condition, so there is a choice. It then means keeping proper records and filing returns. Ask TRA or an approved tax consultant first.

### Who can I ask for help?
Ask TRA through its offices or website. You can also ask a tax consultant who is approved, and ask for a written receipt for any fee.

## What to do next

1. Open the TRA income tax page and compare the table with this post.
2. Estimate your yearly sales and find your band. Use a calculator and keep the numbers honest.

Taxes become less scary when you know the numbers. A small step now saves a lot of stress later.
