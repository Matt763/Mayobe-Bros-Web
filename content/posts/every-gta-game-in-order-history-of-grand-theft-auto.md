Grand Theft Auto started as a small idea at a studio in Dundee, Scotland, and it is now one of the best-known names in video games. This post lists the main games in the order they came out, with the year, the setting, the platforms and a short note on why each one mattered. These are michezo ya video made for adults, so we keep the descriptions plain and non-graphic.

As checked on 19 November 2026, Rockstar's own page for Grand Theft Auto VI shows "Coming November 19, 2026" for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Today is that date, so this is a good moment to look back at how the series grew.

## The short answer

- The first Grand Theft Auto came out in late 1997 from DMA Design, a studio in Dundee. That studio is now called Rockstar North.
- The big change came with Grand Theft Auto III in 2001, the first game in the series in full 3D.
- Vice City (2002) and San Andreas (2004) made the series famous for big, detailed worlds.
- Grand Theft Auto V (2013) was released on PS3 and Xbox 360 and later came to newer consoles and PC.
- Every Grand Theft Auto game is a Mature, adults-only title. See our guide to [game age ratings](/post/video-games-updates/game-age-ratings-esrb-pegi-and-parental-controls/) before anyone younger plays.

## Where it began: a small studio in Dundee

DMA Design was founded in 1988 in Dundee, Scotland, by David Jones and friends from a local computer club. Its first big hit was Lemmings, released in 1991. According to Wikipedia's history of the studio, the Grand Theft Auto idea grew out of Mike Dailly's tests of drawing 3D buildings seen from above. A demo with a giant dinosaur that destroyed a city slowly turned into a driving game.

That driving game was first called Race'n'Chase. Work on it began in April 1995, and it was later renamed Grand Theft Auto. Many people shaped it, among them designers, an artist, writers and musicians. The credits list a whole team, not one person.

It is a nice thing to remember when you see the series today. A small team in one Scottish city made a top-down game on the computers of the 1990s. Nobody then could have drawn a map of where it would go.

The studio later became part of Rockstar Games' family. Take-Two Interactive merged DMA Design with Rockstar Games in March 2002, and the name changed to Rockstar North in May 2002. Today it is based in Edinburgh.

## The 1990s: top-down roots

The first game looked very different from what came later. You saw the city from above, like a map. It used three cities that share names with later games: Liberty City, San Andreas and Vice City. It was released on PC and PlayStation, and on Game Boy Color in 1999.

London 1969 followed in April 1999 as an expansion pack. It moved the action to a fictional 1960s London with period cars and British slang. It was also sold on its own. Grand Theft Auto 2 came in October 1999 and set the action in a made-up, retro-futuristic city.

These early games are simpler to look at, but they set up the idea that mattered most. You are free to go where you like in a living city.

## 2001 to 2004: the 3D trilogy

Grand Theft Auto III arrived on PlayStation 2 in October 2001, with Windows following in 2002 and Xbox in 2003. It was the first game in the series in 3D. It is often called a landmark of the open-world idea, where you roam a large city instead of playing one level after another.

Vice City followed in October 2002. It was set in 1986 and gave the series a bright, music-filled look of its own. San Andreas came in October 2004. It was set in 1992 and moved from one city to a whole state with several cities and countryside between them.

These three are often grouped as one set. In November 2021 Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition, a remastered pack of all three.

## 2005 to 2009: handheld games and Liberty City again

Liberty City Stories reached the PlayStation Portable in October 2005. It was set in 1998, before Grand Theft Auto III. It showed that a big city could fit in your hands. It later came to PlayStation 2 and phones. A second handheld story, Vice City Stories, followed in 2006.

Grand Theft Auto IV came out for PS3 and Xbox 360 in April 2008, and for Windows in December 2008. It rebuilt Liberty City in a more realistic style, with four boroughs inspired by New York. Many players remember it for its slower, more serious mood.

Chinatown Wars was released on Nintendo DS in March 2009 and then on PSP, iOS (2010) and Android (2014). It used a top-down view like the 1997 original, so the series had come round in a circle. The Ballad of Gay Tony arrived in October 2009 as the second of two expansions for Grand Theft Auto IV. It also came in a standalone pack called Episodes from Liberty City.

## 2013: Grand Theft Auto V and GTA Online

Grand Theft Auto V launched on 17 September 2013 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360. It is set in the fictional state of San Andreas, with Los Santos as its main city, based on Los Angeles. Later it came to PS4 and Xbox One (November 2014), Windows (April 2015), and PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (March 2022).

Guinness World Records lists it as the fastest entertainment property to reach $1 billion in sales. It took three days, from 17 to 20 September 2013. The record before that was held by Call of Duty: Black Ops II, which took 15 days in November 2012. We checked this on the Guinness page. We could not confirm a current total of copies sold on an official page, so we leave that number out.

GTA Online started on 1 October 2013, two weeks after the main game. Rockstar's news posts at the time said access was free with every retail copy of Grand Theft Auto V. Rockstar still adds updates. Take-Two's earnings release lists a "Kortz Center Heist" summer update from July 2026.

## Why the series grew with its players

Look at the table again and you can see a pattern. The series moved from a top-down view to 3D, then to bigger worlds, then to an online world that keeps changing. Each step followed the machines people owned, from PC and PlayStation to handhelds, phones and today's consoles.

Many people who played the PlayStation 2 games when they were young are now adults with their own jobs and children. The games grew with them. That is one reason the series is still talked about so much.

It also helps to remember that the teams grew. The 1997 game was made by a small studio. Today's games take far larger teams and years of work, which is also why release dates sometimes move.

## Common questions

### Which Grand Theft Auto game came first?

Grand Theft Auto, released in late 1997 by DMA Design. In Europe it came out on PC in November 1997 and on PlayStation in December 1997. North America followed in 1998.

### Do I have to play the games in order?

No. The stories are separate, even though some places and names return. A few games are prequels, such as Liberty City Stories, which is set before Grand Theft Auto III. You can start with whichever game you can run.

### Are the games for children?

No. The series is for adults and has a Mature rating from the age-rating bodies. Parents can read about ESRB and PEGI and how to set limits in our [guide to age ratings and parental controls](/post/video-games-updates/game-age-ratings-esrb-pegi-and-parental-controls/).

### What does DMA Design have to do with Rockstar North?

They are the same studio under different names. DMA Design was renamed Rockstar North in 2002, after Take-Two merged it with Rockstar Games.

### Is there a safe way to play the older games?

Buy them from official stores such as the PlayStation Store, the Xbox store, Steam or the Rockstar store, if they are sold in your country. Avoid free downloads from unknown sites, which are often unsafe.
