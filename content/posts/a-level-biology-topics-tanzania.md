Biology opens the door to medicine, nursing, veterinary science, wildlife management and food science. In Tanzania it is a principal subject in PCB, CBG, CBA and CBN. Many students ask where to get a Biology textbook for free. This guide answers that honestly, and also shows the topics, the exam and a study plan.

## Key points

- Biology (NECTA code 133) has two theory papers and a practical paper. In 2025, 99.62% of candidates passed.
- Form Five centres on cells, nutrition, gas exchange, coordination and classification. Form Six covers transport, growth, reproduction, genetics, evolution and ecology.
- Coordination, Evolution, Regulation and Gaseous Exchange were the weaker topics in 2025.
- Practical skills, especially drawing and dissection, are named by NECTA as a weak point.
- Use official books and reports. Do not download copied textbooks from unknown sites.

## The exam

NECTA's ACSEE formats booklet (revised July 2019) sets out the plan. Its Biology format is based on the 2009 syllabus.

| Paper | Time | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| 133/1 Biology 1 | 3 hours | Seven short questions (10 marks each) and two of three essay questions (15 marks each). 100 marks. |
| 133/2 Biology 2 | 3 hours | Five of six essay or structured questions, 20 marks each. 100 marks. |
| 133/3 Biology 3 | 3 hours 20 minutes | Practical. Three questions of 20, 15 and 15 marks. 50 marks. |

The practical has three equal versions (3A, 3B, 3C), and your school sits one. The format also says the head of the Biology department fills in an on-the-spot assessment form that goes with the scripts.

The 2025 NECTA report describes Biology 1 slightly differently: eleven questions, with eight short questions of which candidates answered seven. So check the format for your year. For May 2026, the NECTA timetable lists Biology 1 on 4 May, Biology 2 on 8 May, and practical versions 3A, 3B and 3C on 12, 18 and 21 May.

## Topics by Form Five and Form Six

The Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE) published the Biology syllabus for Form Five and Six in 2023. It follows the 2023 Advanced Secondary Education Curriculum. The syllabus gives Biology ten periods of 40 minutes each week. Here are the topics, in our own words.

**Form Five**

| Area | What you study |
|---|---|
| The cell | Cell theory, organelles and the molecules of life, with microscope work and biochemical tests |
| Photosynthesis | The light and dark reactions in C3 and C4 plants |
| Digestion | Epithelial tissue and the digestive system, including common disorders |
| Gas exchange and respiration | In mammals and plants, aerobic and anaerobic respiration, how stomata open and close |
| Coordination | Nerve impulses, sight, hearing and balance, and the use of synthetic plant hormones |
| Regulation | Feedback, urine formation and water balance in mammals |
| Classification | Classification systems, scientific names, and the main kingdoms and groups |
| Investigations | Planning, doing and reporting anatomy and physiology work |

**Form Six**

| Area | What you study |
|---|---|
| Transport | Movement of materials in plants, and blood circulation in vertebrates |
| Growth | Growth in plants and animals, including the cell cycle and metamorphosis |
| Reproduction | In plants and animals, including hormones of the menstrual cycle and pregnancy |
| Inheritance | Hereditary material, DNA replication, protein synthesis and dihybrid inheritance |
| Evolution | Theories of the origin of life, evidence for evolution and speciation |
| Ecology | Ways of studying ecosystems, biodiversity, succession and conservation |
| Project | Complete and submit the project begun in Form Five |

## Which syllabus applies to you

In July 2024 the Ministry of Education postponed the improved Form Five curriculum to 2025 (The Citizen, 28 July 2024). The 2026 Form Five joining instructions we opened ask for books under the "new curriculum" (*mtaala mpya*), but that is a school document, not a ministry statement. We did not find a NECTA format for the 2023 syllabus, and we found no official page naming the first ACSEE year to use it, as checked on 24 November 2026. Ask your teacher, and check NECTA before your exam year.

The 2023 curriculum also says students in science combinations who do not study Mathematics must take Basic Applied Mathematics, unless they passed Additional Mathematics at O-Level. It also makes Academic Communication and History of Tanzania and Ethics compulsory. Continuous assessment is 30% and the national exam 70%, according to the syllabus.

## What the 2025 results show

A total of 36,977 candidates sat Biology in 2025, and 36,836 passed. The grades were: 632 A, 5,193 B, 14,026 C, 12,593 D, 3,985 E and 407 S. NECTA's topic table uses the share of candidates who scored at least 35%:

| Topic | Share scoring 35% or more | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| Comparative studies of natural groups | 89.87% | Good |
| Cytology (cells) | 82.67% | Good |
| Genetics | 65.50% | Good |
| Gaseous exchange and respiration | 59.71% | Average |
| Regulation (homeostasis) | 58.28% | Average |
| Evolution | 46.77% | Average |
| Coordination | 43.39% | Average |

NECTA names these reasons for average results: weak knowledge of the tested ideas, little practice with dissection, poor drawing of structures, and answers that did not match what the question asked. Compare this with 2024, when some topics such as Nutrition and Transportation were rated weak and improved. So the weak spots shift every year.

## How to study Biology

1. **Draw, label and check.** Practise biological diagrams until the lines, labels and titles are neat. NECTA says drawing was a gap, and it advises practising magnification calculations.
2. **Dissect when you can.** The report suggests practising on small mammals, cockroaches and frogs. If your school has no specimens, study labelled charts and ask your teacher for a demonstration.
3. **Read the question twice.** Underline the command word, such as describe, explain or compare. NECTA lists wrong answers to the question asked as a reason for lost marks.
4. **Turn processes into steps.** Nerve impulses, the kidney, the menstrual cycle and protein synthesis are sequences. Write them as numbered steps, then draw them.
5. **Learn the vocabulary early.** Make a small list every week and test a friend.
6. **Test each other.** NECTA recommends peer and self-assessment. Swap questions with a classmate and mark each other.

## Combinations and degrees

Biology is part of **PCB**, **CBG**, **CBA** and **CBN**. Read our guides on [PCB](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/), [CBG](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-cbg-combination/) and [CBN](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-cbn-combination/).

Examples from the TCU 2026/27 guidebook:

- Medicine, dental surgery and pharmacy: Physics, Chemistry and Biology with at least 6 points and a D in each.
- Nursing: Chemistry, Biology and either Physics, Advanced Mathematics or Nutrition, with at least 6 points, a C in Chemistry and a D in Biology.
- Veterinary Medicine at Sokoine University of Agriculture: two principal passes in Chemistry and Biology and at least 4.5 points.
- Zoology and wildlife science at the University of Dar es Salaam: Biology plus one of Physics, Chemistry, Geography or Advanced Mathematics, 4.0 points.

These rules change and differ by university. Use our guide on the [TCU admission guidebook](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-read-tcu-admission-guidebook/) and on [programme entry requirements](/post/educational/career-development/how-to-read-tcu-programme-entry-requirements/).

## Official free learning materials

Many students search for "Biology textbook download free". Copied textbooks posted on unofficial sites are usually shared without the author's or publisher's permission. They can also hide harmful files, and a book written for another country's exam may not match the Tanzanian syllabus. Choose these safer options:

- **TIE.** The syllabus is on the TIE website, and TIE links its online library (Maktaba Mtandao) there. We could not open the library site from our connection on 24 November 2026, so use the link on the TIE home page. TIE's library also lends books to students and teachers at its Dar es Salaam office, and it publishes an email address for enquiries.
- **Buying.** TIE says you can buy its books at its shops in Dar es Salaam, Morogoro and Dodoma, paying with a control number.
- **School library.** Ask your teacher for the book list for your year.
- **NECTA.** The examiners' reports are free and show real questions with common mistakes.

## Common questions

### Can I study Biology without Chemistry?

In the combinations on the TAMISEMI list, Biology comes with Chemistry (PCB, CBG, CBA, CBN). We did not find a combination with Biology and no Chemistry among the science ones, apart from BNS (Biology, Food and Human Nutrition and Sports). Check the list on the Selform website.

### How do I get ready for the practical?

Practise drawing, microscope work and dissection whenever possible. In 2025 candidates did well on all three practical questions, so it is a good place to gain marks.

### Is there a free official Biology book?

TIE publishes the syllabus and runs an online library. Whether a particular student book is free to read there, we could not confirm. Ask TIE through its published contacts.

## What to do next

1. Get the Biology syllabus from the TIE website and mark each topic as strong, medium or weak.
2. Spend your first extra hours on Coordination, Evolution and Regulation.
3. Draw one labelled diagram every day for a short time.
4. If you are still choosing a combination, check the degrees you want against the guidebook.

Keep going step by step, and ask for help early.
