Posting your first ad is exciting, and a little scary. You do not know who will call or whether the ad will be accepted. This guide explains how Jiji works, what its rules say, and how to sell in a way that protects you. Everything here comes from Jiji's own help pages, as checked in November 2026. Jiji can change its rules, limits and prices at any time, so always check the page itself before you rely on a detail.

## The short answer

Jiji is a classifieds site. It connects buyers and sellers. It does not deliver goods for you and it does not hold your money.

- You post an ad, Jiji reviews it, and buyers contact you directly.
- Each category has a limit on free ads. After that you choose a paid package, or stop.
- Jiji says it has no delivery service. You and the buyer agree how the item will be delivered.
- Jiji recommends no prepayments: the buyer inspects the item and pays if satisfied.
- Your rating comes from past customers' feedback, so honest sales matter.

## How do I post an ad that gets approved?

Jiji's help page gives the steps. You sign in to your profile, press "Sell", fill in the details, and press "Post Ad". The details are a suitable category, photos, a clear title, a description and a fair price.

Then the ad is reviewed to check it follows the rules and the information is accurate. If it is accepted, Jiji sends you an email. If something needs fixing, the page says Jiji lists the edits.

Jiji's posting rules, as we read them, say:

- **Title:** precise, with the name of the item and no contact details.
- **Description:** short, clear and true. Never include false information.
- **Photos:** unique pictures you took yourself, with no contact details or watermarks.
- **Category:** choose the right one. A wrong category can lead to rejection.
- **Price:** it should match real market prices for similar items.
- **Contact details:** correct, and you should answer calls or call back.
- **Location:** products and services must be in Tanzania.
- **Legal items only:** see the prohibited list below.
- **One item per ad:** Jiji writes "1 ad = 1 item".
- **No duplicates:** identical ads can be rejected.

For better pictures, read our guide on [product photos with your phone](/post/business-ideas/e-commerce/product-photos-with-phone-online-selling-tanzania/).

## Why are ads declined?

Jiji lists reasons. They are useful as a checklist before you press "Post Ad".

- Title with repeated words, no clear item name, or contact details.
- A category that should be changed.
- A price outside the market range.
- A description that does not match the item.
- Photos that are screenshots, show several items, are not your own, are irrelevant, low quality, used before, or show contact details, prices, logos or inscriptions.
- A colour that does not match the ad.
- A duplicate of an ad that already exists.
- An item that is not allowed on Jiji.

If your ad is declined, read the reason, fix that one thing and try again.

## What can I not sell?

Jiji has a list of prohibited items, and it says the list is not complete. As checked in November 2026, it includes narcotics and prescription-only medicines, weapons, stolen property, pirated copies, sexual services, loans and money transactions, pyramid and multi-level marketing schemes, and wildlife products such as ivory. It also covers food, drinks, cosmetics, medicines and chemicals that are not registered with the proper authority, and goods that are banned or fail required standards.

Read the full page at jiji.co.tz/faq/14 before you list anything unusual. If you are unsure, do not post it.

## Ad limits and premium services

Jiji explains an "ads limit" as the number of free listings you can post in a category. Once you have used them, you choose a premium package if you want to post more.

To see your limit, Jiji says to go to its premium services page and pick the category that fits your product. The page then shows how the free listings compare with the packages. If you are unsure which package to choose, Jiji says you can ask for a call from a manager.

We do not list prices or package sizes. Jiji's own FAQ lists a "price changes" entry for March 2024, which shows that they change. Check the current figures on Jiji.

One more caution. Jiji's seller tips say premium services can help you sell faster. Treat that as marketing. No ad, free or paid, can promise you any number of customers or sales. Start free, see what happens, and spend only what your records show you can afford.

## How do I make safe deals?

Jiji's safety page is written mainly for buyers, but the advice shows you how to behave as a seller too.

- **Do not ask for prepayment.** Jiji says it recommends no prepayments. Its seller tips say to make your ad risk free by asking for no prepayment and using delivery that allows payment after inspection.
- **Meet in a public place.** A busy, open spot is safer for both sides.
- **Let the buyer inspect, then pay.** Jiji's advice is that the buyer inspects the item and pays only if satisfied.
- **Confirm the money yourself.** Never hand over goods because of a message or screenshot. Check your own mobile money wallet or bank app first. Our post on [fake payment messages](/post/business-ideas/e-commerce/fake-mobile-money-payment-messages-sellers-tanzania/) explains how.
- **Protect your details.** Jiji says never to share PIN codes or passwords, and to watch for fake payment services and fee requests.
- **Be careful with deals that look too good.** Jiji warns about unrealistic offers.

For delivery, Jiji's answer is short: it does not have a delivery service, and you can discuss a comfortable way of delivering with the buyer. If you send an item by bus or courier, keep the receipt, and wait for confirmed money first.

## How do feedback and rating work?

Jiji's tips say your rating depends on feedback from past customers, and good ratings help attract buyers. Its advice for sellers includes:

- Fill in every field in your profile and your ad.
- Answer calls and messages quickly.
- Ask customers to leave feedback after a sale.
- Never include false information about your product.

## Do I still need a TIN and records?

Selling through Jiji does not remove your duties. TRA says that a person who becomes liable to tax by carrying on a business should apply for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) within fifteen days of starting the business. TRA's page does not mention online classifieds, so this is our reading of it, not TRA's statement about Jiji.

A one-off sale of a used personal item, such as your old phone, may not be "carrying on a business". If you sell regularly to earn money, ask TRA what applies to you. ## Common questions

### Does Jiji deliver my item or hold the money?

No. Jiji says it connects buyers and sellers and has no delivery service. You and the buyer agree on payment and delivery.

### How many ads can I post for free?

It depends on the category. Jiji's premium services page shows the free limit for each category. We do not quote numbers because they can change.

### Why was my ad declined?

Common reasons are a poor photo, a wrong category, a price outside the market range, a duplicate ad, or a prohibited item. Jiji's help page lists the reason and the edit needed.

### Should I take a deposit?

Jiji recommends no prepayments. Taking a deposit can make buyers doubt you, and some people use a deposit request as a scam sign. Meet, let the buyer inspect and then confirm payment.

## What to do next

1. Read Jiji's posting rules and prohibited items pages in full.
2. Take your own clear photos of one item and write a plain, honest title.
3. Post one ad, check the reason if it is declined, and fix it.
4. Decide how you will handle payment and delivery before the first call comes.

Your first ad does not need to be perfect. It needs to be true, clear and safe, and you can improve it each time.
