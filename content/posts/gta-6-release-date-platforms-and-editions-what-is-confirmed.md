Grand Theft Auto VI, the new Rockstar Games mchezo that people have waited for since 2023, has a release date of 19 November 2026. That is today. This page lists what Rockstar and the platform stores have confirmed, what has changed over time, and what has not been announced.

Everything below was checked on 19 November 2026. We could not confirm from official pages whether the game is already playable where you are, so check your own store before you plan your day.

## The short answer

- **Release date:** 19 November 2026, according to Rockstar's store page and the PlayStation Store page.
- **Platforms:** PlayStation 5 (including PS5 Pro) and Xbox Series X|S. It is not for PS4.
- **PC:** no release date or window has been announced.
- **Editions:** Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition.
- **US prices on the PlayStation Store:** $79.99 (Standard) and $99.99 (Ultimate). We found no confirmed price in Tanzanian shillings.
- **Age rating:** M (Mature 17+) from the ESRB and PEGI 18. It is a game for adults.

## How the date has changed

The date has moved twice, so it is fair to ask whether it can move again. Here is the timeline from an independent tracker (Rockstar Report) and from TechRadar, which reported Rockstar's own message.

| Date | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 4 December 2023 | First trailer. The game was given a 2025 window. |
| 2 May 2025 | Moved from fall 2025 to 26 May 2026. |
| 6 November 2025 | Moved again to 19 November 2026. |
| 25 June 2026 | Pre-orders opened (per the tracker). |
| 10 November 2026 | Digital pre-load began. |
| 12 November 2026 | Code-in-box copies reached shops (per the tracker). |
| 19 November 2026 | Scheduled launch. |

When Rockstar announced the second move in November 2025, it wrote: "We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait." It said the extra months were to finish the game with the polish players expect.

Rockstar's own news pages for both dates still exist, titled "Grand Theft Auto VI is Now Coming May 26, 2026" and "Grand Theft Auto VI is Now Set to Launch November 19, 2026". The first date is now history. The second is the one on the store pages today.

Once pre-loads and shop copies are out, a last-minute move is much less likely. That is our reading, not a promise from Rockstar. Only Rockstar's channels can confirm a change.

## Platforms: what we know about PC

Rockstar's store page and the PlayStation Store list PS5 and PS5 Pro. The tracker and several news sites list Xbox Series X|S as well. Nothing on the pages we opened mentions PS4, Xbox One or mobile.

PC has no date. You may see videos or posts that give a PC month. Treat those as guesses until Rockstar says it. If your plan is to play on a computer, the honest answer is to wait for an announcement.

## Editions, bonuses and prices

Two editions are listed.

- **Standard Edition:** $79.99 on the PlayStation Store.
- **Ultimate Edition:** $99.99. It adds extra vehicles, weapons and clothing, according to the PlayStation Store.

The PlayStation page also lists an "Ultimate Edition Upgrade" as a separate add-on. On the day we checked it showed as unavailable, so we cannot say how or when it can be bought.

Digital pre-orders came with the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription. The offer wording on the PlayStation page is not consistent: one part says purchases must be made before 20 November 2026, another says 19 November. Read the offer terms on your store before you rely on it. The page also says the GTA+ month must be claimed by 31 March 2027.

A "code-in-box" version is listed on Rockstar's store. It is a box that holds a download code, with no disc inside. If you buy a boxed copy, you still need a download and storage space.

## Age rating: who it is for

The PlayStation Store lists M (Mature 17+) from the ESRB. The ESRB descriptors shown are blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, strong sexual content, and use of drugs and alcohol. Rockstar's store lists PEGI 18, with descriptors for violence, sex, drugs and in-game purchases.

In plain words, this game is for adults. If you are a parent, do not buy it for a child because they ask or because friends are playing. ## What is still not announced

As checked on 19 November 2026, we did not find an official answer on these points:

- A PC version or date.
- A price in Tanzanian shillings. The prices we found are in US dollars.
- Whether your local store will sell it at the same price as the US store.
- Any detail about online modes beyond Rockstar's note that some features may need an internet connection and a Rockstar Games account, and may cost extra.

Rockstar's store page shows an "unavailable in your region" notice to some visitors. We cannot tell whether it appears for Tanzania, because it depends on where the page is opened.

## What Tanzanian readers should check before buying

Waiting years can make anyone want to rush. A few calm checks save money.

1. **Open the store on your own console.** Look for the game in the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store for your account's region. The price you see there is the one that counts, not a price in a social media post.
2. **Check how you will pay.** Cards, gift cards and mobile money do not work the same on every store account. Confirm your payment method before launch day, not during it.
3. **Check storage and data.** A 212 GB download is a big cost on mobile data. Use home internet or a data plan you trust.
4. **Buy only from official stores or known shops.** Be careful with "cheap codes" and "free GTA 6" offers. They are common around big launches. See our guide on [GTA 6 scams, fake downloads and leaks](/post/video-games-updates/gta-6-scams-fake-downloads-and-leaks-how-to-stay-safe/).
5. **Check the date again.** Rockstar's own site and its Newswire are the place to confirm any change.
