You finished Form Four, you waited for your results, and now you wait again. This guide explains selection ya kidato cha tano: who decides, where to look for your own name, what the joining instructions ask you to bring, and what to do if the result is not what you hoped. It does not list student names. The government publishes the lists every year, and the steps below show you how to find yours.

## Key points

- Form Five selection is run by the President's Office, Regional Administration and Local Government (PO-RALG, also called TAMISEMI). It is not run by the school you finished.
- You find your result on the official Selform portal, `selform.tamisemi.go.tz`, by choosing the selection round, then your region, council and school.
- Each selected student has joining instructions for the school. Read them in full before you buy anything.
- In recent years the government has said that school transfers are not possible because places are limited.
- Nobody can sell you a place or a change of school. Treat every such offer as a scam.

All details here are as checked on 24 November 2026. Dates and steps change from year to year, so confirm them on the official portal.

## How does Form Five selection work?

After the Form Four examination (CSEE), NECTA releases the results. For the 2025 examination, NECTA released them on 31 January 2026, according to The Citizen. Selection for Form Five and technical colleges follows later in the year. In 2026, the minister announced the placements in early June, reported The Citizen.

The minister said in 2025 that selection was done "based on performance and available space". That is the government's own wording, and it tells you two things. Your results matter, and the number of places matters too.

Here is what the government reported for 2026, as quoted by The Citizen:

- 246,197 students were selected for Form Five and technical colleges.
- 172,114 were placed in Form Five at 856 secondary schools.
- 74,083 were placed in technical and vocational institutions, including health diploma and teacher training colleges.

So not every selected student goes to Form Five. Some are placed in a college for a diploma. If you are in that group, the college sends your admission instructions, as the minister said.

## What decides your school and your combination?

Two things work together.

First, your choices. Before selection, Form Four leavers can fill in or change their choices of schools, colleges and combinations in the Selform system. The official student manual from TAMISEMI (March 2019 edition) says a student logs in with the exam index number, for example in the form S0101.0020.2018, and can change personal details and choices. The manual also says the student sees only the combinations and schools that fit the student's results.

Second, your results and the places available. A combination is the set of subjects you study in Form Five and Form Six, such as PCB or HGL. Our guide to the [A-Level combinations and the careers they lead to](/post/educational/combination-na-aina-ya-kazi-utakayofanya/) explains the letters. For one example in detail, see [jobs and degrees with the PCB combination](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/).

In 2024 the government added 49 new combinations, bringing the total to 65, and opened an online window for Form Four graduates of 2023 to revise their choices, as reported by the Daily News. That window ran from 19 March to 30 April 2024. Windows like this change each year. As checked on 24 November 2026, the Selform home page shows a notice sending students to a second site, SIS (`sis.tamisemi.go.tz`), to fill in their choices. We could not open any steps inside SIS, so follow the instructions on the page itself.

If you want to understand your own result first, read [how to read your Form Four results](/post/educational/results/how-to-read-csee-results-divisions-points/).

## How do I check my selection on the official portal?

Use only the official address. As checked on 24 November 2026, the steps look like this.

1. Open `selform.tamisemi.go.tz` in your browser.
2. Go to the selection and allocation page (`selform.tamisemi.go.tz/content/selection-and-allocation/`). It was headed "Uchaguzi wa Wanafunzi wa Kidato cha Tano na Vyuo vya Kati, 2026" and listed one round, "First Selection, 2026".
3. Open the round. The page says "Chagua mkoa ulikosoma", which means choose the region where you studied.
4. Choose your council (halmashauri), then the school you finished.
5. Find your index number in the list. The list shows the school or college you were selected for, the combination or course, the type of school and where the school is.
6. Open the joining instructions for your new school. On the school list, each school name links to its instructions as a PDF file.

The same page also had a search by index number and a column for joining instructions, but when we opened it, that part of the page showed no content. If search does not work for you, use the region, council and school path above.

There was also a link for "all students sent to one school", which lists the students placed in each school.

Keep your own index number ready. It looks like S0806.0001.2025: the school's number, your number at that school and the year. Check that the year is the one you sat the exam.

Be careful with copies of lists on blogs and social media. They can be wrong, old or fake, and they often ask for your phone number or money. Go to the portal yourself.

## What do the joining instructions contain?

Joining instructions are called "Maelekezo ya kujiunga na Kidato cha Tano". Each school writes its own. We read the 2026 documents of several government schools. They differ in detail, but they share a pattern.

- **A letter to the parent or guardian.** It states the school, the combination, the date term starts, the date to report and the last day for reporting. In the copies on the portal the dates are blank, so use the date in the government announcement or the one the school gives you.
- **School uniform and personal items.** Examples are shirts, shoes, a sweater, a mattress, a lockable trunk, a mosquito net, bedsheets and exercise books. Each school lists its own.
- **Books and equipment.** Some ask for a scientific calculator, or textbooks for your combination.
- **Contributions.** Several schools list a total, paid into the school's bank account with a pay-in slip that carries the student's name. Bring the receipt on reporting day. The amounts differ by school. One school in the 2026 set listed TSh 80,000 for its contributions. Do not treat that as a national figure.
- **Forms to fill in.** A medical examination form completed by a recognised hospital, a form with student and parent details, a student contract and the school rules.
- **Papers to bring.** Two of the 2026 letters we read asked for a copy of the birth certificate and a copy of the Form Four result slip, not the originals.
- **Health insurance.** Some letters ask for proof of health insurance, or an amount of TSh 50,400 to buy it at school. Read your own letter for this.
- **Rules about phones.** The letters we read say students must not bring phones to school.

Read the letter of your own school only. A friend's list may not apply to you.

## When do I report, and what if I am late?

For 2026, The Citizen reported that the first term would begin on 6 July 2026, with reporting between 4 and 31 July 2026. In 2025 the reporting window was 6 to 21 July, and in 2024 the minister gave 14 July as the deadline. So the pattern is that selection is announced around May or June and reporting happens in July. The exact dates are announced each year.

What happens if you miss the date? We could not find a general government rule that applies to everyone. One school's letter we read says that delaying a child's arrival on opening day "can cause loss of the place given". Another says reporting ends within two weeks of the start of term. If you cannot report on time, tell the school head as early as you can and keep proof of the reason, such as a medical note. Do not wait until the last day.

## What if I am not happy with my school or combination?

Be honest with yourself about the limits. In June 2025 the minister said there would be no room for school changes because of a lack of vacancies, and in 2026 the minister said the same about the top performing schools, whose places are full. The Citizen reported both statements. So do not plan on a transfer.

What may still be possible:

- **During the choice window,** you can revise your choices in the system, if a window is open for your year. After selection results are out, that window has closed.
- **For health reasons.** TAMISEMI published a 2026 list of Form Five students whose combination and school were changed for health reasons. We could not find the official steps for asking, so ask the school head or the regional education office what documents are needed.
- **If you were not selected at all.** Our post on [what to do after poor Form Four results](/post/educational/results/what-to-do-after-poor-form-four-results/) covers retaking the exam and technical certificates, and says to check that a college is registered.

## Common questions

### Who selects students for Form Five?

PO-RALG, through TAMISEMI, runs the selection and publishes it on the Selform portal. Your school does not choose for you.

### Is the selection list the same every year?

No. A new list is published each year for that year's Form Four leavers. Make sure you open the round for your own year.

### Can I pay to change my school or combination?

No. No one can sell you a place. If someone asks for money, do not pay, and tell your school head.

### Do I need my result slip when I report?

Some letters ask for a copy of it. Read your own joining instructions and keep the original safe.

## What to do next

1. Open the official portal yourself and find your index number through region, council and school.
2. Download the joining instructions for your school and read them with a parent or guardian.
3. Make a list of the items and papers, with prices from nearby shops, and plan the contributions before reporting day.
4. Save your index number, receipts and every message in one folder.

Form Five is a big step, and you earned your place. Take it one task at a time.
