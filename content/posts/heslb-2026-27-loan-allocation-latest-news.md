The Higher Education Students' Loans Board (HESLB) has released another list of students allocated loans and grants for the 2026/27 academic year. The public notice is dated 13 November 2026 and was posted on the HESLB website on 14 November. This post sets out what the three notices say, what they do not say, and what to do if you applied and are still waiting.

All facts below were checked on 23 November 2026, on HESLB's own news page and in press reports. "2026/27" is the intake year, meaning students who start or continue university in the academic year that began in 2026. It is not the same as the 2026 exam year.

## Key points

- HESLB's latest notice (dated 13 November 2026) lists 46,435 students allocated loans and grants worth TSh 148.03 billion.
- It says 1,807 of the first-year students in that list received an allocation after their appeals succeeded.
- Earlier, on 10 October, HESLB listed 165,775 students (TSh 535.7 billion). On 21 October it listed 90,235 more (TSh 284.73 billion).
- The notice tells students who were not allocated a loan to apply when the 2027/28 window opens. It gives no opening date.
- Keep checking your own SIPA account. Never pay anyone to "put your name on the list".

## What HESLB announced

HESLB has released the 2026/27 allocations in stages. The Executive Director, Dr Bill Kiwia, told the press in October that there was no fixed number of batches, because allocation depends on admissions and on results sent in by universities.

| Date of notice | Students | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 October 2026 (first list) | 165,775 | TSh 535.7 billion |
| 21 October 2026 (second list) | 90,235 | TSh 284.73 billion |
| 13 November 2026 (latest list) | 46,435 | TSh 148.03 billion |

For the second list, HESLB also gave a running total: 256,010 students and TSh 820.4 billion across the first two lists. The November notice does not give a new running total, so we do not add one here.

### The notice of 13 November 2026

The notice breaks the 46,435 students down like this:

- **First-year students:** 15,374 students, loans worth TSh 48.24 billion. The notice says this includes 1,807 first-year students who were allocated after a successful appeal.
- **Second year and above:** 25,371 students, loans worth TSh 80.64 billion. Of these, 5,422 received a loan for the first time, worth TSh 17.67 billion.
- **Samia Scholarship:** 37 new students (grants of TSh 188.12 million) and 231 continuing students (TSh 1.29 billion).

One thing we could not explain: the lines in the notice add up to fewer students than the headline figure of 46,435, even with the Samia Scholarship lines included. The notice does not say why. Use the official total, and if the difference matters to you, ask HESLB.

### The earlier lists

The first list (10 October) covered 74,769 first-year degree students, 8,538 first-year diploma students, 873 new Samia Scholarship students, and continuing students. The second list (21 October) covered 25,630 first-year degree students, 3,445 diploma students, 417 students of the Postgraduate Diploma in Legal Practice, 6,225 continuing students getting a loan for the first time, and others. In that second notice HESLB said allocation for first-year students was complete and that it was still allocating grants for new Samia Scholarship students.

### The application window

HESLB's own notice of 19 June 2026 says the window opened that day, would stay open for 73 days and would close on 31 August 2026. It also launched four guidelines (degree, diploma, Postgraduate Diploma in Legal Practice and Samia Scholarship) and asked applicants to use the OLAMS system. Daily News reported on 5 September that the deadline was extended by 14 days to 14 September, and that Dr Kiwia said there would be no further extension. We did not find the extension notice on HESLB's own news list, so check heslb.go.tz if the date matters to you.

## What it means if you applied

HESLB's October notices tell applicants who are not yet allocated to keep checking their application status on SIPA, the Student's Individual Permanent Account. Your SIPA account is the place to look. We could not confirm from the notices where the full list of names is published, so do not rely on a list that someone sends you on WhatsApp.

Three practical points:

1. **Check your own account, not a list.** Log in to SIPA with your own details.
2. **Keep your details complete.** HESLB has said that beneficiaries must keep their information, including bank account and phone details, complete so money can be paid.
3. **Admission comes first.** HESLB has said a student must be admitted to a higher learning institution before a loan is considered. If you are chosen by more than one university, read how to [confirm just one admission](/post/educational/results/how-to-confirm-university-admission-tcu-multiple-selection/).

The notices do not say when the money will be paid, so we cannot give a payment date. Ask your university's loans office or HESLB.

## If your name is not there

The 21 October notice said students not yet allocated who have extra information to support an appeal could appeal from 25 October 2026. The 13 November notice says 1,807 first-year students were allocated after appeals, so the appeal route did change results for some people.

The 13 November notice itself gives no appeal steps and no closing date for appeals. It advises students who did not get a loan to apply when the 2027/28 window opens. That is what the notice says; we could not find a later notice that reopens appeals, so check HESLB's website or call before you assume the window is shut.

For how the appeal form works and what to prepare, read our guide: [No HESLB loan allocated? How the appeal works](/post/educational/results/heslb-loan-not-allocated-what-to-do-appeal/).

## Beware of fake lists and fees

HESLB does not take payment to place a name on a list. Messages that say "pay TSh 20,000 to be added" are fraud. Be careful with:

- blog posts and WhatsApp files that claim to hold "the full list of names". They can be old, wrong or copied from a scam;
- anyone who asks for your SIPA password or an SMS code;
- people who say they can "clear" or reduce a loan for a fee. On 4 September 2026 HESLB reported that a man who posed as one of its staff and told borrowers he could cancel their loans admitted the offence in the High Court, Kigoma zone, and was ordered to pay a fine and return the money he took. Dr Kiwia told borrowers to use the proper repayment process and avoid middlemen;
- loan apps that pretend to be HESLB. Our post on [checking whether a loan app is real](/post/technology-solutions/cyber-security/fake-loan-apps-tanzania-how-to-check/) explains how to check.

HESLB's published contacts are +255 736 665 533 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 4pm) and info@heslb.go.tz. Its office is at PSSSF House, Makole Road, Dodoma.

## Common questions

### Is the 2026/27 allocation finished?

We could not confirm that. The October notice said first-year allocation was complete, and the 21 October notice said continuing students would be allocated as universities send their results. HESLB has not said that the 13 November list is the last one.

### Does "allocated" mean I have already been paid?

No. The notices are about allocation, meaning HESLB has decided to give you a loan or grant. They do not give payment dates.

### How much will I get?

It depends on your criteria and need. In October HESLB said loans range from TSh 3 million to TSh 3.6 million, as reported by Daily News. Your own amount is shown on your SIPA account.

### I applied for the 2026 exam year. Is that a different thing?

Yes, do not mix them up. "2026/27" is the university year for students who start in late 2026. If you sat your Form Six exam in 2026, you will apply for 2027/28 when that window opens.

## What to do next

1. Log in to SIPA and read your own status today.
2. If you are not allocated and have new information, check heslb.go.tz or call HESLB before you decide the appeal is closed.
3. Ignore anyone who sells a place on a list.
4. If you need the full process for next year, see our [HESLB application and repayment guide](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/).
