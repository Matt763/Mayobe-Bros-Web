Grand Theft Auto V, or GTA 5, came out in 2013, and people still play it every day. Many readers ask the same thing: why is an old mchezo still so popular, and is it worth buying now? This guide explains the game, the different versions, what you need to run it and who should play it. It is a game for adults.

All versions, prices and requirements were checked on 24 November 2026. They change, so confirm them on the official store page.

## Key points

- Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world game from Rockstar North, set in the fictional city of Los Santos and the countryside around it.
- It has two parts: Story Mode, which you can play alone, and GTA Online, a separate online world with its own rules and in-game purchases.
- It first came out on 17 September 2013 for PS3 and Xbox 360. Later versions exist for PC and for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- Take-Two says it has sold in nearly 235 million units, which is why it is so well known.
- It is rated M (Mature) by the ESRB. It is not for children.

## What is Grand Theft Auto V?

Rockstar describes the story in one line: a young street hustler, a retired bank robber and a terrifying psychopath get pulled into the criminal underworld, and they must pull off dangerous heists to survive in a city where nobody can be trusted.

Those three people are the game's big idea. Rockstar's 2013 launch announcement said it was the first time in the series that players could jump in and out of the lives of three playable lead characters. You switch between them during the game. The story is set in a made-up version of Southern California, with the big city of Los Santos and a wide countryside called Blaine County.

You can drive, fly, swim, play golf and do side activities, or you can follow the missions. The launch announcement mentions things like golf, base jumping and yoga alongside the heists. You choose your pace.

## What made it special

**Three heroes, one story.** Switching between the characters changes how the story feels. Each person has a different life, so missions have different moods.

**A huge, detailed map.** Many reviewers at launch praised the size of the world. We will not quote scores here, because scores change with time and taste. The simple fact is that the map is large and full of activities.

**A game that kept growing.** GTA Online started on 1 October 2013 and has received many updates since. The latest update in Take-Two's November 2026 results list is a summer update from July 2026. Few games from 2013 are still updated.

**It was sold again and again for new machines.** Rockstar released it on PC in April 2015, then on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in March 2022, and a PC "Enhanced" version in March 2025. Each release brought new players.

### PC: Enhanced and Legacy

Rockstar's March 2025 announcement says the Enhanced version is a free upgrade for people who already own the game on PC, and they can move their Story Mode and Online progress across. Someone who buys it for the first time gets Enhanced and the original Legacy version. Enhanced added ray tracing, support for AMD FSR and NVIDIA DLSS, DualSense controller support and faster loading on suitable drives.

Rockstar also says the Legacy version stays for people whose computer is too weak for Enhanced. But players on the two versions are kept apart. If you want to play online with friends, you must all be on the same version.

On 15 April 2025, Rockstar said the Enhanced version was also available through PC Game Pass in the Xbox app, as well as the Rockstar Games Launcher, Steam and Epic Games Store.

That is a big game. Many shared and school computers will not meet it. The Legacy version needs less: Steam lists 4 GB of RAM and an older graphics card, but it needs 125 GB of space. These figures come from the Steam page, which is the publisher's own text.

If your computer is slow, try our steps to [speed up a slow Windows PC for games](/post/video-games-updates/speed-up-slow-windows-pc-for-games/). A cheap or old laptop may still be too weak, and that is a fair reason to play on a console or wait.

## Story Mode and GTA Online are different

Story Mode is a finished story you can play alone. It needs no one else and works well for a first try.

GTA Online is a separate online world for up to 30 players, according to Rockstar. You build a character, earn money, start businesses and join heists or races. It is updated often, which is good, but it also has things to know:

- You need an internet connection and a Rockstar account. Online play in a country with expensive data can use a lot of bundles.
- Rockstar sells in-game money. Parents should set limits first.
- Lag matters. Our guide to [reducing lag and high ping](/post/video-games-updates/reduce-lag-and-high-ping-in-online-games/) can help.
- Some modes are crowded with experienced players, and a new player may feel lost at the start. Rockstar added a Career Builder to help new players choose a starting path.

### Mods and cheating

Many people ask about mods. Rockstar's 2025 announcement says the upgraded PC version includes kernel-based anti-cheat protection, and it points to its Community Guidelines. We could not open Rockstar's current support page on mods, so we cannot quote its exact rules. The safe approach is simple. Do not use mods or cheat tools in GTA Online, and read Rockstar Support before changing any game files. Mod files from random sites are also a common way to spread malware.

## How it plays and looks now

The game is more than ten years old, but it still looks good, especially the Enhanced and console versions. The controls are smooth and the city is busy. Some parts feel old, such as a few missions that repeat and a story that some players find less warm than earlier games. That is opinion, not fact.

The game is also large and slow to install. Plan for the time and the data.

## Honest pros and cons

**Pros**

- A big world with plenty to do.
- Three playable characters give the story variety.
- Still updated, with a lively online world.
- Plays well on many platforms.

**Cons**

- Large install, and a demanding PC for Enhanced.
- GTA Online has in-game purchases and can feel hard to enter.
- Content for adults only.
- The online world needs a steady connection.

- **You have never played a Grand Theft Auto game:** yes, it is a good entry point, and it gives you a story and a big world.
- **You want to play with friends online:** yes, if everyone has the same version and a good connection.
- **You prefer story with less online play:** play Story Mode only. For a more serious story, see [our GTA 4 guide](/post/video-games-updates/classic-games/gta-4-worth-playing-today/).

## Common questions

### Is GTA 5 free?

No. The game is sold in official stores. Offers of "free" copies are illegal and often carry malware. See our post on [GTA scams and fake downloads](/post/video-games-updates/gta-6-scams-fake-downloads-and-leaks-how-to-stay-safe/).

### Do I need to buy GTA Online separately?

Rockstar's page says the Grand Theft Auto V version includes Story Mode and GTA Online. GTA Online is also listed as its own product on some platforms, so read the store page.

### How many copies has it sold?

In a press release on 19 November 2026, Rockstar says the game has sold in nearly 235 million units. "Sold in" means shipped to shops and stores, not the number of players.

### Can I play on a phone?

We found no official phone version on Rockstar's pages.
