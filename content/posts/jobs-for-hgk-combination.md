HGK means History, Geography and Kiswahili as your three principal subjects in Form Five and Form Six. Many students call these "arts combinations", and some people wrongly treat them as the easy road. The TCU guidebook says otherwise: HGK qualifies you for arts teaching, public administration, social work, law, tourism and more. This guide is for Form Four leavers choosing a combination, Form Six students waiting for results, and parents helping with the choice.

We read the entry rules below in the Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) 2026/27 guidebook in November 2026. Rules change each year, so use this as a map and check the latest edition before you apply.

## Key points

- NECTA lists HGK among the arts combinations: History, Geography and Kiswahili. General Studies is compulsory for all candidates.
- The strongest path is a Bachelor of Arts with Education. Several universities accept History, Geography and Kiswahili for it.
- Many degrees ask only for "two principal passes" from a long list that includes all three of your subjects, with a 4.0 points minimum.
- HGK does not fit degrees that need Economics, Advanced Mathematics or two language passes such as Kiswahili plus English.
- Entry is competitive and no result guarantees a place or a job.

## Is this combination only for the weak? No

Some students hear that arts combinations are "for those who cannot do science". That idea is false, and it costs people good futures.

The guidebook does not rank combinations by pride. It lists the subjects a programme needs and the points you must reach. HGK students sit the same Form Six examination as everyone else and must read widely, argue clearly and write well. Law, teaching and public service all need exactly these skills.

If you are afraid of choosing wrong, that fear shows you take your future seriously. Choose with facts, not with gossip.

## What the TCU guidebook says about points

The guidebook is published every year for Form Six leavers. It lists each degree, the subjects asked for, the minimum points, the number of places and the years of study.

For results from 2016 onward, the points are A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and S = 0.5. For most degrees you need two principal passes with at least 4.0 points in the subjects the programme names.

Say you get C in History, C in Kiswahili and D in Geography. The two subjects that count for a programme might be History and Kiswahili, which gives 3 + 3 = 6 points. That is above 4.0 on paper, but universities receive more applicants than places, so stronger grades are safer. Two universities, UDSM and Mkwawa, list "5 from 3 subjects" instead of 4.0. Ask them how they count it.

## Degrees HGK can reach

The table shows entries we found in the 2026/27 guidebook where History, Geography and Kiswahili qualify. Each university sets its own entry, so open the exact line for your choice.

|---|---|---|---|
| Bachelor of Arts with Education | University of Dodoma | Two passes from History, Geography, Kiswahili, English and other arts subjects | 4.0 |
| Bachelor of Arts with Education | University of Dar es Salaam | Two passes from History, Geography, Kiswahili, English, Economics and others | 5 from 3 subjects |
| Bachelor of Arts with Education | Mkwawa University College of Education | Two passes from History, Geography, Kiswahili, English and others | 5 from 3 subjects |

Most of these programmes run for 3 years. Law at the University of Dodoma is listed at 4 years.

## Teaching: the most direct path

A Bachelor of Arts with Education trains you to teach arts subjects in secondary schools. Look at the listed places in the guidebook: the University of Dodoma lists 1,800, Mkwawa 1,450 and UDSM 1,386. Large numbers mean many seats exist, though they are still competitive and the guidebook cannot tell you how many applicants there will be.

Teaching Kiswahili, History or Geography is a calling as well as a job. We could not confirm current pay figures from an official source, so we do not give any. Pay and hiring depend on the employer and the year.

## Kiswahili as a profession

If you love the language, the University of Dodoma lists a Swahili-medium programme, Shahada ya Awali ya Sanaa katika Kiswahili. It asks for at least a C in Kiswahili and one other listed subject.

Do not assume every language degree accepts HGK. The Mkwawa programmes in Translation and Interpretation and in Literary Studies and Creative Writing list only language subjects such as Kiswahili, Fasihi ya Kiswahili, English, French and Chinese. HGK gives you just one of these, so you would likely fall short. Read each line before you dream about it.

## Public service, law and community work

The University of Dodoma's Political Science and Public Administration and International Relations lines list all three HGK subjects. Mzumbe University lists Public Administration at 4.5 points with one subsidiary pass. That is a higher bar, so ask Mzumbe which subsidiary counts.

Law needs a closer look. UDOM asks for two principal passes at D or above and says an applicant without History or English at A-level needs a C at O-level. HGK students have History, so that rule is met. Some other universities ask for English at O-level or a subsidiary pass in English. Check your target university's line.

Social work and community development accept HGK at the institutes and universities in the table. They suit people who want to work with families, villages and local councils.

Tourism is also open. The Institute of Accountancy Arusha lists a Bachelor of Tourism and Hospitality that accepts History, Geography and Kiswahili among its subjects. Our post on [Tanzania's tourism boom](/post/educational/tanzania-tourism-boom-kilimanjaro-serengeti-zanzibar-world/) gives background on that field.

## If you are in Form Four: is HGK right for you?

Check these before you choose.

1. **Kiswahili:** do you read and write it well, and enjoy essays?
2. **History:** can you remember events and explain causes in your own words?
3. **Geography:** do you like maps, climate and how people use land?
4. **English:** if you want law or journalism, your English must be strong. Even though English is not in HGK, some universities set an English rule.
5. **Your dream:** write three jobs you could imagine. Search each degree in the guidebook.

If you want medicine or engineering, HGK is the wrong door. See the [PCB guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/) for health careers.

## A note to your heart

You may be the first in your family to reach Form Five. Your parents may be selling crops or stretching a small salary to pay for your books. You feel the weight of their hope, and that is real.

Here is the honest truth. No combination promises you a degree or a job. But a student who studies steadily, asks questions and plans early has real chances, and HGK is a fair combination to build them on. Imagine you sit in a lecture hall in three years, holding the first book your family ever needed a degree for. That picture is possible. Work toward it one week at a time.

## If you are in Form Six: after the results

1. Add your best points from your three principal subjects and compare them with the guidebook lines above.
2. Shortlist 5 programmes, not one. Mix a hard choice with a safer one.
3. Apply only through the official university or TCU systems. Nobody can sell you a place.
4. Read our guide on [how to read the TCU guidebook](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-read-tcu-admission-guidebook/) before you submit.
5. Look at [how to apply for a HESLB student loan](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/) early.

## What if your points are not enough?

1. **Try a related programme.** Social work, community development and tourism often accept the same subjects with less competition than law.
2. **Look at diploma routes.** Colleges registered with NACTVET offer diplomas in related fields. Check accreditation first. TCU also has a separate guidebook for diploma holders who want to join a degree later.
3. **Improve your weakest subject early** in Form Five, not in the final month.
4. **Plan another year.** Some students retake examinations. Talk to a teacher first, because it costs time and money.

## Common questions

### What jobs can I get with HGK?

HGK can lead to teaching, public administration, social work, community development, tourism and law, depending on the degree and your points. A degree does not guarantee a job, and openings vary by year and employer.

### Can HGK students study law?

Yes, at several universities. The University of Dodoma lists Law at 4.0 points, and HGK students have History, which some law rules ask for. Check the English rule for each university.

### Can I study economics with HGK?

Usually not. The UDSM Bachelor of Arts in Economics asks for Economics or Advanced Mathematics, which HGK does not include. See [HGE](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-hge-combination/) if economics is your goal.

### Is HGK enough for journalism?

It can be. The University of Dodoma lists a BA in Journalism and Public Relations that accepts History, Geography and Kiswahili, but it has an English grade rule. Read the full line before you apply.

### What if I want to change my combination?

Talk to your school early. Changes after Form Five starts depend on the school and the education officers, and we could not confirm a national rule.

## What to do next

1. Open the TCU guidebook and search for three programmes that interest you.
2. Write down the subjects and points each one asks for.
3. Compare them with your current marks in History, Geography and Kiswahili.
4. Talk to a teacher or a university student in that field.
