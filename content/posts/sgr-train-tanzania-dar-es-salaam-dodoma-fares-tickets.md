The electric Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) now carries passengers between Dar es Salaam, Morogoro and Dodoma. In Swahili it is called reli ya kisasa, the modern railway. This post explains what the train costs, how to buy a ticket, why seats sometimes look sold out, and what is coming next. We checked the facts in November 2026 against official documents and news reports.

## The short answer

- The government regulator LATRA set maximum fares for the ordinary class in June 2024. Dar es Salaam to Dodoma is TSh 31,000 on the train that stops at every station and TSh 50,000 on the express.
- Children aged 4 to 12 pay half. Children under 4 do not pay a fare.
- Tickets are sold on the Tanzania Railways Corporation (TRC) ticket website, sgrticket.trc.co.tz. TRC's call centre number is 0800 11 00 42.
- TRC added more trips from 1 September 2026, and one extra Friday train from Dodoma in June 2026. Buy early, because popular trips fill up.
- Trains to Mwanza are expected to start in 2027 and to Kigoma in 2028, if the work and testing finish on time.

## What the SGR is

"Standard gauge" describes the distance between the two rails, 1.435 metres. It is the same width as most of the world's main railways. TRC says the trains are electric and can run at 160 km per hour or faster.

The government is building the line in sections. The Dar es Salaam to Morogoro section is 300 km and the Morogoro to Makutupora section, near Singida, is 422 km, according to TRC. Passenger service started in mid-2024. A news report from August 2024 said the Dar es Salaam to Dodoma trip takes about three hours, compared with eight to ten hours by bus.

The Daily News reported on 24 August 2026 that more than seven million passengers had travelled on the SGR since passenger services began in July 2024. We could not find TRC's own figure, so treat that number as a news report.

## What the fares are

LATRA is the Land Transport Regulatory Authority. In Order No. 01 of 2024, published in the Government Gazette on 14 June 2024, it set the highest fares TRC may charge for the ordinary class ("daraja la kawaida"). The order lists fares from Dar es Salaam to each station on the line.

| From Dar es Salaam to | Distance | Adult, stops at every station | Child 4 to 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pugu | 19 km | TSh 1,000 | TSh 500 |
| Ruvu | 73 km | TSh 5,000 | TSh 2,500 |
| Morogoro | 192 km | TSh 13,000 | TSh 6,500 |
| Kilosa | 265 km | TSh 18,000 | TSh 9,000 |
| Dodoma | 444 km | TSh 31,000 | TSh 15,500 |
| Makutupora | 531 km | TSh 37,000 | TSh 18,500 |

The order also lists Soga, Ngerengere, Mkata, Kidete, Gulwe, Igandu and Bahi. Open the LATRA page in the sources below for the full list.

The express train stops at fewer stations and costs more:

| Trip | Adult, express | Child 4 to 12 |
|---|---|---|
| Dar es Salaam to Morogoro | TSh 22,000 | TSh 11,000 |
| Dar es Salaam to Dodoma | TSh 50,000 | TSh 25,000 |

Behind the table is a rate per kilometre. The stops-everywhere train is TSh 69.51 per km, including a small regulation fee, and the express is TSh 112.60 per km.

Here is a simple example. Two adults and one child aged 8 travel from Dar es Salaam to Dodoma on the train that stops everywhere. That is TSh 31,000 plus TSh 31,000 plus TSh 15,500, or TSh 77,500 one way. A return trip would be TSh 155,000.

### What this table does not cover

The order covers only the ordinary class. A TRC official spoke in July 2026 about business and royal class tickets, so higher classes exist. We could not find a LATRA table for them, so we do not give their prices. We also could not confirm whether the 2024 fares have changed since. Check the price shown when you book, and compare it with the LATRA order.

The order also says the operator must display station-to-station fares where passengers can see them, and must have a system for compensating passengers for lost luggage. If the price at the counter is higher than the order for your class, you can ask about it and report it.

## How to buy a ticket

TRC lists its SGR ticket site as sgrticket.trc.co.tz. LATRA's order requires TRC to use an electronic ticketing system connected to LATRA's own systems, so ticket sales are meant to be digital and recorded.

The ticket website itself loads as an application, and we could not read its steps or its list of payment methods. So we cannot tell you exactly which mobile money services it accepts or which ID details it asks for. Open the site before the day you travel and check these things:

1. Your departure and arrival stations.
2. The train type: stops everywhere or express.
3. The class and the price shown.
4. The passenger details it asks for, so your name matches the name you will give at the station.

Only buy from TRC's own website or station counters. Fake ticket pages and "agents" on social media are a common trap with anything people want in a hurry. Our post on [fake mobile money payment messages](/post/business-ideas/e-commerce/fake-mobile-money-payment-messages-sellers-tanzania/) shows how these tricks work. Never send money to a personal number for a ticket.

## Why trains look full

In July 2026 the Deputy Minister for Transport, David Kihenzile, told TRC to improve its ticketing system. Passengers had complained that the system said a train was full while some seats looked empty. TRC's communications officer, Athuman Idd, answered that many people buy tickets, mostly in business and royal class, and then do not travel because their plans change. He said the seats were paid for, so TRC lost no money.

For a traveller, this means two things. First, a sold-out message does not always mean every seat will be used, but you cannot count on a last-minute seat. Second, if your plans may change, ask TRC about its rules for changing a ticket before you pay. We did not find those rules in the sources we could open.

TRC also reacted to demand. On Friday 12 June 2026 it ran one extra electric train from Dodoma at 7 pm. In a statement on 25 June 2026 it said one extra Friday trip would leave Dodoma at 7.10 pm, and it advised passengers to buy early. From 1 September 2026 it planned to add more electric trips among Dar es Salaam, Morogoro and Dodoma, but the report did not give the number of trips or times. The timetable on TRC's website is a picture, so read it on the TRC page and confirm it when you book.

## Where the line goes next

The same reports say work continues on the western sections:

- Makutupora to Tabora, then Tabora to Isaka, and Isaka to Mwanza. Passenger trips to Mwanza are expected in 2027.
- Tabora to Kigoma. Operations are expected in 2028.

These are targets from August 2026 and they depend on finishing construction and testing. Dates for big projects often move, so do not plan a trip or a business around them yet.

Freight is part of the plan too. Electric freight trains have run between Dar es Salaam and Dodoma since June 2025. If you move goods, read our post on [the Dar es Salaam port and the SGR cargo link](/post/tanzania-news/dar-es-salaam-port-record-cargo-kwala-dry-port-sgr/).

## Common questions

### How much is the SGR from Dar es Salaam to Dodoma?

For the ordinary class, LATRA's 2024 order sets TSh 31,000 for an adult on the train that stops at every station and TSh 50,000 on the express. Children from 4 to 12 pay half. Business and royal class prices are not in that order.

### Do children need a ticket?

Children aged 4 to 12 pay the half fare. A child under 4 pays no fare, but the order says their details are still recorded in the passenger system.

### Where do I complain if I am overcharged?

LATRA regulates the fares. Our [post on Tanzania transport fares](/post/tanzania-news/tanzania-new-transport-fares/) lists LATRA's contact lines. You can also call TRC on 0800 11 00 42. Keep your ticket and a photo of the price you were shown.

### Can I take the train to Mwanza now?

Not yet. The reports we found say passenger service to Mwanza is expected in 2027, but construction of parts of the line is still in progress.

## What to do next

1. Open sgrticket.trc.co.tz a few days before you travel and look at the train type, class and price.
2. Compare the ordinary price with the LATRA table above.
3. If you travel on a Friday evening or on a holiday, buy as early as you can.
4. Keep your ticket and booking details until the trip is over.
