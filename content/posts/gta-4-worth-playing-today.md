Grand Theft Auto IV, often called GTA 4, is an older mchezo that people still talk about, and many younger players have never tried it. This guide explains what the game is, why it felt different in 2008, and whether it is worth your time and data today. It is a game for adults, and we keep the description plain.

All prices, stores and versions below were checked on 24 November 2026. They change, so always look at the official store page for your own country.

## Key points

- Grand Theft Auto IV is an open-world action game by Rockstar North and Rockstar Toronto, set in a fictional version of New York called Liberty City.
- Rockstar's page gives 29 April 2008 as the release date and lists PS3, Xbox 360, Xbox One and PC.
- On PC, the version sold today is the Complete Edition (24 March 2020). It includes the two story add-ons but has no multiplayer.
- It is rated M (Mature) by the ESRB and PEGI 18. It is not for children.
- Unofficial "free" copies are illegal and often carry malware. Buy it from an official store or skip it.

## What kind of game is it?

Grand Theft Auto IV is an open-world game. That means you move around a large city freely, drive cars, walk, talk to people and choose when to start a mission. You are not stuck on one level.

Rockstar's own description tells the story in a few lines. Niko Bellic arrives from Europe hoping to leave his past behind. His cousin Roman dreams of finding fortune in Liberty City. Instead they slide into debt and meet a string of dangerous people. The city, Rockstar says, is a place that worships money and status.

That is all you need to know about the plot. The story is the strongest part of the game, so it is better to discover it yourself.

## What made it special in 2008

Earlier games in the series, such as Vice City and San Andreas, were bright and a little silly. Grand Theft Auto IV took a different road. It is darker and slower, and it treats its main character like a real person with a heavy past. Many players call it the most serious game in the series. That is an opinion, but it is a common one.

Three things are worth knowing.

- **A city that feels lived in.** Liberty City is made to look like a real American city, with crowded streets, bridges, traffic and different districts. Compared with the earlier cartoon-like worlds, this was a big change in style.
- **Body movement with physics.** The Steam listing credits Euphoria, a technology from a company called NaturalMotion. In practice, characters stumble, fall and react to hits in a less scripted way than in older games.
- **Stories that connect.** The Complete Edition holds three stories set in the same city. Niko's is the main one. The other two follow Johnny Klebitz, a member of a biker gang, and Luis Lopez, who works for a nightclub owner. Steam describes them as three distinct stories woven together.

It also shaped what came later. The next main game, Grand Theft Auto V, kept the large, detailed city but returned to a lighter mood. If you want the full order of the series, read our [history of every GTA game](/post/video-games-updates/every-gta-game-in-order-history-of-grand-theft-auto/).

## What you can legally buy and play today

Rockstar's page for the game lists four platforms: PS3, Xbox 360, Xbox One and PC. Here is how that looks in practice.

| Platform | How to play it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PC (Windows) | Complete Edition on Steam or the Rockstar Games Launcher | Released 24 March 2020. No multiplayer. |
| Xbox One | Backward compatibility | Rockstar announced this in February 2017. |
| Xbox Series X and S | Backward compatibility | Rockstar's November 2020 post lists GTA IV among the older Xbox games that carry over. |
| PS3 and Xbox 360 | Old consoles and discs | Only if you already own the console. |

On PC, the Complete Edition is now the version in the stores. Rockstar says people who owned Grand Theft Auto IV or Episodes from Liberty City on Steam got the Complete Edition in their library. People with a boxed copy and the key from the manual can add it to the Rockstar Games Launcher.

As checked on 24 November 2026, the Steam page showed a price of $19.99 in US dollars. We found no price in Tanzanian shillings. Your price may differ by region, and sales are common, so check the store.

We did not find any official phone version on Rockstar's pages. A file that claims to be "GTA 4 for Android" is not from Rockstar. Treat it as unsafe.

### What the PC Complete Edition changed

This part matters if you have heard bad things about the PC game. The official notes say the Complete Edition has no Games for Windows Live, no multiplayer mode and no leaderboards. Three radio stations (RamJam FM, Self-Actualization FM and Vice City FM) were listed as temporarily unavailable when it launched.

So today you buy the story, not the online play. That suits most readers, because the story is the reason to play.

## Will it run on a modest PC?

The requirements on the Steam page are low by today's standards. As checked on 24 November 2026, the minimum is Windows 10 (64-bit), an Intel Core 2 Duo at 1.8 GHz or similar, 1.5 GB of RAM, a graphics card with 256 MB of memory and 22 GB of free space.

Those numbers are what the publisher lists. We did not test the game, so we cannot promise smooth play on an old computer. Two practical points help:

- 22 GB is a big download for many mobile data plans. Plan it for a night when you have a good connection or a cyber cafe that allows it.
- If the game feels slow, our guide on how to [speed up a slow Windows PC for games](/post/video-games-updates/speed-up-slow-windows-pc-for-games/) lists safe steps.

## How it plays and looks now

The look is dated. Faces, textures and the sky show their age next to a game from this year. The city still has a strong mood, especially at night and in the rain, but do not expect modern graphics.

The controls and pace are also from another time. The game is slower to start than many new titles. Missions often ask you to drive across the city, and some players find that tiring. Others enjoy it, because it makes the city feel real. Both views are fair.

It helps to play with a controller if you can. That is a tip, not a rule, and the PC version works with a keyboard and mouse.

## Honest pros and cons

**Pros**

- A strong, serious story with a memorable main character.
- A large, detailed city with a clear identity.
- Three stories in one package on PC and Xbox.
- A low price compared with new games (check the store).

**Cons**

- The graphics and controls feel old.
- The PC Complete Edition has no multiplayer.
- Many missions repeat the same pattern of drive, fight and return.
- It is a violent game for adults, with strong language and drug use.

## Age rating and what parents should know

Rockstar lists these content notes for the game: intense violence, blood, strong language, strong sexual content, partial nudity, and use of drugs and alcohol. The Steam page shows an ESRB rating of M (Mature) and PEGI 18.

That means it is meant for adults. Parents who are unsure what the labels mean can read our guide to [game age ratings and parental controls](/post/video-games-updates/game-age-ratings-esrb-pegi-and-parental-controls/).

## Who should play it today

- **You want a great story and do not mind old graphics:** yes, it is worth it, especially at a sale price.
- **You loved Vice City or San Andreas:** try it, but expect a darker mood.
- **You want online play with friends:** no. On PC the Complete Edition has no multiplayer. Look at Grand Theft Auto V instead (see [our GTA 5 guide](/post/video-games-updates/classic-games/gta-5-worth-playing-today/)).
- **You have a very slow PC or little data:** wait. A 22 GB game is a lot to commit to.
- **You want to see where the series started its 3D style:** read about [GTA 3 in our own guide](/post/video-games-updates/classic-games/gta-3-worth-playing-today/).

## Common questions

### Is there an official free version of GTA 4?

We found none on Rockstar's pages. The game is sold. Any site that offers it free is not official, and the files can hold malware or steal your accounts. Our post on [GTA 6 scams and fake downloads](/post/video-games-updates/gta-6-scams-fake-downloads-and-leaks-how-to-stay-safe/) shows the same warning signs.

### Do I need to play the older GTA games first?

No. The story of Grand Theft Auto IV stands on its own.

### Can I play GTA 4 on a PS5?

Rockstar's pages that we opened list PS3, Xbox 360, Xbox One and PC for this game. They do not list PS5. Check the PlayStation Store for your region.

### Is GTA 4 safe for a teenager?

It is rated for adults (ESRB M, PEGI 18). Decide as a family, and use console or Steam family settings if you allow it.
