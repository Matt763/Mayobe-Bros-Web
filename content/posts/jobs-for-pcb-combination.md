PCB means Physics, Chemistry and Biology as your three principal subjects in Form Five and Form Six. In Tanzania it is the main route into medicine, pharmacy, dentistry and many other health and science degrees. This guide is for Form Four leavers choosing a combination, Form Six students waiting for results, and parents helping with the decision.

We checked the entry rules below against the TCU admission guidebook for the 2026/27 cycle. Rules change every year, so treat this as a map and confirm the details before you apply.

## Key points

- PCB is the combination most health degrees ask for: medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and medical laboratory sciences all list Physics, Chemistry and Biology in the TCU guidebook.
- Many health degrees ask for at least 6 points from three principal passes. Some universities add minimum grades in each subject.
- PCB also opens science teaching, environmental health, food science, agriculture-related degrees and laboratory work.
- PCB usually does not qualify you for engineering, which normally needs Advanced Mathematics.
- A place is never guaranteed. Entry is competitive, and pay and job openings differ a lot from one career to another.

## What the TCU guidebook says about points

The Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) publishes a guidebook every year for Form Six leavers. It lists each degree, the subjects needed, the minimum points, the number of places and the length of study.

Points are counted from your principal passes. For results from 2016 onward, the guidebook uses A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and S = 0.5. For most degrees the general minimum is 4.0 points from two subjects that relate to the programme. Health degrees are stricter.

Here is a hypothetical example. A student gets C in Chemistry, C in Biology and D in Physics. That is 3 + 3 + 2 = 8 points. Another gets D, D and D, which is 6 points. Both meet a "minimum of 6 points" rule on paper, but the first student is far safer, because universities usually receive more applicants than places.

## Health degrees that ask for PCB

The table summarises what the 2026/27 guidebook lists for common health programmes. Always open the guidebook for the exact wording of your chosen university.

| Degree | Subjects asked for | Minimum points |
|---|---|---|
| Doctor of Medicine (MD/MBBS) | Principal passes in Physics, Chemistry, Biology | 6 |
| Doctor of Dental Surgery | Physics, Chemistry, Biology | 6 |
| Bachelor of Pharmacy | Physics, Chemistry, Biology | 6 |
| Medical Laboratory Sciences | Physics, Chemistry, Biology | 6 |
| Nursing (BSc) | Chemistry, Biology and one of Physics, Advanced Mathematics or Nutrition | 6 |
| Physiotherapy and Optometry | Physics and Biology with Chemistry or Advanced Mathematics (check each) | 6 |
| Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics | Chemistry, Biology and one of Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Nutrition, Geography or Agriculture | 6 |

Some universities set stricter rules than this harmonised table. One private university, for example, asks nursing applicants for at least a C in Chemistry and Biology and a D in Physics. Another asks for a D in every PCB subject for its medicine degree.

### How long do these degrees take?

It depends on the university. In the 2026/27 guidebook, medicine and dentistry are listed at 5 years, pharmacy and nursing at 4 years, and medical laboratory sciences at 3 or 4 years depending on the institution. After the degree, many health professions require supervised practice and registration with a professional council. Ask the university or the council what applies to your profession.

## Careers beyond the hospital

PCB is not only for people who want to be doctors. Other degrees in the guidebook accept PCB subjects, though the exact subjects differ by university.

- **Science teaching.** Bachelor of Science with Education programmes ask for two principal passes from subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Advanced Mathematics, Computer Science or Geography. Listed durations are 3 years. Check current government pay scales before you decide.
- **Food science and technology.** One university asks for Chemistry plus one of Biology, Physics, Nutrition, Agriculture or Advanced Mathematics. This work can lead to jobs in food processing, quality control and safety.
- **Environmental health.** The harmonised table lists Chemistry and Biology with Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Nutrition, Geography or Agriculture, with a minimum of 6 points.
- **Veterinary medicine.** One university (Sokoine) lists two principal passes in Chemistry and Biology plus a subsidiary pass, with 4.5 institutional points and 5 years of study.
- **Biotechnology and laboratory sciences.** The same university lists Chemistry and Biology principal passes with a subsidiary in another science.
- **Research and public health.** These usually start with a science degree, then a master's. They take longer, and you will need to plan for fees.

## What each PCB subject does for you

Each subject carries real weight in the guidebook, not just Biology.

- **Physics** is named in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and medical laboratory sciences. It is also the subject many PCB students find hardest, so give it steady practice from Form Five.
- **Chemistry** appears in almost every health and food-science entry we checked. Drug action, laboratory tests and nutrition all build on it.
- **Biology** is the base for nursing, veterinary work, environmental health and biotechnology. The body, disease, genetics and ecology all come from it.

A practical point: because universities add minimum grades per subject, one weak subject can cost you a place even when your total points look fine. Plan to lift your weakest subject early, not in the last month before NECTA examinations.

## Where PCB does not fit

Engineering is the main example. The civil engineering listings we checked ask for Advanced Mathematics, usually with Physics. A PCB student without Advanced Mathematics would not meet these. If engineering interests you, think about PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) instead, before you choose your combination.

Law, accounting and business degrees are more open than people expect. In the guidebook, several law and accounting programmes accept two principal passes from a long list of subjects, including Physics, Chemistry and Biology, though they may also ask for a subsidiary pass in English or Mathematics. PCB does not block these paths, but it is not the combination built for them.

## What if your points are not enough for medicine?

Many applicants miss their first choice. You have options.

1. **Apply to a related programme.** Pharmacy, medical laboratory sciences, nursing, physiotherapy and environmental health also use PCB subjects. Read each university's own grade rules.
2. **Look at the diploma route.** Technical colleges offer diplomas in clinical medicine, pharmacy, nursing and laboratory sciences. These are regulated by NACTVET, not TCU. Check that the college and programme are accredited before you pay anything.
3. **Check the equivalent-entry route.** The TCU has a separate guidebook for diploma holders who want to join a degree later.
4. **Apply in the next cycle.** Some students retake examinations privately. This costs time and money, so ask your teachers whether it makes sense for your results.

## Money, pay and jobs: what we can and cannot say

We do not list salary ranges or job numbers. We could not confirm them with official Tanzanian sources, and pay varies a lot between government, private and mission employers.

What is safe to say: government employers publish pay scales, and vacancies appear on the official government job portal. Private employers set their own pay. Before you choose a career, ask people working in it, and look at several real job adverts, not social media claims.

For tuition, many students apply to HESLB for loans. Our guide on [how the HESLB student loan works](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/) explains the process.

## A simple plan for Form Five and Form Six

Starting early helps more than any trick.

- **Form Five, first term:** choose two or three target programmes and read their entry lines in the guidebook.
- **Form Five, later terms:** track your marks in each PCB subject. If one is far behind, ask for extra practice or a study group.
- **Form Six, before NECTA:** do past papers under timed conditions, including practical work.
- **After results:** check the TCU dates for applications in the current almanac, and apply only through the university websites. Nobody can sell you a place, and anyone who says they can is a risk to your money.

## Choosing your combination well

If you are still in Form Four, these steps help.

1. Pick three or four careers you could imagine, not just one.
2. Open the TCU guidebook and search for each degree. Write down the subjects and points.
3. Compare your results in Physics, Chemistry and Biology so far. PCB needs all three, and a weak Physics grade can end your medicine plan.
4. Talk to a student or worker in the field. Ask what the work is really like.
5. Read our short guide on [how combinations and careers connect](/post/educational/combination-na-aina-ya-kazi-utakayofanya/).

Good study habits matter more than the label on your combination. Our post on [10 study techniques for students](/post/educational/study-tips/10-proven-techniques-to-study-effectively-for-students/) has practical tips for revision.

## Common questions

### Can a PCB student study engineering?

Usually not. Engineering degrees in the guidebook normally ask for Advanced Mathematics, often with Physics. Check the exact rule for each programme.

### Is PCB only for future doctors?

No. It also leads to pharmacy, nursing, laboratory work, nutrition, environmental health, teaching and food science. Some of these need less competitive points than medicine.

### Do I need 6 points to study nursing?

The harmonised table lists three principal passes in Chemistry, Biology and one other listed subject with at least 6 points. Individual universities can set higher grade rules, so read their entries.

### Can PCB graduates work abroad?

Possibly, but each country has its own licensing rules for health workers. We did not verify any specific country's rules, so check with the professional council before you plan around this.

### Which is better, PCB or PCM?

Neither is better. PCB suits health and life sciences. PCM suits engineering and physical sciences. Choose by the careers you want and the subjects you do well in.

## What to do next

1. Download the latest TCU guidebook for Form Six holders and search for three programmes you like.
2. Write down the exact subjects, points and years of study for each, plus the universities offering them.
3. Ask your school teacher or a university student which of those programmes suits your current grades.
4. Keep a backup plan, such as a related degree or an accredited diploma.
