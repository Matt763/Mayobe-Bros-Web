If the loan results did not go your way, you may be feeling worried, maybe even ashamed in front of your family. Many plans depend on this money. You are not alone, and a "no" or a small amount is not always the end of the road. This post is only about results and appeals. For how to apply and repay, read our [HESLB loan guide](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/).

## The short answer

HESLB (the Higher Education Students' Loans Board) tells successful applicants through the Student's Individual Permanent Account, called SIPA. If you are not happy with your allocation, an appeal can be possible during a special appeal window.

- Check your result in your SIPA account, not through a stranger or a message that asks for money.
- The 2025/26 guideline said the appeal is made through the Online Appeal Form in SIPA, and there is no appeal fee.
- Appeal windows are set for each year. Read the current HESLB guideline for the dates that apply to you.
- Both first-year applicants and continuing borrowers who are not satisfied could appeal in 2025/26.
- Nobody can sell you a loan. Only HESLB allocates loans.

## How do I see my allocation?

The HESLB guideline for 2025/26 says successful applicants are told of their allocations through SIPA. It also advises applicants to follow their results through their SIPA accounts.

So the first step is simple. Log in and look. HESLB publishes news too. As checked in November 2026, the HESLB news page showed public notices about 2026/27 allocations, dated 10 October 2026 and 21 October 2026. The second notice said 90,235 students were given loans and grants in the second phase. We mention this only to show that allocation did not come in one single day.

If you cannot log in, do not ask a friend to "fix it" with your password. Use the official HESLB channels listed below.

## Why does allocation come in batches?

The HESLB news page shows notices for a first round and a second round of allocation in October 2026. From this we can say that results can arrive in more than one batch. We could not find an official page that explains why, so we will not guess at reasons.

What that means for you: if your name is not on the first notice, check again after the next notice. Do not decide too early that you got nothing.

## How does the appeal work?

Here is what the HESLB guideline for 2025/26 (issued June 2025) says. We show it as a dated example. It is not the rule for your year until you read the current guideline.

- A specific appeal window is opened through the online application system, OLAMS.
- Fresh applicants and continuing borrowers who are not satisfied with their allocation are eligible.
- You submit the appeal through the Online Appeal Form in your SIPA account.
- The appeal does not cost anything. The guideline says "No Appeal Fee".
- The 2025/26 window was 1 to 15 November 2025.

For the 2026/27 year we could not confirm the appeal dates. The HESLB guideline files for 2026/27 are on the HESLB website, but we could not read the appeal part, and the news page we checked in November 2026 did not describe an appeal window. So please do not use the 2025/26 dates for your own plan. Look in OLAMS and in the current guideline, or call HESLB to ask.

One more careful point. We did not find any official list of reasons that win an appeal, and we will not invent one. What the form asks for is shown in the form itself.

## What should I check before I appeal?

A few minutes of checking can save you a lot of stress.

- Your details. Is your name, index number and programme correct in OLAMS and SIPA?
- Your application. Did you finish it, pay the fee, and upload what the guideline asked for? The 2025/26 guideline lists a guarantor with an ID such as a National ID number, a voter card or a driving licence, and says any forgery disqualifies an applicant for that year.
- Your documents. Keep copies of what you uploaded and your results notice.
- Your story. If your family situation has a real reason, such as a parent's loss of income, be ready to say it truthfully and show papers if the form asks for them.
- Your deadline. Do it early in the window, not on the last day.

Never submit false papers. The guideline says forgery can end an application for the whole year.

## How to write your reason clearly

Whatever the form asks, a short and true explanation helps whoever reads it. Say what you applied for, what you received, and why it is not enough. Use plain facts, such as your fees and the real cost of your course, and keep the tone calm.

Say you were given an amount that covers only part of your costs. Write what the shortfall is, and what documents show it. This is an example of how to think, not a form we have seen. Follow the wording and the limits of the real form in SIPA.

Do not write a long story, and do not exaggerate. If the form has a place for papers, upload clear scans, not dark photos.

## What if the appeal does not change the result?

This can happen, and it hurts. Take a day. Then look at the other doors. Ask your university about payment plans or a student support office. Look for scholarships and sponsors that you can check are real. Ask HESLB whether you can apply again in a later round or year. Plans can be built in stages.

## Beware of people who promise loans for money

Every year, some people say "pay me and I will get your loan approved". Please do not believe them. HESLB's own guideline says the appeal costs nothing, and loans are given by the Board through its system. If someone wants cash or your password, stop and report it to HESLB.

## Common questions

### Is there a fee to appeal?

The 2025/26 guideline says there is no appeal fee. Check the current guideline for your year.

### Where do I appeal?

In the guideline we read, the appeal window was opened through OLAMS, and the form was inside the applicant's SIPA account.

### When is the 2026/27 appeal window?

We could not confirm it. Check OLAMS and the current HESLB guideline, or ask the call centre.

### Who can I ask for help?

The 2025/26 guideline gave the HESLB Call Centre as 0736 66 55 33 and WhatsApp as 0739 66 55 33 during working hours. The HESLB home page, as checked in November 2026, also gave info@heslb.go.tz. Check the HESLB site for the latest contacts.

### Can I still go to university if I get no loan?

Some students find other ways, such as family support, sponsors or scholarships, while others wait for a later round. Talk to your university's student office. Our post on [what to do if you are not selected](/post/educational/study-tips/not-selected-for-university-what-to-do-next/) may also help you think through your options.

## What to do next

1. Log in to SIPA today and read your result carefully.
2. Open the current HESLB guideline for your programme level and read the appeal section.
3. Prepare copies of your documents and write down your reason in plain, true words.
4. Contact HESLB through its official channels if anything is unclear.

A loan result is one answer on one day. Keep going step by step, and ask for help early.
