Good revision starts with the right book and the right list of topics. The Tanzania Institute of Education (TIE) is the body that sets the school curriculum, and it has an online library and public syllabus files. This post shows you where to find them and how to use them well.

## The short answer

TIE has an online library at ol.tie.go.tz. You sign in with a Google account, then read books online. The official syllabus for each subject is a separate PDF on the TIE website. Use the syllabus as your checklist and the library book as your reading.

- Go to ol.tie.go.tz and choose "Continue with Google".
- You need a Google (Gmail) account.
- The TIE sign-in guide says you can read books online. It does not say whether you can download them.
- Syllabi for Forms I to IV and Forms V to VI are on the TIE website as PDFs.
- Always start from tie.go.tz, not from a blog that says "free TIE books PDF".

As checked in November 2026, the library address loaded and showed the title "TIE Online Library". We could not see inside the library from our check, so we cannot tell you which subjects or books are there. Please look at what your account shows.

## What is the TIE online library?

TIE is Tanzania's official curriculum body. Its website has a how-to page, written in Kiswahili, called "Maelezo ya jinsi ya kuingia kwenye Maktaba Mtandao", which means "How to sign in to the online library". The library is called Maktaba Mtandao in Kiswahili.

The idea is simple. Instead of searching the internet for a book, you read the official one on the official site. That helps you in three ways. You know the book matches what the curriculum body publishes. You avoid files with missing pages or wrong content. And you avoid sites that can harm your phone.

## How do I sign in, step by step?

These are the steps from the TIE guide, which we read in November 2026.

1. Open your browser and type ol.tie.go.tz in the address bar.
2. When the page loads, tap "Continue with Google".
3. Enter your Gmail address and password.
4. After you sign in, tap "Continue with Google" again if the page asks.
5. You will arrive at the library's main page, where the guide says you can read books online.

A few practical points follow.

- **You need a Google account.** If you do not have one, a Gmail account is free to create. Keep your password private, and never share it, even with a friend who says they will "help you log in".
- **Fees.** The sign-in guide and page did not mention any fee. We cannot promise the whole library is free beyond that. If any page asks you for money to enter, stop and ask your school or TIE.
- **Downloads.** The guide only says you can read online. Do not assume you can save books to your phone.
- **If the page does not open.** Websites sometimes go down. Try again later in the day, or ask your teacher whether the school has another way to reach the books.

Reading online uses data. Our post on [saving mobile data when studying online](/post/educational/online-learning/save-mobile-data-when-studying-online/) has settings that can help.

## Where can I find the official syllabus for my subject?

A syllabus is the official list of topics you must learn in a subject. It tells you what the curriculum covers. TIE publishes syllabi on its website as PDF files.

There are two pages we opened:

- **Forms I to IV (lower secondary).** The page is called "Syllabus for lower secondary academics". It lists PDF files by subject. When we checked, most were dated 2023, and one Kiswahili syllabus was dated March 2025.
- **Forms V to VI (upper secondary).** The page is called "Syllabus for upper secondary education". It lists PDFs by subject, and one entry we saw was dated April 2025.

Each page has several pages of results, so scroll to the bottom and use the page numbers. Tap the title of your subject to open the PDF. Syllabus files can be large, so use Wi-Fi if you can. Check the year on the file, since TIE may publish a newer version.

A small warning about the TIE website itself: when we opened these pages through our tool, the footer text included an unrelated link that looked like spam. Do not tap links that have nothing to do with education. Stay on the syllabus list.

## How do I use the book and the syllabus together?

This is where the work pays off. Here is a simple routine.

1. Open the syllabus for your subject and find the list of topics for your form.
2. Copy the topics into your notebook as a checklist, one line per topic.
3. Open the library and look for a book for your subject and level.
4. Read one topic at a time. After each, write three lines in your own words.
5. Answer past questions or exercises on that topic, if you have them from your teacher.
6. Tick the topic off the checklist. If something is not clear, mark it and ask your teacher.

Say you study Geography in Form Five and your syllabus has twelve topics. If you read one topic every two evenings, you would finish the list in twenty-four evenings, about three and a half weeks. That is only an example. Your own pace may differ, and your teacher may set a different order.

## Why are unofficial PDF sites risky?

Search "free TIE books PDF" and you will find many pages. We do not link to them, and here is why.

- The file may be old, incomplete or changed, and you cannot tell.
- Some sites copy books without permission, so using them may break the owner's rights.
- Some pages carry pop-ups or ask you to install an app. That can put your phone at risk.
- Some ask for your phone number or for a payment.

The safer habit is to begin with tie.go.tz and ol.tie.go.tz, and to ask your teacher if the school has the official books.

## Common questions

### Do I need to pay to use the TIE online library?

The official sign-in guide and page we read did not mention a fee. We cannot say more than that. If you are asked to pay, check with your school or TIE.

### Can I download the books?

We could not confirm this. The guide only says you can read the books online. Check what the library shows once you are signed in.

### Can I sign in without a Gmail account?

The guide describes only the "Continue with Google" route. We found no other method on the page.

### Which syllabus should I follow, the old one or the new one?

Follow the one for your form and year, as your teacher says. Look at the date on the PDF, and ask your teacher if two versions exist.

## What to do next

1. Make sure you have a Google account and a safe password.
2. Open ol.tie.go.tz and sign in using the steps above.
3. Download the syllabus for your subject and make a topic checklist.
4. Read one topic a day, and tick it off.

Small, steady reading from official sources builds more than hours of scrolling. You can do this.
