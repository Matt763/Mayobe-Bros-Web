FIFA 17 is one of the football games many of us remember, and people still search for it every month. It is also a game that has changed a lot since 2016. Its online servers are closed, it is no longer sold in the main stores, and newer football games have taken its place. This article explains what the game was, what made it different, and whether it is worth your time in 2026.

Everything below was checked on official pages as of 24 November 2026. Stores and services change, so check the page for your console or PC before you decide anything.

## Key points

- FIFA 17 was released worldwide on 29 September 2016 (27 September in North America) by EA.
- It was the first FIFA game to run on the Frostbite engine, and it added a story mode called The Journey.
- EA closed the online services for FIFA 17 on 14 February 2023. Ultimate Team and other online modes no longer work.
- EA's own notice says a game can still be played offline after its online services end. Kick Off and Career Mode are reported to work.
- It is not worth chasing for most players today. A newer game gives you current teams and online play.
- Never use a "cracked" or unofficial copy. It is illegal, and it is a common way to catch a virus.

## What FIFA 17 was

FIFA 17 is a football simulation game made by EA Vancouver and EA Romania. EA released it on PC, Xbox One, PlayStation 4, Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3. The worldwide date was 29 September 2016.

The cover athlete was Marco Reus of Borussia Dortmund. EA says fans chose him in an online vote with more than 3.1 million votes. The other choices were James Rodriguez, Anthony Martial and Eden Hazard.

In the ESRB system in the United States, the game is rated E for Everyone. The PlayStation Store page for FIFA 17 shows PEGI 3. To learn how these labels work, read our guide to [game age ratings](/post/video-games-updates/game-age-ratings-esrb-pegi-and-parental-controls/).

## What was new in 2016

### The Frostbite engine

A game engine is the software that draws the players, the pitch and the lights. EA's press release said that, "for the first time", FIFA 17 was powered by Frostbite. EA also said Frostbite was only on the Xbox One, PlayStation 4 and PC versions. The older Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3 versions did not get it.

### The Journey

The Journey was a new story mode. You follow Alex Hunter, a young player trying to make it in the Premier League. EA said your choices on and off the pitch, and how you play, shape the story. You could pick any of the 20 Premier League clubs.

Like Frostbite, The Journey was only on Xbox One, PlayStation 4 and PC. For many players it was the reason to buy the game that year.

### Ultimate Team and new ways to compete

Ultimate Team (often called FUT) lets you build a squad from player cards. FIFA 17 added Squad Building Challenges, where you complete themed squads to earn rewards. It also added FUT Champions, a ranked online mode.

These modes are the part that needed the internet. They are also the part that has now closed.

### Teams and leagues

EA's Spanish-language page for the game listed more than 30 leagues and more than 650 teams. The Premier League, Bundesliga, LaLiga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and MLS were all listed. EA also noted that Brazilian club players would have generic names and would not be in Ultimate Team. We could not find a list of African leagues on that page. African teams appear mostly as national sides, with a few clubs such as Kaizer Chiefs and Orlando Pirates under "Rest of World".

## What happened to FIFA 17 after 2016

### The online servers closed

EA keeps a public list of closed online services. It shows that FIFA 17 online services for PC, Xbox 360, Xbox One, PlayStation 3 and PlayStation 4 ended on 14 February 2023. FUT Champions had ended much earlier, on 4 September 2017. EA also removed FIFA 17 from its EA Play subscription list on 6 May 2021.

EA says it retires online services when too few people still play a game. It also says that, in those cases, "the game may still be played and enjoyed offline."

### It left the stores

In September 2023, news sites reported that EA had removed its FIFA games from FIFA 14 to FIFA 23 from stores such as Steam, the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Nintendo eShop and the Epic Games Store. The reports said EA had not given a reason. People who had already bought a digital copy could still install it again.

Some store pages still exist. The Xbox Store page shows FIFA 17 with a note that a newer release exists in the franchise, and no price. The PlayStation Store page for Türkiye also still shows the game. We could not confirm whether either one sells it today. If a store does not let you buy it, do not look for another source (see the warning below).

## What still works offline

A report from Realsport101 says that after the shutdown, Ultimate Team, Online Seasons and Pro Clubs stopped working, while offline modes such as Kick Off and Career Mode still work. We could not find an official EA page that lists which modes work offline. We also could not confirm whether The Journey still works, because EA does not say.

Two more things to know:

- The squads are from 2016 and 2017. Players have moved, retired or grown old.
- On PC, FIFA 17 needs a lot of space. EA lists 50 GB of free disk space and 8 GB of RAM as the minimum.

### PC requirements from EA

EA's PC page lists these minimums: Windows 7, 8.1 or 10 (64-bit), an Intel Core i3-2100 or AMD Phenom II X4 965 processor, 8 GB of RAM, an NVIDIA GTX 460 or AMD Radeon R7 260 graphics card, DirectX 11 and 50 GB of disk space. The recommended list asks for an Intel i5-3550K or AMD FX 8150, and a GTX 660 or Radeon R9 270. We read the Spanish-language version of EA's page, so check the English version if you need to be sure.

## Warning: no cracks and no unofficial copies

Because FIFA 17 is hard to buy new, some websites and videos offer a free "full version" or a "crack". These are illegal copies. They also carry real risks: viruses, stolen passwords and fake files that never run. The safe options are simple. Use a copy you already own, a second-hand disc from a shop you trust, or a game that is on sale in an official store today.

## Is it worth playing today?

It depends on who you are.

| Player type | Our view |
|---|---|
| You own a disc or a digital copy already | Yes, for offline matches and nostalgia. Do not expect online play. |
| You want to play with friends online | No. The online modes are closed. |
| You want current teams and players | No. Pick a current game. |
| You have a weak PC or phone | FIFA 17 needs 50 GB on PC. Free mobile games may suit you better. |
| You like football history | Maybe. It is a clear picture of how the series looked in 2016. |

## Common questions

### Can I still play FIFA 17 online?

No. EA's list shows the online services ended on 14 February 2023.

### Is FIFA 17 still on sale?

Reports from September 2023 say EA removed FIFA 14 to FIFA 23 from the main stores. Some store pages still show the game, but we could not confirm that you can buy it. Check your own store.

### Does The Journey work offline?

We could not confirm it. EA says offline play may still work after online services close, but it does not list modes. Do not buy a copy only for The Journey.

### Is FIFA 17 safe for children?

The ESRB rating is E for Everyone and PEGI 3 is shown on the PlayStation Store. Online chat and purchases were part of the original online modes, and those are now closed.

## What to do next

1. Check if you already own FIFA 17 on a disc, or in your store library.
2. If you do not, skip it and try a current game. Start with the eFootball and EA Sports FC comparison linked above.
3. Set a spending limit before you try any game with packs or in-game purchases.

Football games are fun when you pick one that fits your device and your wallet. FIFA 17 had its season, and there are good ways to enjoy the sport today.
