Waiting for a government job can feel heavy, especially when you have done everything right and still hear nothing. A clean application is the part you control. This post shows how applying through the Ajira Portal works, what to prepare first and which small mistakes cost people their chance.

## The short answer

For jobs advertised by the Public Service Recruitment Secretariat (PSRS), you apply online through the Ajira recruitment portal. Prepare your certified documents before you start, create your account with an email address you can open, fill your profile honestly, and send the application for the post you want.

- Apply only through the official portal. The advert we read says applications sent by any other means will not be considered.
- Read the advert for the post. Each advert has its own conditions, qualifications and deadline.
- Scan certified copies of your certificates. Do not scan Form IV or Form VI result slips if the advert says they are not accepted.
- Name three referees with contacts that work.
- Never pay anyone to apply for you or to "push" your application.

## What the Public Service Recruitment Secretariat does

The PSRS is part of the President's Office. When public institutions need staff, the PSRS publishes a vacancy announcement on their behalf. The announcement we read in November 2026 was written for the Tanzania Electric Supply Company (TANESCO), and it said the PSRS was inviting applications on behalf of that company.

This matters because the advert is your rulebook. It lists the duties of each post, the qualifications and the general conditions for all applicants. You apply on the strength of what the advert says.

The government main portal also lists other official systems for jobs. Besides the PSRS portal, it names a portal for health cadre jobs run through the Ministry of Health and another for teaching and health jobs run through TAMISEMI. Check which one your advert points to. This post is about the PSRS portal, but the habits in it help on all of them.

We could not open the portal's own applicant guide when we checked in November 2026. The portal pages did not show their content to our tools. So we do not describe each screen here. Screens and buttons can change, and the portal's own guide is the best source for the exact layout.

## Documents to prepare before you open the portal

Do this part first. Hunting for a document while the page is open is how people make mistakes.

The general conditions in the October 2026 advert asked applicants to attach certified copies of these items:

- Degree, diploma or certificate (whatever the post asks for)
- Transcripts for degrees and diplomas
- Professional registration and training certificates, where the post needs them
- Birth certificate

The same advert said it would not accept Form IV and Form VI result slips, testimonials or partial transcripts. Other conditions in that advert included:

- A recent passport-size photo uploaded to the portal
- Three reputable referees with contacts that work
- Foreign certificates recognised by the right authorities (TCU, NECTA and NACTVET were named)
- A signed application letter in Swahili or English

These are the conditions of one advert. Another advert may add or change rules, for example on age limits. Always read the conditions section of the advert you are answering, then work from that list.

The Ministry of Health portal guide gives a similar list for health jobs. It also asks for a CV, a passport photo, a NIDA number if you have one, and an application letter. If your names differ across documents, it asks for proof of a change of name. This is guidance for that portal only, but it shows a good habit: make sure your name is spelled the same on every document.

Put everything in one folder on your phone or computer. Name each file clearly, for example `degree-certificate.pdf`. Check each scan is readable before you upload it.

## How to create your account and fill your profile

Here is what the Ministry of Health guide describes for its own portal. We read it in November 2026. It is not a guide to the PSRS portal, so expect differences.

1. Use a valid email address that you can open. The account name is your email and you choose a password.
2. Choose the right category when asked. The guide warns that a wrong choice can stop you from going further.
3. Fill in personal details such as your names, date of birth, phone number and email.
4. Add your academic qualifications, then professional qualifications if you have them.
5. Add working experience and language skills.
6. Attach your documents and send the application.

Take your time on the profile. Many portals keep your profile for later applications, so careful work now saves time in the future. Write your names exactly as they appear on your certificates.

Keep your password somewhere safe, and do not share it. If a stranger offers to "create your account," you are giving them access to your personal documents, your NIDA details and your future applications.

## How to apply for one vacancy

Once your profile is ready, the steps are simple in idea, even if the screens differ:

1. Find the advert for the post you want on the official PSRS page or the portal. Read the duties, the qualifications and the general conditions.
2. Check honestly that you meet the qualifications. If the advert asks for a certain level of study or registration and you do not have it, wait for a post that fits.
3. Prepare your signed application letter. Our post on [how to write an application letter](/post/jobs-and-internships/application-letter-for-a-job-tanzania-how-to-write/) shows a simple layout.
4. Write a CV that matches the advert. See [how to write a CV for your first job](/post/jobs-and-internships/how-to-write-a-cv-for-your-first-job-tanzania/).
5. Choose the post in the portal, attach the right documents and review everything.
6. Send the application before the deadline in the advert. Do not wait for the last day, so a slow connection does not cost you the chance.

The October 2026 advert said the letter should be addressed to the Secretary of the PSRS at an address in Dodoma. Because adverts can change, copy the address from the advert you are using and not from this page.

After you send it, keep a copy of what you submitted. The advert we read said only shortlisted candidates are told the date of an interview. So no news does not always mean you were rejected, but it does mean you should keep checking the official page.

## Mistakes that get applications rejected

- **Applying by another route.** The advert said anything sent outside the portal will not be considered. Emailing or delivering a bundle of papers is not enough.
- **Uncertified copies.** The advert asked for certified copies. Ask the right office to certify your copies early, because it can take a few days.
- **Sending slips the advert refuses.** Form IV and Form VI result slips and testimonials were listed as not accepted in that advert.
- **Weak referees.** Choose three people who know your work, tell them first, and check their phone numbers and emails.
- **Wrong or mixed names.** If your birth certificate and your degree show different names, find out how to prove they are the same person before you apply.
- **False information.** The advert warned that forged certificates and false information lead to legal action. Never take that risk. A real, modest record is better than a fake one.
- **Paying a "helper".** The portal route does not need a middleman. Read our post on [fake job offers](/post/jobs-and-internships/fake-job-offers-tanzania-warning-signs-never-pay/) before you pay anyone anything.

## Common questions

### Can I apply for a government job by email or by hand?

For PSRS adverts, the one we read said all applications must be sent through the recruitment portal and anything else will not be considered. Check each advert.

### Do I need to pay to use the Ajira Portal?

The advert did not mention any fee for applying. Be careful with anyone who asks for money for jobs, shortlisting or interviews. If you are unsure, ask the official office named in the advert.

### Can I use one account for several vacancies?

Portals normally keep your profile for later use, but we could not confirm how the PSRS portal handles this. Check the portal's guide.

### What if I am already working in the public service?

The October 2026 advert said public servants should follow a government circular and not apply directly, and that retired public servants should not apply. Other adverts may differ, so read the conditions.

## What to do next

1. Gather your certificates and ask for certified copies now, before any advert comes.
2. Choose three referees, ask each one, and write down their contacts.
3. Make a clear CV and a draft application letter that you can adjust for each post.
4. When a post fits you, open the official advert, read every condition and apply through the portal well before the deadline.

Applying well is a skill, and you get better each time. Keep going, and be proud that you are doing it honestly.
