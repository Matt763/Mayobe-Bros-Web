The University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM) takes its applications through its own online system. This guide shows how a first-degree application works, how it differs for postgraduate and short courses, and which mistakes cost applicants a place. Everything time-sensitive is as checked on 24 November 2026. UDSM and the Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) can change dates and rules, so use their pages for the final word.

## Key points

- You apply directly to UDSM through its Online Admission System at `admission.udsm.ac.tz`. TCU does not take bachelor's applications for you.
- The UDSM undergraduate booklet for 2026/2027 says the non-refundable application fee is TZS 10,000 for Tanzanians. Check the booklet for the current figure before you pay.
- You choose at least 3 and at most 15 programmes.
- If you are admitted by more than one university, you must confirm only one.
- Postgraduate and short courses have separate pages and dates.

## Is it TCU or UDSM that takes my application?

Both are involved, in different ways. TCU publishes the yearly admission guidebooks and the national calendar. The 2026/2027 guidebook says applications for bachelor's degrees are submitted directly through the online systems of the institutions. TCU's FAQ says the same: you apply to your preferred programme and institution.

So for UDSM, you open a UDSM account. TCU then coordinates the confirmation of students who are selected by more than one university. Our post on [the TCU admission guidebook](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-read-tcu-admission-guidebook/) explains the two guidebooks, and our post on [confirming admission when several universities select you](/post/educational/results/how-to-confirm-university-admission-tcu-multiple-selection/) covers the one-place rule.

Older web pages talk about a "Central Admission System". The 2026/2027 guidebook describes applications as going to each institution, so follow the current guidebook and the UDSM page, not old forum posts.

## What are the entry requirements?

The UDSM undergraduate booklet 2026/2027 lists two parts.

**General minimum rules for direct applicants (Form Six):**

- A CSEE or equivalent with passes in five approved subjects, three of them at credit level.
- ACSEE with two principal passes and a total of at least 4.0 points in the two subjects that define the programme.
- For arts programmes, the booklet says principal passes in appropriate subjects with at least 5 points from three subjects.

**Equivalent applicants (diploma holders):** an appropriate diploma with a GPA of at least 3.5, or equivalent grades, from a college registered by NACTVET and approved by the UDSM Senate.

On top of this, each programme has its own subjects. The booklet has a table with the programme code, length, acceptable combinations and entry subjects. For example, it lists a statistics degree that needs a principal pass in Advanced Mathematics. Read the row for your own programme.

The points per grade for most applicants are A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1, S = 0.5. Our guide on [reading TCU entry requirements](/post/educational/career-development/how-to-read-tcu-programme-entry-requirements/) shows how to turn your grades into points.

## How do I apply for an undergraduate programme?

The booklet gives these steps for the Online Admission System (UDSM-OLAS).

1. **Open the portal.** Go to `admission.udsm.ac.tz` and use the Undergraduate tab. Read the instructions first.
2. **Register an account.** You give your first name, surname (as on your Form Four certificate), email, mobile number and a password. You also type the captcha. The system sends an email, and you click the link to activate the account. Your email address is your username.
3. **Start "My Application".** It has four steps.
4. **Step 1, profile.** Choose the application category and write your names exactly as in your secondary certificates.
5. **Step 2, application fee.** Generate a control number in the system. The booklet gives the fee for Tanzanians as TZS 10,000, non-refundable, payable by mobile money using the "Malipo ya Serikali" option and your reference number. After payment, the system updates and lets you continue.
6. **Step 3, academic qualifications.** Add your O-Level and A-Level index numbers and the year. If you have several sittings, you can add each one. Diploma holders add the Award Verification Number (AVN) from NACTVET and upload their diploma certificates as PDF. Tanzanians with foreign certificates first get equivalence numbers from NECTA.
7. **Step 4, programme choices.** Pick between 3 and 15 programmes.

Afterwards, the university tells you the result by email and through your account. The booklet says it is your responsibility to confirm that you accept. If you do not confirm, you are treated as not accepting the offer, and the place goes to others.

## What is the timeline each year?

The TCU almanac for the 2026/2027 cycle shows the pattern:

| Activity | Date in 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Guidebooks published | 4 July |
| First application window | 10 July to 10 August |
| First-round admissions announced | 8 September |
| Second application window and confirmation of first-round offers | 8 to 21 September |
| Second-round admissions announced | 6 October |
| Confirmation of second-round offers | 6 to 19 October |
| Earliest date universities open | 19 October |

As checked on 24 November 2026, the UDSM admission page says undergraduate applications for 2026/2027 are closed, that results for all rounds are in the applicants' accounts, and that orientation week was 17 to 23 October 2026. If you want to apply for the next year, expect a similar pattern, but wait for the new TCU almanac and guidebook. Do not assume the dates repeat.

## What does it cost?

The application fee is given above. For tuition, the 2026/2027 booklet has a table by programme. In the part we read, Tanzanian tuition fees per year ranged from TZS 1,000,000 to TZS 1,800,000, depending on the programme. The booklet says fees paid are not refundable and that the university may revise fees.

Every student also pays direct university costs. The booklet's table totals TZS 255,400 for Tanzanians. Separately, it gives an advisory figure of TZS 2,670,000 a year for books, meals and accommodation, and says more may be needed. These numbers belong to the 2026/2027 booklet, so look up your own programme in the current one. For loans, see our guide to [student loans in Tanzania](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/). The booklet also says evening programmes do not get HESLB financial support.

## What about postgraduate and short courses?

Postgraduate applications also use `admission.udsm.ac.tz`, under the Postgraduate menu. A UDSM announcement for 2025/2026 listed intakes in July, October and March, with different deadlines, and listed minimum entry rules. For example, a master's by coursework and dissertation needs at least a lower second-class degree (GPA 2.7). It did not state an application fee. As checked on 24 November 2026, the October intake window for 2026/2027 was closed. Read the current announcement and the postgraduate brochure for the next intake.

Undergraduate non-degree programmes (certificates and diplomas) are also applied for through the same system, with the fee paid in the same way.

Short professional courses are on a separate site, `dps.udsm.ac.tz`, where you register, log in and press Apply on a course. Each course has its own fee and dates there.

## Common mistakes

- **Names that do not match.** Names and index numbers must match your exam records. The guidebook says so.
- **Applying below the minimum.** If you do not have two principal passes with 4.0 points, the guidebook advises not to apply.
- **Using agents.** Applications and payments go through official portals. Our post on [checking whether a university is registered](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-check-university-or-college-is-registered-tanzania/) also explains agents to avoid.
- **Forgetting to confirm.** An unconfirmed offer is lost.
- **Expecting to defer.** The booklet says selected students who cannot join cannot defer to the next year.
- **Losing your email or password.** You need the email to confirm your admission.

## Common questions

### Do I apply through TCU?

No. For bachelor's degrees you apply to each university. TCU publishes the rules and dates.

### How many programmes can I choose at UDSM?

At least 3 and at most 15, according to the 2026/2027 booklet.

### Who do I ask if something goes wrong?

The booklet lists admission.undergraduate@udsm.ac.tz for undergraduate questions. The admission page lists helpdesk lines open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00. Postgraduate: admission.dpgs@udsm.ac.tz.

### Can foreign applicants apply?

Yes, through the same system. The booklet has separate fee rules for international applicants. Read section 4.3 of it.

## What to do next

1. Download the latest UDSM undergraduate booklet and find your programme row.
2. Check your points against the entry rule.
3. Prepare your index numbers, certificates and an email address you control.
4. Watch the TCU page for the next almanac, then apply early in the window.

If the first try does not work, our post on [what to do if you are not selected](/post/educational/study-tips/not-selected-for-university-what-to-do-next/) lists the next options.
