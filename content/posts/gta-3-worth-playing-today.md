Many people still talk about Grand Theft Auto III, often called GTA 3 or GTA III, even though it first came out in 2001. Maybe you played it on a friend's PC years ago, maybe you only know the name, or maybe you wonder why a game this old still matters. This post explains what the mchezo is, why it was different, and whether it is worth your time today.

It is a game for adults, with strong violence and language. Please read the age rating section before anyone younger plays.

## Key points

- Grand Theft Auto III is the first 3D game in the series. Rockstar's North American PlayStation 2 release came in October 2001, and a Windows version followed in May 2002.
- It helped make the "open world" idea popular: a whole city you can explore freely, instead of one level after another.
- Today you can buy it legally on phones, and as part of a remastered trilogy on consoles and PC. Its look and controls feel old, and the 2021 remaster was criticised.
- Both the original and the remaster are rated for adults (ESRB Mature 17+, PEGI 18).
- Unofficial copies are illegal and often carry malware. Buy only from official stores.

## What is Grand Theft Auto III?

Grand Theft Auto III is an action-adventure game. You play Claude, a criminal who never speaks, in a fictional place called Liberty City. The city is loosely based on New York. A bank robbery goes wrong at the start, and the story is about revenge and crime in the city's underworld.

The game was made by DMA Design, a studio in Scotland, and published by Rockstar Games. DMA Design was later renamed Rockstar North. According to Wikipedia's article on the game, about 23 people made up the core team. We use Wikipedia here for the story of how the game was made, because Rockstar's own pages do not cover it. Treat those details as a guide.

The story is not the main reason people remember it. The main reason is the freedom. You can take a mission, or ignore it and just drive around.

## What was new in 2001?

Earlier Grand Theft Auto games showed the city from above, like a map. This one put the camera behind your character, in full 3D. That sounds normal now. In 2001 it was a big step, and it was possible because the PlayStation 2 console was more powerful.

Several things came together in one game:

- **A living city.** There were cars, pedestrians, police and gangs, all going about their business on three connected islands that open up as the story moves on.
- **Free choice.** You could walk, drive or steal a car whenever you wanted. Missions were optional steps in a larger playground.
- **Voices and radio.** The game had recorded voice acting and car radio stations with music and talk shows, so the city felt full of sound.
- **A police system.** Your crimes raised a "wanted" level, shown as stars. More stars meant a stronger police response.

One critic quoted on Wikipedia said the game did not invent most of its ideas, but "brought them all together". That is a fair way to see it. Other games had open worlds. This one made the idea popular, and many later games copied it.

## The sound and the radio

The radio is a big part of why the game is remembered. Wikipedia reports about three and a half hours of radio material in the game, including a full talk-radio channel. You pick a station while you drive, like a real car.

This was unusual then. It made driving feel like part of a place, not just a way to reach the next mission. Today many open-world games have radio, and GTA 3 is one of the games that made it normal.

## How successful was it?

Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, said in a May 2002 press release that the game had quickly become the best-selling video game of 2001. Its October 2002 release said GTA 3 had global sales above 8 million units by then. We could not open an official, up-to-date total, so we leave later sales numbers out.

Reviewers were very positive. Wikipedia's summary of the aggregator Metacritic gives the PlayStation 2 version 97 out of 100, based on 56 reviews. That is the site's score for the game at the time and says nothing about how it plays now.

It was also controversial. Wikipedia lists criticism of its violence and sexual content and a ban for a time in Australia. That is why age ratings matter so much for this game.

## How does it play today?

This is the honest part. Games from 2001 can feel rough, and GTA 3 is no exception.

**What still works.** The city is interesting to explore. The radio, the humour and the sense of freedom still feel good. The missions are varied and the game is easy to understand.

**What feels old.** Aiming with a controller was awkward even when the game was new. Wikipedia notes that some reviewers in 2001 called combat clunky, and the mobile version was criticised for touchscreen controls. The graphics are simple by today's standards, and missions can be hard when you fail far from the start.

The 2021 remaster tried to fix some of this. Rockstar's store page says it adds "Grand Theft Auto V-style controls and targeting", better lighting, and sharper textures. We cover that next.

## The 2021 remaster: Definitive Edition

On 11 November 2021, Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy, The Definitive Edition. It bundles GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas. Rockstar's store page says it was "adapted by Grove Street Games" and lists release on 11 November 2021 for PC, with versions on PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo Switch.

Its launch went badly. According to Wikipedia, critics praised the lighting and the better controls, but criticised technical problems and the new character looks. It lists Metacritic scores of 56 for Xbox Series X|S, 54 for PS5, 49 for Windows and 46 for Switch. Rockstar published an apology note after launch, titled "A Note from the Rockstar Games Team RE: Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy" and has released updates since. Wikipedia reports a later update that added a "classic lighting" option, but we have not tested it ourselves.

Several music tracks and cheats from the older versions were also removed, according to the same Wikipedia article. If the original music matters to you, keep that in mind.

## PC requirements for the remaster

Rockstar's store page lists these minimums for the Definitive Edition on PC:

- Windows 10, 64-bit
- Intel Core i5-6600K or AMD FX-6300
- 8 GB of memory
- Nvidia GeForce GTX 760 2 GB or AMD Radeon R9 280 3 GB
- 45 GB of disk space for the trilogy

That is a lot for an old game. The remaster is heavier than the 2001 original, and it will not run on many older computers. ## Age rating: why parents should read this

The ESRB rating for the remaster on the PlayStation Store is Mature 17+. Steam lists PEGI 18 and ESRB Mature, with descriptors including intense violence, blood and gore, sexual content, strong language and drug references. Google Play and the App Store also show a 17+ rating.

This is not a game for children, and an age rating is not a suggestion. Our guide to [game age ratings and parental controls](/post/video-games-updates/game-age-ratings-esrb-pegi-and-parental-controls/) explains what the labels mean and how to set limits on phones and consoles.

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- A key piece of gaming history that is still fun to explore.
- Large map, varied missions and great use of radio.
- Cheap on phones, with a free trial to test it first.

**Cons**

- Aiming and some missions feel frustrating.
- The remaster had a poor launch, and the PC version is heavy.
- Strong adult content. Not for children.

## So, is it worth playing today?

Here is our view by type of player. This is opinion, not a score.

- **Curious newcomer (an adult).** Try the free 30-minute trial on a phone first. If you enjoy it, you will know quickly. If the controls annoy you, you have lost nothing.
- **Nostalgic player.** The remaster brings back the places, but the changed look and missing songs may disappoint you. Read about the version before paying full price.
- **Low-end PC owner.** The Definitive Edition asks for a lot. A phone version may suit you better, or play something that matches your hardware.
- **Young teen.** No. It is rated for adults.

## Common questions

### Is GTA 3 free?

Not as a full game. The mobile version has a free 30-minute trial, after which you pay once to continue. Any site offering a "free full download" is not official.

### Is it safe to use unofficial copies?

No. Unofficial copies are illegal and often hide malware, fake installers or scams. Our guide to [fake downloads and scams](/post/video-games-updates/gta-6-scams-fake-downloads-and-leaks-how-to-stay-safe/) shows the warning signs. Buy from the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Steam, Rockstar Store, App Store or Google Play.

### Should I play GTA 3 before the newer games?

You do not have to. The stories are separate. Play it for history, not because you need it.

### Can a low-end Android phone run it?

We could not confirm phone requirements from the store pages. The trial lets you test your own phone, and our post on [free games for low-end Android phones](/post/video-games-updates/free-games-for-low-end-android-phones/) has tips for checking ratings and storage.

## What to do next

1. Decide if you are an adult player and if the content suits you.
2. Open the official store page for your device and check the current price and age rating.
3. Try the free mobile trial before spending money on a bigger version.
4. Avoid any site that offers a free full copy.

Old games can be worth the time, and trying one on a phone costs very little.
