Opening a drawer and not finding your certificate is a sinking feeling, especially when a college or an employer is waiting for it. The good news is that the National Examinations Council of Tanzania (NECTA) has a service for exactly this. This post explains which service fits your case, and what we could and could not confirm.

## The short answer

NECTA offers two main ways to replace a lost or damaged certificate. Which one you use depends on whether your old certificate carried passport photos.

- **Replacement Certificate:** for a lost or damaged certificate that had passport photos.
- **Statement of Results:** for a lost or damaged certificate that had no passport photos.
- You apply through the NECTA eServices portal. Each service on the NECTA Services page has an Apply link.
- The Services page, as checked in November 2026, does not state fees or the papers needed. Check the portal for those.
- Never buy a certificate from anyone. Only NECTA issues them.

## Replacement certificate or statement of results?

NECTA's Services page describes the two services in simple terms. A Replacement Certificate is for candidates whose lost or damaged earlier certificates had passport photos. A Statement of Results is for candidates whose lost or damaged earlier certificates had no passport photos.

So the first question to ask yourself is: did my certificate have my photo on it? If you are not sure, ask a family member who saw it, or look at a photocopy if you kept one. If you still cannot tell, ask NECTA before you apply. A short email can save you a wrong application.

NECTA's FAQ page says you can apply for a Replacement Certificate or a Statement of Results if you lose a certificate, and points you to the Services menu for details. It also says each examination sitting has its own certificate, and NECTA does not combine certificates from different sittings. So if you sat O-Level once and A-Level once, you have two separate certificates, and a replacement is for the one you lost.

## How do I apply?

We can describe the route, but not every click.

1. Open the NECTA website and go to the Services page.
2. Choose the service that matches your case (replacement or statement).
3. Press its Apply link. It takes you to the NECTA eServices portal.
4. Follow the instructions on the portal.

The Services page does not list fees, required documents or waiting times, and we did not find them on the pages we opened. Please read the portal carefully and do not rely on a figure from a friend or a social media post. Prices and rules can change.

If you want to speak to someone, NECTA's contact page, as checked in November 2026, gave these details:

- Email: esnecta@necta.go.tz
- Phone: 0738743381 or 0262610690
- Working hours: Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 16:30
- Address: The Executive Secretary, NECTA, P.O. Box 2624 or 32019, Dar es Salaam

When you write, include your full name, the exam (CSEE or ACSEE), the year, your school and your index number if you know it. A clear message helps staff find your record.

## What if my name is spelled wrongly?

This is a different problem with a different service. NECTA has a Correction of Names service for candidates whose names were recorded incorrectly during exam registration. It corrects misspelled names on certificates.

If your certificate exists but the name is wrong, use that service and not a replacement. Check the eServices portal for what it asks you to provide.

## Are old certificates held by NECTA?

Possibly, in one case. NECTA lists a service called Returned Certificates. It is for private candidates who sat exams before 2015 and never collected their certificates from the Postal Office, so that NECTA received them back.

If that sounds like you, you may not need a replacement at all. Your certificate may be waiting. Use the Returned Certificates service on the portal to ask.

## What about appeals and withheld results?

Two more services are worth knowing about, because people sometimes confuse them with a lost certificate.

- **Release of Exam Results:** for candidates whose results are withheld because the exam fee was paid late.
- **Appeals:** for candidates who disagree with their results. NECTA describes it as a formal, clear and fair way to follow up when there is a disagreement.

A missing certificate and a withheld result are not the same thing. Use the one that matches your situation.

## How can I avoid losing it again?

Once your replacement or statement arrives, protect it.

- Scan it or take clear photos, and keep copies in two places, such as your email and a phone folder.
- Keep the paper original in a plastic sleeve in one fixed place at home.
- Make a few photocopies for applications, and give out copies when an original is not required.
- Write down your index number, school and exam year on a separate page. It makes any future request faster.

These small habits take ten minutes and can save weeks.

## Beware of fake certificate sellers

Some people online promise a "certificate in two days" or "change your grades". That is fraud. A fake certificate can cost you a job, a place at university, and can bring legal trouble. Use only the NECTA channels above, and keep every receipt and message.

## Common questions

### Can a school or a friend collect the certificate for me?

We did not find this on NECTA's pages. Ask NECTA through its email or phone before you send anyone.

### Does a statement of results work like a certificate?

NECTA lists it as the service for those who lost a certificate that had no photos. Whether a college or employer accepts it is for them to say, so ask them early.

### I sat the exam as a private candidate. Does this apply to me?

The replacement and statement services are described for candidates in general. For private candidates before 2015, see the Returned Certificates service above.

### Is there a deadline?

We found none stated. Ask NECTA.

## What to do next

1. Decide which service fits: replacement, statement, name correction or returned certificate.
2. Gather your full name, exam year, school and index number, and ID papers for the portal.
3. Apply through the NECTA eServices portal, or email NECTA if you are unsure.
4. While you wait, ask the college or employer if they accept a letter, a result slip or a statement. Our guide on [how to read NECTA examiners' reports](/post/educational/study-tips/use-necta-examiners-reports-to-revise/) is useful if you are also preparing to re-sit.

Losing a paper does not erase what you achieved. Your results are still on record, and you can get proof again.
