Being selected by two or three universities feels like good news and a puzzle at the same time. You worked hard for this, and now the system asks you to choose. Take a breath. This post explains the rule, what the official documents say, and what to ask your university.

## The short answer

If more than one university selects you, you may confirm your admission at only one of them. The Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) calls this the confirmation step. You confirm the programme and university you prefer most.

- The TCU admission guidebook says applicants admitted in more than one institution must confirm the most preferred programme and institution.
- TCU's technical system document says a code from TCU is used to confirm one institution only.
- You can ask for that code through the online system of the university.
- Follow the instructions in your university portal, because each university runs its own page.
- Confirmation dates change every year, so check the current guidebook.

## Why can you be selected by more than one university?

Universities select students for their own programmes, so two of them can both say yes to the same student. The official documents treat this as a normal case and give a rule for it: you keep one place in the end.

This is not a mistake and it is not a warning sign. Being selected twice means your results were strong enough for both. It only means one decision is now yours to make.

## What is the one-confirmation rule?

Two official documents describe it. Both are TCU documents, and we opened both in November 2026.

The first is the Bachelor's Degree Admission Guidebook for 2026/2027. Its general rules say that applicants admitted in more than one institution must confirm their admission to the most preferred programme and institution.

The second is a technical document for university systems (the API specification, version 4.3, dated July 2021). It says each applicant with multiple selections shall use a TCU token or code to confirm selection to only one higher learning institution, and that the choice is also confirmed with TCU.

The second document is older and written for system developers, so we use it only to explain the idea. The guidebook for the current year is the document to trust for rules and dates.

TCU also publishes lists of applicants who were admitted more than once. Those lists contain students' names, so we do not link to them. Use your own university account to see your own status. The TCU notices page also showed a third round of lists for 2026/27, so a year can have more than two rounds.

Notice what the rule does not say. It does not say that your other offers are punished or that you will be blocked. It says to confirm one.

## How does the confirmation code work?

The technical document describes a confirmation code. In plain words:

1. You have more than one selection.
2. You ask for a confirmation code through the online system of the university you want to join.
3. The university passes that request to TCU.
4. You use the code to confirm that one university.

We could not find a plain-language TCU page that shows the screens and buttons. The TCU guidebook gives the rule, not a click-by-click guide. So we do not describe any steps that we could not check. Screens and wording differ from one university to another.

What you can do is this. Log in to the online admission page of your preferred university. Look for the words "confirm" or "confirmation code" in your account. If you cannot find them, read the admission message that came with your selection, or contact the admissions office.

Never pay anyone for a code. The code comes through the university system. If a stranger on social media offers to "get your code", do not send money or share your password.

## Confirmation windows in 2026/27, as an example

TCU gives dates for each admission year. The 2026/2027 guidebook lists two confirmation windows:

| Round | Admission announced | Confirmation window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First application window | 8 September 2026 | 8 to 21 September 2026 |
| Second application window | 6 October 2026 | 6 to 19 October 2026 |

As checked in November 2026, both windows have already closed for this year. We show them only so you can see how the process works. The dates for your year will be different. Read the guidebook for your own admission year on the TCU website, and note that the guidebook says the earliest date for universities to open for 2026/27 was 19 October 2026.

If you are reading this in the middle of a window, do not wait for the last day. Websites are slower at the end of a rush, and a weak mobile network can cost you hours.

## What if you miss the window or lose the code?

This part needs honesty. The guidebook does not tell applicants what happens to an unconfirmed selection. We found no official sentence that explains it, so we will not guess.

What we do know from the technical document is that the system has actions for an applicant who confirmed and then changes their mind. An applicant who had confirmed can unconfirm through the online system of the university, and an applicant can cancel or reject an admission the same way. Those details are about the university system, and the exact steps may differ.

If you think you missed a deadline or lost your code:

- Contact the admissions office of the university where you want to study. The guidebook says questions about applications and admissions go to the institution you applied to.
- Do it quickly, in writing if you can, and keep a copy of your message.
- Keep your application number and index number ready.
- Be calm and polite. Admissions staff handle many cases like yours.

## Common questions

### Can I confirm two universities to be safe?

No. The rule is one confirmation. Confirming the one you really want is the safe choice.

### How do I choose between two universities?

Compare things you can check: the programme name, the fees, the place, the cost of living, and whether the university is registered. Our post on [how to check if a university or college is registered in Tanzania](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-check-university-or-college-is-registered-tanzania/) helps with that last point. If money is a worry, read the loan rules before you decide, in our [HESLB loan guide](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/).

### Does TCU confirm me directly?

The technical document says the confirmation is passed to TCU through the university system. The applicant works through the university page. Follow your university portal's instructions.

### I was not selected anywhere. What now?

That is a different situation and there are real options. See [Not selected for university yet? What you can do next](/post/educational/study-tips/not-selected-for-university-what-to-do-next/).

### Is it safe to share my code?

Treat it like a password. Only enter it on the official page of your university.

## What to do next

1. Read the current TCU admission guidebook for your admission year, especially the dates table and the general rules.
2. Decide which programme and university you want most, and write down why. Talk to your family or a teacher you trust.
3. Log in to that university's admission page and look for the confirmation option. Ask the admissions office if you cannot find it.
4. Finish before the window closes, and keep a screenshot or message that shows you confirmed.

You earned this choice. Make it calmly, and confirm the place you want.
