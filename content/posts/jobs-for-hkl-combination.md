HKL means History, Kiswahili and English Language as your three principal subjects in Form Five and Form Six. In Tanzania it is a combination for people who love words, stories and the past. It leads to teaching, translation, writing, journalism, law and public service. This guide is for Form Four leavers choosing, Form Six students waiting for results, and parents.

We checked the entry rules in the Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) guidebook for 2026/27 in November 2026. Rules change every year and differ between universities. Treat this as a map and confirm your own university's rule.

## Key points

- The government's list of combinations gives HKL as History, Kiswahili and English Language, a Social Science combination.
- Two of your three subjects are languages. That fits translation, interpreting, language studies, literature and creative writing degrees.
- With History, it also fits arts teaching, law, journalism and public administration.
- Most arts degrees ask for two principal passes and 4.0 points, so you have one spare subject.
- HKL does not lead to medicine, engineering or most science degrees.
- No combination promises a job. Entry is competitive and pay differs between jobs.

## What HKL means

The TAMISEMI selection site lists HKL as "History, Kiswahili and English Language" in the group of Social Science combinations (tahasusi za Sayansi ya Jamii). We checked it in November 2026.

Some people read the L as "Literature". Our check found the official list says English Language. Confirm the exact subjects with your school before you choose.

## How points work

The guidebook counts points from your principal passes. For results from 2016 onward, A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and S = 0.5. The general minimum is 4.0 points from two subjects that define the programme.

Say you get C in Kiswahili and C in English Language. That is 3 + 3 = 6 points. Say you also get B in History, so your best two subjects are B and C, which is 7 points. Several language degrees ask for a "C" in one language subject. That grade matters as much as the total.

The University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM) writes its minimum as "5 from 3 subjects". Read its page to see how it counts. For more on reading these lines, see [how to read TCU entry requirements](/post/educational/career-development/how-to-read-tcu-programme-entry-requirements/).

The same guidebook lists Journalism and Media Studies at UDSM, which asks for two principals at "C" or above in any subject, and Mass Communication and Multimedia Studies, which also asks for a "C" in English at O-Level.

## Teaching: the clearest road for HKL

Look at the Mwalimu Nyerere Memorial Academy lines in the table. Three education degrees match HKL pairs exactly: Kiswahili and English, History and Kiswahili, History and English. Teaching also uses your strongest two subjects, which is a good use of a three-subject combination.

Other universities list BA with Education programmes that accept two principals from History, Geography, Kiswahili and English. Government teaching pay follows official scales, so look up the current scale rather than trust a rumour.

## Translation, writing and language careers

Translation and interpretation degrees exist because people need others to understand them in courts, hospitals, offices and meetings. These degrees are for strong readers and writers in two languages. UDOM asks for a "C" or above in one language subject.

Creative writing and literature degrees are for people who love a story. We cannot say how many jobs exist for them, and we found no official figure. Many writers also work as teachers, editors or communicators. Treat this path as one that rewards effort, not as a guaranteed job.

## Law, journalism and public service

For law, the guidebook shows History and English among the accepted principals at UDSM, UDOM and the University of Iringa. UDSM notes that an applicant without principal passes in both History and English needs subsidiary passes or a "C" at O-Level. HKL students already have both as principals. UDSM and UDOM list law at four years, Iringa at three.

After a law degree, the Law School of Tanzania offers a Postgraduate Diploma in Legal Practice for people who plan to practise. Check the school's own notices for rules and fees. We found no official figures, so we give none.

For journalism, your English and Kiswahili are real tools. Newsrooms and offices work in both languages. UDOM also lists Political Science and Public Administration and International Relations for History, Kiswahili or English principals.

## For the student who is afraid

Maybe a classmate told you HKL is "easy" or "weak". Maybe an uncle asked why you do not take sciences. Their words can sit in your chest for weeks.

Here is the truth. Words carry law, news, schools, courts and government. Every degree in the table above is a real degree, taught at real universities, and many have hundreds of places. Arts subjects are not a lesser road.

Maybe your parents gave up something so you could stay in school. You want to give them a good result. That love is a reason to start early, not a reason to freeze. HKL is hard in its own way: History needs long answers, Kiswahili and English need daily reading and writing. But you can do daily work. Read one page and write five lines each day, and in two years you will be far from where you started. No one can promise the result. You can promise the effort.

## Where HKL does not fit

- **Health degrees** such as medicine and pharmacy need Physics, Chemistry and Biology. See the [PCB guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/).
- **Engineering** needs Advanced Mathematics in the entries we read. See the [PCM guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcm-combination/).
- **Accounting and finance** often need Mathematics at some level. HKL does not give you that, and a missing pass can close the door in some universities.
- **Geography-based degrees** such as Geography and Environmental Management ask for Geography. HGL or HGK is a better fit. See the [HGL guide](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-hgl-combination/).

## What if your points are not enough?

1. **Try a related programme.** If translation is full, look at language studies, literature, education or journalism.
2. **Look at a diploma.** Colleges offer diplomas in teaching, journalism and office work. Check the college is registered, using our guide on [how to check if a university or college is registered](/post/educational/study-tips/how-to-check-university-or-college-is-registered-tanzania/).
3. **Try another university.** Points differ from place to place.
4. **Retake if it makes sense.** Ask your teachers first.

## If you are in Form Four: is HKL for you?

Check these signs.

- Do you get good marks in Kiswahili and English, including essays and literature?
- Do you enjoy History, even the long answers?
- Can you read for an hour without getting bored?
- Do you picture yourself teaching, writing, speaking or working with people?

If Mathematics and sciences are your strong subjects, ask a teacher whether a science combination suits you better. Search your dream job in the guidebook and see what it asks for.

## If you are in Form Six: your plan after results

1. List three degrees you want and one safer choice.
2. Open the latest guidebook and read each entry at each university.
3. Pay special attention to grade rules, such as "C" in a language subject.
4. Apply only through official university or TCU channels.
5. Read our [HESLB loan guide](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/) for fees.

## A plan for Form Five and Form Six

1. **First term:** choose two or three target degrees and note their rules.
2. **Each week:** write one English essay and one Kiswahili composition, and read History notes aloud.
3. **Before NECTA:** practise timed past papers. Our guide on [study techniques](/post/educational/study-tips/10-proven-techniques-to-study-effectively-for-students/) can help.
4. **After results:** compare your points with the guidebook and apply early.

## Other combinations worth comparing

- [HGL](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-hgl-combination/) swaps Kiswahili for Geography. It fits planning, tourism and heritage.
- [HGK](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-hgk-combination/) keeps History and Kiswahili with Geography, and drops English.
- [HGE](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-hge-combination/) adds Economics, which helps with public policy and development.
- [ECA](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-eca-combination/) points to business and accounting.

## Common questions

### Is HKL a good combination?

It is good for teaching, translation, language studies, journalism and law, if you are strong in languages and History. It does not open health or engineering.

### What does HKL stand for in Tanzania?

HKL stands for History, Kiswahili and English Language. The TAMISEMI list of combinations gives this meaning.

### Can I become a lawyer with HKL?

Yes, a law degree accepts History and English Language among its principals at several universities. After the degree you still need practical legal training, for example at the Law School of Tanzania.

### Can I become a teacher with HKL?

Yes. The Mwalimu Nyerere Memorial Academy lists education degrees in Kiswahili and English, History and Kiswahili, and History and English.

### How much do HKL careers pay?

Pay varies and we could not confirm official figures, so we give none. Check government pay scales and real job adverts.

## What to do next

1. Open the latest TCU guidebook and search for translation, teaching and law.
2. Write down the subjects, grades and points for each entry.
3. Ask your Kiswahili and English teachers about your strengths.
4. Keep a backup degree or diploma.
