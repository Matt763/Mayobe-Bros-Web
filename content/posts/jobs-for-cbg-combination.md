CBG means Chemistry, Biology and Geography as your three principal subjects in Form Five and Form Six. NECTA lists it among the natural science combinations of the Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE). It leads to agriculture, forestry, wildlife, environmental science, food and nutrition degrees, some health degrees and science teaching. It does not lead to medicine, dentistry or pharmacy.

This guide is for Form Four leavers choosing a combination, Form Six students waiting for results, and parents. We read the entry rules in the TCU admission guidebook for 2026/27 in November 2026. Rules change every year, so use this as a map and confirm before you apply.

## Key points

- CBG is a real combination: NECTA's ACSEE page lists it as Chemistry, Biology and Geography.
- It fits the land, water, animals, plants, food and environment: agriculture, forestry, wildlife, fisheries, environmental science and food science.
- A few health degrees accept it, such as Environmental Health Sciences, Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics, and Public Health. They ask for at least 6 points at the main universities we checked.
- Medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and medical laboratory sciences ask for Physics. CBG cannot enter them.
- Many degrees only ask for 4.0 points from two subjects, so CBG students often have more choice than they fear.
- No degree or job is guaranteed. Pay and vacancies differ a lot, and we do not list salary figures.

## Why CBG can feel like a second choice, and why it is not

Many Form Four students hear that "the best students take PCB". If your Physics is weak, or you simply love plants, animals and the land, that sentence can hurt.

Here is the honest picture. PCB opens the door to medicine and pharmacy. CBG does not. But CBG is built for a different set of careers, and many of them are needed in Tanzania: farming that feeds towns, forests that keep rivers running, wildlife that brings visitors, food that is safe to eat. These are not small things. They are work that families and villages depend on.

If you are thinking about the [PCB combination](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/), read that guide too and compare. Choose by what you want to do and what you do well, not by what sounds most important.

## How points work

TCU, the Tanzania Commission for Universities, publishes a guidebook each year for Form Six leavers. It lists each degree, the subjects needed, the minimum points, the places available and the years of study.

For results from 2016 onward the scale is A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1 and S = 0.5. The general rule is two principal passes with a total of at least 4.0 points in the two subjects that define the programme. Health degrees are stricter and count three subjects.

Here is a hypothetical example. Say you get C in Chemistry, D in Biology and E in Geography. That is 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 points. On paper that meets the 6-point rule for health degrees that accept Geography, and it also gives you 5 points from Chemistry and Biology for a degree that asks for two subjects. Places are limited, so higher grades are always safer.

## Degrees that accept CBG

The table lists entries in the 2026/27 guidebook where Chemistry, Biology and Geography meet the subject rule. Each university writes its own rule, so open the guidebook for the exact words.

| Degree and university | Subjects the guidebook asks for | Minimum points | Years |
|---|---|---|---|
| BSc Agriculture, Sokoine University of Agriculture (SUA) | Biology plus one of Chemistry, Physics or Agriculture | 4.0 | 3 |
| BSc Forestry, SUA | Biology plus one of Chemistry, Physics, Geography or Agriculture | 4.0 | 3 |
| BSc Wildlife Management, SUA | Biology plus one of Chemistry, Physics or Geography | 4.0 | 3 |
| BSc Aquaculture, SUA | Biology plus one of Agriculture, Chemistry, Physics or Geography | 4.0 | 3 |
| BSc Environmental Science and Management, SUA | Chemistry plus one of Biology, Advanced Mathematics, Geography, Physics, Food and Nutrition or Agriculture | 4.0 | 3 |
| BSc Food Science and Technology, SUA | Chemistry plus one of Biology, Physics, Nutrition, Agriculture, Advanced Mathematics or Geography | 4.0 | 3 |
| BSc Human Nutrition, SUA | Chemistry plus one of Biology, Home Economics, Agriculture, Food and Human Nutrition, Advanced Mathematics, Physics or Geography | 4.0 | 3 |
| Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine, SUA | Chemistry and Biology, with a subsidiary pass in one of Advanced Mathematics, Physics, Geography, Nutrition or Agriculture | 4.5 | 5 |
| BSc Environmental Health Sciences (harmonised rule) | Chemistry, Biology and one of Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Nutrition, Geography or Agriculture | 6 | 4 at MUHAS |
| BSc Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics (harmonised rule) | Chemistry, Biology and one of Physics, Advanced Mathematics, Nutrition, Geography or Agriculture | 6 | 4 at UDOM |
| BSc Public Health, Zanzibar University | Chemistry, Biology and one of Physics, Mathematics, Nutrition, Geography or Agriculture | 5 | 3 |
| BSc with Education, SUA | Two principal passes in the combination from Chemistry, Biology, Geography, Advanced Mathematics, Agriculture, Nutrition or Computer Science | 4.0 | 3 |

The three health degrees also ask for minimum grades. For Environmental Health Sciences the harmonised rule asks for at least a C in Chemistry, a D in Biology and an E in the third subject. Public Health at Zanzibar University asks for D in Chemistry and Biology and E in the third.

Two cautions. The veterinary entry lists Geography as an accepted subsidiary subject and asks for a C in O-Level Physics, Basic Mathematics and English if Physics is not among the subsidiaries. Ask SUA how a principal pass in Geography is counted. Second, universities set their own grade rules on top of the common ones, so a degree that suits CBG at one university may be stricter at another.

## What kind of work follows

A degree is not a job, and we cannot promise one. These are the kinds of work the programmes above prepare you for.

- **Agriculture and food.** Crop and livestock extension, farm management, horticulture, food processing and quality control.
- **Forestry and wildlife.** Forest management, conservation, tourism-related work with parks and wildlife areas.
- **Environment and water.** Environmental assessment, waste management, land use planning support, work with water sources.
- **Health and nutrition.** Environmental health officer, public health work, clinical nutrition and dietetics at hospitals and programmes.
- **Teaching.** Secondary school teaching in Chemistry, Biology or Geography after a BSc with Education.
- **Research and further study.** A first degree can lead to a master's, which takes longer and needs a fee plan.

Government and private employers set their own pay and publish their own vacancies. We could not confirm salary figures from an official source, so we do not give them. Ask people working in a field and read real job adverts before you decide.

## A heart-to-heart before you choose

Imagine you are sitting with your selection form and the list of combinations. Your parents or guardians may have sold something, borrowed or saved for years to keep you in school. You feel that weight. You want to choose well, and you are afraid of choosing wrong.

Here is what we can say honestly. No combination makes you someone. Your effort in Form Five and Form Six counts for more than three letters on a certificate. A student who studies Chemistry, Biology and Geography with care, asks for help when stuck and keeps going on hard days can reach a good degree. A student who chooses PCB and does not study may not.

You may still be unsure, and that is normal. The fear of choosing wrong is a sign that you take your future seriously. Use it to ask good questions, not to rush. Nothing here is a guarantee, but a clear plan makes the road less frightening.

## If you are in Form Four: is CBG right for you?

Look at your own marks and your own interests before you choose.

1. **Chemistry and Biology.** Both are in every CBG entry. Health degrees ask for at least a C in Chemistry and a D in Biology at A-Level, so start strong.
2. **Geography.** It helps with land, climate, maps and environment. If you enjoy fieldwork and map reading, it will feel natural.
3. **Basic Mathematics.** Several entries ask for a pass or a D in Basic Mathematics at O-Level, and the food science entry at one university asks for a D in Basic Mathematics and in Physics or Chemistry and Biology. Do not ignore it.
4. **Physics.** If you want medicine or pharmacy, you need PCB. If you are sure you do not, CBG is fine.
5. **Your interests.** Do you like animals, farms, forests, food or the environment? Say them out loud. If most of your answers are yes, CBG may suit you.

Then check what your school offers. Not every school teaches every combination, and Form Five places are allocated by the government. Our overview of [how your combination affects your career](/post/educational/combination-na-aina-ya-kazi-utakayofanya/) explains the wider picture.

## If you are in Form Six: what to do after your results

1. Read your points with our guide to [reading your Form Six results](/post/educational/results/how-to-read-acsee-results-points-and-divisions/).
2. Open the current TCU guidebook and search for each programme you like. Write down the exact subjects, minimum points and years of study.
3. Apply through the university websites in the dates TCU announces. No one can sell you a place.
4. Plan fees early. Our guide to [HESLB student loans](/post/educational/study-tips/students-loan-tanzania-complete-guide/) explains how to apply.
5. Keep a second and third choice that also accept CBG.

## What if your points are not enough?

1. **Move to a related programme.** Forestry, wildlife, aquaculture and environmental science often have different competition from agriculture or veterinary medicine.
2. **Think about a diploma.** Technical colleges offer diplomas in agriculture, forestry, laboratory work and nutrition. They are regulated by NACTVET, so check that the college and course are accredited.
3. **Plan the diploma-to-degree route.** Read our guide on [going from diploma to degree](/post/educational/career-development/diploma-to-degree-tanzania-entry-requirements/) and the explainer on [NTA levels](/post/educational/career-development/nta-levels-explained-tanzania/).
4. **Try again.** Some students retake examinations privately. It costs time and money, so ask your teachers first.

## Where CBG does not fit

- **Medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, medical laboratory sciences.** The harmonised entry rules ask for Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
- **Nursing.** The harmonised rule lists Chemistry, Biology and Physics, Advanced Mathematics or Nutrition. Geography is not in that list, so CBG does not meet it. Some universities may differ, so check.
- **Engineering.** It normally asks for Advanced Mathematics, usually with Physics.

## Other combinations worth comparing

- [CBN (Chemistry, Biology, Nutrition)](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-cbn-combination/) swaps Geography for Nutrition. It meets the nursing rule, which CBG does not.
- [PCB](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcb-combination/) is the route to medicine and pharmacy, but it asks for strong Physics.
- [PCM](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pcm-combination/) suits engineering and physical sciences, with Chemistry and Mathematics.
- [PGM](/post/educational/study-tips/jobs-for-pgm-combination/) keeps Geography and adds Physics and Mathematics, which suits surveying and engineering routes.

## Common questions

### Is CBG a real combination in Tanzania?

Yes. NECTA's ACSEE page lists CBG as Chemistry, Biology and Geography. Whether your school offers it is a separate question, so ask the school.

### Can CBG students study medicine?

No. Medicine needs principal passes in Physics, Chemistry and Biology in the TCU guidebook. If medicine is your dream, you need PCB.

### Which degrees can I study with CBG?

Agriculture, forestry, wildlife, aquaculture, environmental science, food science, human nutrition, veterinary medicine at SUA, and Environmental Health, Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics and Public Health at universities that list Geography. Check each entry.

### Can a CBG student become a teacher?

Yes. Bachelor of Science with Education programmes list Chemistry, Biology and Geography among accepted subjects, for example at SUA and Zanzibar University.

### Is CBG easier than PCB?

We cannot say. It depends on your strengths. Chemistry and Biology still need steady work. The difference is that CBG leaves out Physics and adds Geography.

## What to do next

1. Download the latest TCU guidebook for Form Six holders and search for the programmes above.
2. Write down the subjects, points and years for three degrees you like.
3. Ask a teacher or a university student which of them match your current marks.
4. Keep a backup plan: a related degree and an accredited diploma.
