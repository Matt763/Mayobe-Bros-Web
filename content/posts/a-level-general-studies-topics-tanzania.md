Every Form Six candidate in Tanzania hears about General Studies. It is not part of any combination, yet NECTA says everyone takes it. This guide explains what it is, what the paper looks like, and why a student who started Form Five under the newer curriculum may see different subject names.

It is written for Form Four leavers, Form Five and Six students, and parents. We keep to what NECTA and TIE have published.

## The short answer

- NECTA's ACSEE page says General Studies is compulsory for all candidates, and other subjects depend on the combination. This was the wording as checked on 24 November 2026.
- The 2019 NECTA format gives one paper, three hours, seven essay questions, five to answer, 100 marks.
- Six topics are tested: philosophy and religion, contemporary issues, international affairs, democratic process, science and technology in development, and life skills.
- The 2023 TIE curriculum for Forms Five and Six does not list General Studies. It lists two compulsory subjects instead: Academic Communication and Historia ya Tanzania na Maadili.
- We could not confirm which Form Six year first sits an exam on the 2023 subjects. Ask your school and check NECTA.

## What General Studies is

General Studies is a broad subject. It is not about one body of facts. It tests whether you can read an issue, give reasons, and write a balanced essay. The NECTA format says the exam should assess whether you can communicate on social and economic issues, show creativity and critical thinking, describe cross-cutting issues, understand your role in society, show patriotism and moral values, and learn on your own.

The same format says it is based on the General Studies Syllabus of 2009 for Advanced Level. TIE's current list of advanced secondary syllabi does not include a General Studies syllabus, so the 2009 one is the one NECTA's format points to.

## The paper: how it is built

From NECTA's 2019 booklet, which we could open in November 2026:

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Paper | One paper, code 111 |
| Time | Three hours |
| Questions | Seven essay questions |
| What you answer | Five questions; question one is compulsory |
| Marks | 20 per question, 100 in total |

This means you can drop two questions, but you must write five long, well-organised essays in three hours. That is about 35 minutes each, with no time left over. Planning matters more than speed.

## The six topics

NECTA lists these topics. The table of specifications gives each a share of marks in the older format. We show the shares as rough guides only, because the weights change from year to year.

| Topic | What it tends to ask |
|---|---|
| Philosophy and religion | Beliefs, values, thinking skills, the role of faith in society |
| Contemporary and cross-cutting issues | Current social problems, such as health, gender, environment, corruption |
| International affairs | Organisations, regional bodies, relations between countries |
| Democratic process and practices | Elections, rights, rule of law, how government works |
| Science and technology in development | How technology helps or harms development |
| Life skills | Personal skills, decision making, relationships, coping |

In the 2019 table, international affairs had two questions and the other five topics had one each. That is a fact about one year's plan. It could differ in the exam you sit.

## How the 2023 curriculum changes the names

The 2023 curriculum for Forms Five and Six, published by TIE, says every student studies two subjects outside the combination. They are Academic Communication and Historia ya Tanzania na Maadili (History of Tanzania and Ethics).

- **Academic Communication** is taught in English. It covers things like punctuation, paragraphs, searching for information with ICT, presentations, reports, emails and project proposals.
- **Historia ya Tanzania na Maadili** covers Tanzania's history and national values: society before colonial rule, the colonial period (1890 to 1960), nation building (1961 to 1966), the Arusha Declaration years (1967 to 1985), and the period from 1986 to now. It also has a project. It gets four periods of 40 minutes each week.

Both use the same marking plan as other subjects. Thirty percent is continuous assessment and seventy percent is the national exam at the end of Form Six.

So there are two sets of names in print. NECTA's pages and 2019 format talk about General Studies. TIE's 2023 documents talk about Academic Communication and History of Tanzania and Ethics. We found no official NECTA note explaining how the two meet, and no published newer format. The honest advice is to ask your school which subjects your class will be examined in, and to check NECTA's news and publications pages.

## Does General Studies count for university?

In the TCU admission guidebook for 2026/27, General Studies appears mainly inside some entry lines. For example, one Social Work degree line asks for a credit pass in General Studies at A-Level. Most programmes base the points on principal subjects instead. Read your chosen programme line, and see our guide on [reading TCU entry requirements](/post/educational/career-development/how-to-read-tcu-programme-entry-requirements/). Our guide on [reading ACSEE results](/post/educational/results/how-to-read-acsee-results-points-and-divisions/) explains how the points are worked out.

## How to prepare

- **Read the news.** Many questions use current affairs, so a daily newspaper or news bulletin is the best textbook.
- **Practise a standard essay frame.** An opening that defines the key term, three to four points with a local example each, and a short close that answers the question.
- **Use real Tanzanian examples** you know to be true, such as a public service, a law you have read about, or a local project.
- **Time yourself.** Write one essay in 30 minutes, then check it against the question.
- **Pick the questions that need what you know.** Choose your five in the first ten minutes, but remember question one is compulsory.
- Do not memorise model answers. Examiners want your reasoning.

## Common questions

### Is General Studies part of my combination?

No. It is outside the combination. Your combination holds your three principal subjects.

### Can I fail General Studies and still go to university?

That depends on the programme's rule. Most degrees look at principal subjects, but a few list General Studies. Read the programme line before you assume.

### I started Form Five in 2025. Do I take General Studies?

We could not confirm this. The 2023 curriculum lists Academic Communication and History of Tanzania and Ethics as the compulsory subjects. Ask your school and check NECTA.

### Where can I get free official materials?

The TIE online library has official learning materials. See the link below.

## What to do next

1. Ask your teacher for the list of subjects your class will sit.
2. Read the six topics above and mark which ones you know least.
3. Start a notebook of three current issues, each with a short note of causes, effects and solutions.

This is a subject where steady reading and honest practice pay off.
