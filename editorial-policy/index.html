This policy tells you how articles on Mayobe Bros are made, so you can decide how far to trust them.

Our purpose

We publish practical information that helps people in Tanzania make better decisions about study, work, money, business and technology. We aim to be accurate, fair, clear and useful.

How we choose topics

We write about questions readers actually ask, for example how a student loan works or what a new fare means, and about changes that affect daily life. We do not write about a topic only because it is popular or because someone pays for it.

How we research and write

  • We start with official and primary sources, such as government bodies, regulators, universities and the companies that make a product.
  • We use reputable news organisations to report events, and we say which source a fact comes from.
  • Every article ends with a list of the sources we used. We only list pages we have opened and that support what we wrote.
  • We write in plain English, with short sentences and explanations of difficult words.
  • We separate fact from opinion. When we give an opinion, we say so.
  • We never invent quotes, people, studies, statistics or personal stories. Examples we make up are clearly marked as examples.

Use of AI tools

We may use software tools, including AI-assisted tools, to help with research, structure, translation and editing. A person is responsible for every article: we check the facts we use, we do not publish text we have not read, and the byline is the person accountable for it. We do not use AI to invent facts, sources, reviews or experiences. Some pictures on the site are AI-generated illustrations, and we never present an illustration as a photograph of a real event.

Dates and updates

Each article shows when it was published and, if it changed in a meaningful way, when it was updated. Time-sensitive facts such as prices, fees and deadlines say when we checked them. We review important articles regularly and update or remove those that are no longer correct.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it quickly. For a small error, we correct the text. For a significant error, we also add a note at the top of the article saying what changed. To report a mistake, email info@mayobebros.com with the article link, what is wrong and, if possible, a source.

Independence, advertising and sponsored content

  • Advertising, including Google AdSense, pays for the site but never decides what we write.
  • Sponsored content is always labelled "Sponsored" and must meet the same accuracy standards as everything else.
  • We do not take payment to change a fact, praise a product or hide a problem.
  • If an author has a personal interest in a topic, we say so in the article.

Images

We use pictures we have the right to use. Many pictures on Mayobe Bros are illustrations made with AI image tools, or stock pictures, to give a topic a face. They are not photographs of the events or people in the article, and the people in them are not real unless a caption says so. They never show a real organisation's logo as proof of anything. Every picture has a short description for readers who use screen readers, and concept images are described as concepts. If you think a picture is misleading or uses something it should not, write to us and we will change it.

Comments

An automatic check holds some comments until a person has read them. We apply our Comment Policy. Comments are readers' opinions, not our views, and we do not check every claim made in them.

Contact the editor

Write to info@mayobebros.com. Learn more about how we verify facts in our Fact-Checking Policy.
