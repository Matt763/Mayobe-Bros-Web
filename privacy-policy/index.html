Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mayobe Bros ("we", "us") collects when you use https://mayobebros.com, why we collect it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain English. If anything is unclear, email info@mayobebros.com.

1. Who we are

Mayobe Bros is an independent blog based in Tanzania. You can contact the person responsible for the site at info@mayobebros.com.

2. What we collect and why

Comments

If you post a comment we collect the name you type, your optional email address, your comment, the time, a random visitor ID saved in your browser, and a short code made from your IP address. We use this to show your comment, to contact you if needed and to stop spam. An automatic check (a list of abusive and spam words in English and Swahili, run inside our own comments service, with no other outside service involved) decides whether your comment appears at once or waits for a person to review it. If the check flags a comment we keep a short note of why, visible only to us. Your email address is never shown publicly. The IP code is a one-way hash that changes every day, and we do not store your raw IP address.

When you post a comment, your browser also keeps a secret code. It proves the comment is yours, so that you can edit or delete it. We store only a one-way hash of that code, never the code itself. If you edit a comment, we keep the text from before your latest edit so that we can check what changed. When you delete a comment, we erase the name, text, email address and IP code, the secret hash and the earlier text from our comments database at once. If the comment was an answered question, a copy saved in a published page may take a little longer to disappear. The short-lived rate-limit records (one-way codes made from your IP address, and from your email address if you asked for emails) last about a day and are cleared out from time to time rather than the moment they run out, and our hosting provider's database may keep point-in-time copies for a limited period. If the comment has replies, a short note "This comment was deleted" stays in its place. We also remove control characters from names and comments.

If you tick the box to get emails about your comment, we use your email address only to send you a link to edit or delete the comment, and, once you have opened that link, a notice when someone replies to it. That notice shows the name of the person who replied and their reply. We do not use your address for anything else. To send these emails we pass the address and the email text to our email provider, Resend, a company based in the United States, which acts as a processor for us, so the data may be processed outside Tanzania. The first email has a link that works for 7 days and says nothing about your comment except the article it is on. We keep a short record of whether you asked for reply emails and whether you opened the link, so we know whether to send notices. We send at most one reply notice a day for each comment, and every one has a link to stop them. If you delete the comment, we erase your address with it. If an email to an address bounces or is reported as spam, we keep that address on a do-not-email list so that we stop writing to it (see "How long we keep data").

Whether or not you tick that box, when a comment is held for review or looks like a question, we may email its text (at most a few such emails a day), the name you typed, the article and the reason it was held to the site owner through the same email provider. That email does not include your email address. We use it only to review the comment or to answer the question.

Reactions and likes

When you press Like, Amazed, Sad or Angry on a post we store which post, which reaction and the same random visitor ID, so that each browser counts once and can change its mind. When you like a comment we store which comment, the visitor ID and the time. This ID is not linked to your name or email.

Contact messages

When you use the contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are sent through our comments service (Cloudflare) to our email provider, Resend, which delivers them to our inbox as an email. We do not store the message on this website. To stop the form being flooded, the service keeps a one-way code made from your IP address and one made from your email address for about an hour, and a one-way code made from your email address for about a day so that we send each address at most one confirmation a day. We also keep a daily count of messages, with no personal data. We then send a short confirmation email to the address you typed. It does not repeat your message, and it may not always arrive: for example, we send only one a day to each address, we do not email an address that bounced, and a daily limit on emails can be reached. We use your details only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and we keep the email for as long as we need it for that. If the form cannot send your message, the page offers to open your own email app with the message ready to send, and then the message goes through your own email provider.

Your preferences

Your browser stores your theme choice (light, dark or device) and your cookie choice. These stay on your device. See the Cookie Policy.

Listen to this article

The "Listen" button reads an article aloud using the speech voices already built into your device and browser. We do not make or store any audio. Some browsers and devices use online voices run by the browser or phone maker (for example Google or Microsoft). In that case your browser may send the sentences it is reading to that company to turn them into sound. We never receive that text or the audio. Your chosen speed and voice are saved only in your own browser.

Videos

Videos are played by Plyr, a player we host ourselves. A video that we host ourselves does not send your data to anyone else. If an article shows a YouTube video, nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play. After that, YouTube (Google) provides the video from its privacy-enhanced address and may collect data under its own policy.

Search

Site search runs inside your browser. The words you search for are not sent to us.

Technical data

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and our comments service (Cloudflare) automatically handle technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. They use it to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse, under their own policies. We use Google Tag Manager to load measurement tools such as Google Analytics, which count visits and show us which pages are read. It loads only after you press Accept in the cookie notice; if you press Reject, it is not loaded. Google may set cookies and receive your IP address and device details. See Google's Privacy Policy. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or local storage for this and does not fingerprint visitors. It runs for every visitor, whether or not you press Accept, because it stores nothing on your device. We see only totals, such as visits per day, pages, countries and browsers, not who you are. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Ads are loaded only after you accept cookies on this site. When ads are switched on, we will use a consent message certified by Google for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads do not change what we write. We never sell your personal information.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information. We use these service providers to run the site:

  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the website files (GitHub Pages).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs the comments and reactions service and stores that data.
  • Google provides advertising when ads are switched on, and search tools that help us check how the site appears in Google Search.
  • YouTube (Google) provides a video only if you press play on an embedded YouTube video.
  • Resend, an email provider based in the United States, delivers the emails our website sends: the contact form message to us, the confirmation to the sender, and the comment emails described above. It acts as a processor for us and keeps its own delivery records under its own policy. It tells us when an address bounces or reports an email as spam.
  • Our email provider receives messages sent to our email addresses.

These providers may process data in countries outside Tanzania. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect our readers and the site from abuse.

5. Links and share buttons

Our articles link to other websites, and the share buttons open Facebook, X, WhatsApp or LinkedIn in a new tab. We do not load their tracking scripts on our pages. Once you click, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

6. How long we keep data

  • Approved comments stay until you ask us to remove them or we delete them.
  • If you delete a comment, its name, text, email, IP code, secret hash and earlier text are erased from our comments database at once. A copy saved in a published page may take a little longer to disappear. Only a short "deleted" note stays if it has replies.
  • Comments we reject as spam are deleted from time to time.
  • The emailed edit link works for 7 days. We store only a one-way hash of it, and we remove the hash when the comment is deleted. The choice to get reply emails stays with the comment until it is deleted or you press the stop link in a reply email.
  • Rate-limit records, which stop floods of comments, reactions, edits, contact messages and emails, last from about ten minutes to about a day, depending on the kind, and are cleared out from time to time.
  • An address that bounced or reported one of our emails as spam stays on a do-not-email list, with the reason and the date, until we remove it. Deleting a comment does not remove it from that list. Email us if you want an address taken off.
  • We keep a count of how many emails were sent each day, with no names or addresses.
  • Reactions stay until you remove them or the post is deleted. Likes on comments stay until you take the like back or the comment is deleted.
  • Emails we receive are kept as long as needed to deal with your request and for our records.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, for example to remove one of your comments. You can also edit or delete your own comment yourself, in the browser where you posted it. Email info@mayobebros.com with the link to the article and the text of the comment. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights set out in the GDPR, including the right to object and to complain to your data protection authority. In Tanzania, the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 gives you similar rights, and you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

You can change your cookie choice at any time with the "Cookie choices" link at the bottom of every page, and you can clear your browser's stored data to remove your preferences and visitor ID.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS. We collect as little data as we can. Comments are stored in a protected database and moderation needs a secret key. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send us private information you do not need to. Please do not post private information in comments.

9. Children

Mayobe Bros is for a general audience. Our articles are written for students and adults and are not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's permission, because Tanzanian law treats under-18s as children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date. For big changes we will also say so on the home page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: info@mayobebros.com.
